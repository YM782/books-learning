# 031 · The Handmaid's Tale

- **作者：** Margaret Atwood
- **首次出版/成书：** 1985
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：当代反乌托邦文学经典，持续参与性别与公民权利讨论。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《使女的故事》描绘美国在政治危机后被神权极权政权“基列国”取代的未来。女性被剥夺财产、工作和阅读权，能够生育的女性被强制分配给统治阶层家庭，成为所谓“使女”。叙述者奥芙弗雷德以第一人称回忆旧生活、家庭和女儿，也记录她在新制度下的日常监视、仪式和恐惧。她的记忆并非完整档案，而是在压迫环境中不断变化的自我保存方式。小说通过生育控制、宗教话语、服饰标记和语言限制，展示权力如何把人的身体变成政治资源，并利用传统观念为制度暴力提供合法性。奥芙弗雷德与其他女性的关系既包含互助，也受到告密、竞争和不信任的破坏。作品并非对某个国家的简单预言，而是把历史上不同社会的压迫经验组合成警示，促使读者关注公民权利如何在危机中被逐步侵蚀。小说的结尾和附录还让人思考谁有权解释受害者的故事，以及学术机构如何在事后处理暴政留下的材料。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Margaret Atwood’s *The Handmaid’s Tale* imagines a near-future society in which the United States has been replaced by the Republic of Gilead, a rigid theocratic regime formed after political violence and environmental crisis. Women have lost many civil rights: they cannot freely work, own property, read, or control their reproductive lives. Women who are still fertile are assigned to powerful households as “Handmaids,” where they are forced to bear children for the ruling class. The narrator, Offred, describes her daily life in this system while recalling her former family, her daughter, her husband, and the world that existed before Gilead.

Atwood uses first-person narration to show how authoritarian power reaches into the body, language, memory, and private relationships. Religious quotations and rituals are used to justify political control, while uniforms and prescribed names turn women into visible categories. Offred’s memories do not appear as a complete, stable record; they return in fragments, shaped by fear, longing, and the need to preserve a sense of self. Her relationships with other women reveal both the possibilities of solidarity and the mistrust created by surveillance and punishment.

The novel draws on historical examples of gender oppression, reproductive control, religious authoritarianism, and the restriction of literacy. It should not be read as a literal prediction of one country’s future. Instead, it asks how rights can be dismantled gradually when a population is frightened, divided, or persuaded that emergency measures are necessary. The historical notes at the end add another layer by raising questions about who interprets the testimony of victims after a regime has fallen.

*The Handmaid’s Tale* is valuable for studying gender, bodily autonomy, political language, propaganda, memory, and resistance. It encourages readers to examine institutions critically and to recognize that rights require protection, not merely formal declaration.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解生育自主权、性别平等与公民权利。
- 分析宗教语言如何被政治权力利用。
- 观察监控制度对记忆、信任和社会关系的破坏。
- 讨论危机状态下权利如何被逐步剥夺。
- 研究第一人称叙事、碎片记忆与历史证词。
