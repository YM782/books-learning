# 020 · The Tale of Genji

- **作者：** Murasaki Shikibu
- **首次出版/成书：** c. 1008
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：日本古典文学核心作品，具有跨时代的文学史与文化研究价值；成书年代约为十一世纪初。**

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《源氏物语》通常被认为由平安时代宫廷女性作家紫式部创作，约在十一世纪初逐步形成。作品围绕光源氏的一生及其后代展开，描写宫廷恋爱、婚姻、嫉妒、政治关系、审美活动和时间流逝。人物生活在等级森严的宫廷社会，身份、家族关系和礼仪影响他们的情感选择；女性尤其受到婚姻制度、居住安排和男性权力的限制。紫式部细致描写人物的心理变化，也通过季节、花木、香气、音乐、书信和诗歌表现情绪与关系。光源氏拥有财富与魅力，却无法避免失去、衰老和情感上的不满足；后半部分的故事则进一步呈现年轻一代在复杂关系中寻找位置的困难。作品并非现代意义上的单一线性冒险小说，而是由多个章节和人物故事构成的宫廷社会长卷。它让读者看到日本古典审美中的物哀、无常和对细微情绪的敏感，也展示女性作者如何观察男性主导的制度。由于古典文本的成书过程、章节编排和现代译本之间存在学术问题，阅读时应留意版本说明。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

*The Tale of Genji*, traditionally attributed to Murasaki Shikibu, is a major work of Japanese literature from the Heian period. It was composed around the beginning of the eleventh century, although the precise stages of its creation and early circulation remain subjects of scholarly discussion. The narrative follows the life of Hikaru Genji, the son of an emperor, and later turns toward the experiences of younger generations connected to him. Through courtship, marriage, rivalry, political maneuvering, artistic gatherings, and personal loss, the work presents a detailed portrait of aristocratic life at the imperial court.

The characters live within a highly structured social world in which rank, family connections, ritual, and reputation shape personal possibilities. Women’s lives are particularly constrained by marriage arrangements, domestic seclusion, and the authority of men, yet Murasaki gives sustained attention to their emotions, intelligence, strategies, and vulnerabilities. The novel’s psychological subtlety is expressed through small details: seasonal changes, flowers, music, clothing, poetry, letters, and the atmosphere of rooms. These details do not merely decorate the story; they reveal feelings that characters cannot always express directly.

Genji is admired for his beauty and charm, but wealth and status cannot protect him from jealousy, aging, grief, or the instability of desire. The later sections shift attention toward the next generation and show how emotional patterns and social constraints continue beyond one individual life. Rather than following a single adventure plot, the work creates a broad social and psychological tapestry through interconnected episodes.

The Tale of Genji is valuable for understanding classical Japanese aesthetics, court culture, gender relations, memory, and impermanence. It is also important in the history of the novel, although claims about it as the world’s “first novel” depend on how the term is defined. Readers should consult notes on the edition and translation because the text has a complex manuscript and editorial history.

## 核心学习价值

- 认识平安时代宫廷制度、礼仪与审美文化。
- 理解物哀、无常及细节化情感描写。
- 分析女性在父权社会中的处境与能动性。
- 研究人物关系网络与多章节叙事结构。
- 比较古典日本文学与现代小说的形式差异。
