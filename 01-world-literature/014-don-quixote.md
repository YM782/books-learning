# 014 · Don Quixote

- **作者：** Miguel de Cervantes
- **首次出版/成书：** 1605–1615
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：现代小说发展史上的奠基作品；《卫报》2026 年百大小说榜第 26 名。**

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《堂吉诃德》讲述乡绅阿隆索·吉哈诺沉迷骑士小说，决定化名堂吉诃德，骑着瘦马罗西南多出门行侠。他邀请务实的农民桑丘·潘沙担任侍从，并把旅途中遇到的普通人和事物理解为骑士传奇中的人物与冒险：风车被看成巨人，客栈被当作城堡，现实冲突被解释为魔法或阴谋。第一部于1605年出版，第二部于1615年出版，后半部还让人物面对第一部出版后形成的公众名声。塞万提斯既讽刺过时的骑士文学，也对堂吉诃德的理想主义保持复杂的同情。堂吉诃德常因脱离现实而闹出笑话，却也表现出对弱者的关切、对不公的愤怒和不愿向庸俗现实妥协的勇气；桑丘则以生活经验、机智和现实需求不断修正主人的想象。两人相互影响，使小说超越单纯的滑稽冒险，成为关于理想与现实、阅读与身份、自由与社会秩序的思考。作品还通过多层叙述和对文学虚构本身的讨论，预示现代小说的自我反思传统。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Miguel de Cervantes’s *Don Quixote* tells the story of Alonso Quixano, a country gentleman whose excessive reading of chivalric romances convinces him to become a wandering knight. Taking the name Don Quixote, he sets out on an old horse, accompanied by Sancho Panza, a practical farmer who serves as his squire. Don Quixote interprets ordinary places and events through the imaginative world of knightly adventure: windmills become giants, inns become castles, and everyday conflicts appear to be the work of enchanters. These misreadings produce comic disasters, but they also reveal the distance between a person’s ideals and the world that resists them.

The novel appeared in two parts, published in 1605 and 1615. In the second part, the characters are aware of the fame created by the earlier book, and some people manipulate Don Quixote because they know his reputation. Cervantes therefore plays with the relationship between fiction, public identity, and reality. The book parodies the popular romances of chivalry, yet its attitude toward its hero is more complicated than simple ridicule. Don Quixote can be foolish and dangerous, but he also shows courage, generosity, and a desire to defend people he believes are being treated unjustly. Sancho’s practical wisdom often corrects his master, while Don Quixote’s imagination sometimes lifts Sancho beyond narrow self-interest.

Their relationship becomes a sustained conversation between idealism and realism. Cervantes asks whether a life guided by noble ideals is necessarily absurd when society is corrupt or indifferent, and whether practical common sense can become another form of limitation. The novel also experiments with unreliable narration, embedded stories, parody, and self-reference. It is often regarded as a foundational work of the modern novel because it makes storytelling itself part of the subject. Readers can study humor, social change in early modern Spain, the power of books to shape identity, and the tension between imagination and evidence.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解理想主义与现实主义之间的张力。
- 分析讽刺、戏仿和不可靠叙述。
- 思考阅读如何塑造身份、行为与世界观。
- 观察堂吉诃德与桑丘互相影响的过程。
- 认识早期现代西班牙社会及骑士文学传统。

## 关键词

idealism · realism · parody · imagination · identity

## 推荐读者

适合希望理解世界文学、历史背景、人物心理和社会结构，并愿意结合原著与可靠注释进行深入阅读的读者。
