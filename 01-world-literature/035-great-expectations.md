# 035 · Great Expectations

- **作者：** Charles Dickens
- **首次出版/成书：** 1860–1861 (serial); 1861 (book)
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：狄更斯代表作，英国现实主义文学与成长小说的重要经典。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《远大前程》讲述孤儿皮普从乡村铁匠家庭走向伦敦上流社会的成长经历。童年时，皮普在墓地遇到逃犯马格韦契，出于恐惧和同情帮助了他；后来一位匿名资助者为皮普提供财富，使他误以为自己将成为绅士，并把希望寄托在美丽而冷漠的艾丝黛拉身上。进入伦敦后，皮普逐渐疏远善良的姐夫乔和原有生活，开始追求身份、礼仪和社会地位。随着资助者身份揭晓，他发现自己对财富来源、阶级身份和真正的善意都存在误解。狄更斯通过皮普的回忆式叙述，展示羞耻、野心、感恩、偏见和自我欺骗如何塑造一个人的成长。郝薇香小姐停留在婚礼创伤中的生活，也揭示复仇如何腐蚀下一代。小说批判只凭出身和财富判断人的社会观念，同时提醒读者，阶级上升不等于人格成熟。皮普真正的成长发生在他重新理解责任、友谊和劳动尊严之后。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Charles Dickens’s *Great Expectations* follows Pip, an orphan raised by his sister and her husband, the village blacksmith Joe Gargery. As a child, Pip encounters an escaped convict named Magwitch in a churchyard and helps him out of fear and compassion. Years later, Pip is invited to visit the wealthy and eccentric Miss Havisham, whose adopted ward Estella makes him painfully aware of his humble background. When an anonymous benefactor provides money for Pip to become a gentleman in London, he assumes that Miss Havisham is preparing him for a future with Estella.

In the city, Pip becomes increasingly concerned with status, manners, and the appearance of gentility. He grows embarrassed by Joe and by the life he once knew. But the revelation of the benefactor’s identity overturns his assumptions about wealth, gratitude, and social respectability. Pip must confront the difference between the life he imagined and the moral obligations he neglected. Miss Havisham’s fixation on the betrayal she experienced in the past has also shaped Estella’s upbringing, showing how unresolved pain can be passed to another generation.

Dickens uses Pip’s retrospective first-person narration to examine ambition, shame, class prejudice, self-deception, and moral growth. The novel criticizes a society that treats money and birth as reliable measures of human worth. It also recognizes that social mobility can create new opportunities while encouraging people to reject those who supported them. Pip’s real development begins when he learns to value loyalty, kindness, work, and responsibility more than fashionable status.

*Great Expectations* is useful for studying Victorian class structure, education, crime, wealth, and the formation of identity. It also provides a rich example of how a narrator can look back on earlier mistakes and reinterpret them with greater maturity.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解维多利亚时代阶级结构与社会流动。
- 分析财富、礼仪和身份如何影响自我评价。
- 观察回忆式叙述中的成长与自我修正。
- 讨论感恩、友谊、劳动尊严和道德责任。
- 理解创伤与复仇如何影响下一代。
