# 024 · Romance of the Three Kingdoms

- **作者：** Traditionally attributed to Luo Guanzhong
- **首次出版/成书：** 14th century; earliest surviving printed edition 1522
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：中国历史演义文学代表作，长期影响东亚历史想象与大众文化。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《三国演义》以东汉末年至三国归晋的历史为基础，将政治斗争、军事冲突和人物传奇组织成宏大的历史叙事。小说从黄巾起义、董卓乱政和群雄割据写起，重点描绘曹操、刘备、孙权及其谋士和将领之间的联盟、战争与权力竞争。诸葛亮的谋略、关羽的忠义、张飞的勇猛、曹操的政治才能和复杂性格，都成为后世文化中反复引用的形象。作品通过桃园结义、三顾茅庐、赤壁之战等故事，讨论忠诚、名分、战略、人才和政治合法性。它并不是现代意义上的严格历史著作，而是在史书、民间传说和文学想象基础上形成的演义小说，部分人物形象和事件经过明显的道德化与戏剧化处理。阅读时应把文学人物与《三国志》等史料区分开来，避免把小说情节直接当作历史事实。它的学习价值在于理解传统政治文化中的权力观、组织能力、军事策略和英雄叙事，也能训练读者比较文学叙述与历史证据。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

*Romance of the Three Kingdoms* is a historical novel traditionally attributed to Luo Guanzhong. It is based on the period from the collapse of the Eastern Han dynasty through the rivalry of the Wei, Shu, and Wu states and the eventual reunification under the Jin. The narrative begins with rebellion and political disorder, then follows the competing ambitions of Cao Cao, Liu Bei, and Sun Quan, along with the strategists, generals, and advisers who serve them. Famous episodes include the oath of brotherhood in the Peach Garden, Liu Bei’s repeated visits to recruit Zhuge Liang, and the Battle of Red Cliffs.

The novel explores loyalty, political legitimacy, military strategy, personal virtue, and the role of capable advisers. Zhuge Liang becomes an emblem of strategic intelligence and devotion; Guan Yu represents loyalty and moral honor; Zhang Fei is remembered for courage and a volatile temperament; and Cao Cao is portrayed as both an exceptionally capable political actor and a morally ambiguous figure. These characters have shaped Chinese popular culture, theatre, literature, and later discussions of leadership.

Although the story draws on historical records, it is not a modern scholarly history. It combines official chronicles, oral storytelling, folk legends, and literary invention. Events are dramatized, characters are morally emphasized, and some episodes differ from the historical record. Readers should compare the novel with historical sources such as Chen Shou’s *Records of the Three Kingdoms* rather than treating every scene as factual evidence.

The book remains valuable for understanding traditional Chinese political imagination, the ethics of loyalty, the importance of organization and logistics, and the ways later generations turn history into cultural memory. It also provides a useful case study in the difference between historical evidence and narrative storytelling.

## 核心学习价值

- 学习比较文学叙事与正史史料，避免混淆虚构与事实。
- 分析政治合法性、忠诚、人才和组织能力。
- 理解战略、联盟、后勤与军事决策的作用。
- 认识诸葛亮、关羽等人物形象的文化影响。
- 研究历史如何被文学化并成为集体记忆。
