# 015 · The Brothers Karamazov

- **作者：** Fyodor Dostoevsky
- **首次出版/成书：** 1879–1880
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：俄国思想小说与心理小说的重要经典；《卫报》2026 年百大小说榜第 28 名。**

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《卡拉马佐夫兄弟》围绕父亲费奥多尔·卡拉马佐夫及其三个儿子德米特里、伊万和阿廖沙展开。父亲贪婪、放纵且缺乏责任感，三个儿子则代表不同的性格与思想方向：德米特里受激情、嫉妒和欲望驱使；伊万以理性和怀疑追问上帝、苦难与道德秩序；阿廖沙则在宗教信仰和对人的关怀中寻找生活道路。父子之间因金钱、遗产和同一位女性发生激烈冲突，最终演变成谋杀案件和司法审判。案件表面上要确定谁杀了父亲，深层却追问思想、欲望、行动和责任之间的关系。伊万关于无辜儿童受苦的质疑，挑战了简单的宗教解释；小说中的“大法官”故事则讨论人们是否愿意用自由交换安全与服从。陀思妥耶夫斯基让不同立场充分发声，并不把复杂问题压缩成单一结论。作品因此既是家庭悲剧和侦探式悬疑，也是关于信仰、怀疑、自由意志、罪责、宽恕和共同体的哲学小说。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Fyodor Dostoevsky’s *The Brothers Karamazov* centers on the troubled Karamazov family: the irresponsible father, Fyodor Pavlovich, and his three acknowledged sons, Dmitri, Ivan, and Alexei, usually called Alyosha. Each son approaches life from a different emotional and intellectual position. Dmitri is passionate, impulsive, and deeply involved in disputes over money and love. Ivan is intensely rational and skeptical, questioning whether a moral universe can be reconciled with the suffering of innocent children. Alyosha is a novice in a monastery whose faith is expressed through compassion, attention, and a desire to bring people together.

The family’s conflicts intensify around inheritance, resentment, and a woman loved by both Dmitri and their father. When Fyodor is murdered, suspicion falls on Dmitri, and the investigation and trial become the dramatic center of the novel. Yet the question of legal guilt is only one part of the book. Dostoevsky asks how ideas influence conduct, whether people are responsible for the consequences of beliefs they encourage, and what freedom means when human beings are capable of cruelty as well as love. Ivan’s arguments challenge easy religious answers, while the story of the Grand Inquisitor examines the temptation to trade freedom for security and unquestioned authority.

Dostoevsky gives competing voices substantial force rather than reducing the novel to a single philosophical lesson. Characters debate faith, doubt, suffering, justice, forgiveness, and the possibility of moral renewal. The result is at once a family tragedy, a psychological drama, a courtroom narrative, and a philosophical inquiry. Readers can use the novel to examine the relationship between intellectual conviction and personal responsibility, the limits of rational explanation, and the social consequences of treating other people as objects of desire or resentment.

## 核心学习价值

- 比较信仰、怀疑与理性在伦理问题上的不同立场。
- 讨论自由意志、罪责和行为后果。
- 分析家庭创伤、嫉妒与金钱冲突。
- 理解文学如何通过多人物对话展开哲学论证。
- 思考司法事实、道德责任与宽恕之间的区别。

## 关键词

faith · doubt · free will · guilt · moral responsibility

## 推荐读者

适合希望理解世界文学、历史背景、人物心理和社会结构，并愿意结合原著与可靠注释进行深入阅读的读者。
