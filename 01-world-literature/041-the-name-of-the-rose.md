# 041 · The Name of the Rose

- **作者：** Umberto Eco
- **首次出版/成书：** 1980
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：历史小说、侦探文学与思想小说结合的代表作。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《玫瑰之名》发生在十四世纪意大利北部一座本笃会修道院。年轻修士阿德索跟随导师威廉来到修道院，原本为参加神学会议，却卷入一连串离奇死亡事件。威廉以观察、逻辑推理和对文本的理解调查案件，发现图书馆、禁书、修道院权力斗争和宗教争论彼此关联。小说借助中世纪侦探故事，讨论知识是否应当开放、笑声是否威胁权威、解释文本的人是否能够掌控真理。威廉的推理并非永远正确，线索也可能被偶然和偏见误导，这使作品同时反思理性自身的局限。艾柯把符号学、神学、哲学和历史细节融入悬疑情节，让读者看到书籍既能保存知识，也可能成为权力争夺的对象。修道院的迷宫式图书馆象征知识的复杂结构，小说中的手稿和引文则让阅读本身成为故事的一部分。作品适合喜欢历史悬疑的读者，也适合研究中世纪思想、审查制度和文本解释的人。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Umberto Eco’s *The Name of the Rose* is set in a Benedictine monastery in northern Italy in 1327. The young novice Adso of Melk accompanies the Franciscan friar William of Baskerville, who has been asked to participate in a theological meeting. Their visit becomes an investigation when a series of monks die under mysterious circumstances. William uses observation, logic, and close attention to written evidence to reconstruct what may have happened. The monastery’s library, its restricted manuscripts, and the conflicts among religious authorities become central to the mystery.

The novel works as a medieval detective story, but its questions extend beyond the identity of a murderer. Eco explores the control of knowledge, the authority of interpretation, religious disagreement, censorship, and the political uses of learning. The library is arranged as a labyrinth, suggesting that knowledge can be difficult to access and that those who control texts can also influence what others are allowed to think. A manuscript associated with Aristotle’s discussion of comedy becomes especially important because laughter may challenge the seriousness and authority claimed by institutions.

William’s reasoning is powerful but not infallible. He sometimes follows patterns that later prove misleading, reminding readers that rational inquiry must remain open to uncertainty and correction. Adso’s retrospective narration also adds distance between events and their later interpretation. The novel blends historical detail, theological debate, semiotics, philosophy, and suspense, making reading and interpretation part of its subject.

*The Name of the Rose* is valuable for studying medieval European culture, the history of books, religious institutions, censorship, detective fiction, and the limits of knowledge. Readers interested in the history of ideas will find that the mystery is also an introduction to the question of who has the authority to define truth.

## 核心学习价值

- 认识十四世纪欧洲修道院、神学争论与知识制度。
- 分析图书馆、禁书和文本解释背后的权力关系。
- 学习侦探叙事中的证据推理与假设修正。
- 理解符号学、互文性和多层叙事。
- 讨论理性推理的力量及其局限。
