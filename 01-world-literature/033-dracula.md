# 033 · Dracula

- **作者：** Bram Stoker
- **首次出版/成书：** 1897
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：哥特恐怖文学经典，塑造现代大众文化中的德古拉形象。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《德古拉》以多种日记、信件、航海记录和报纸材料拼接叙事。年轻律师乔纳森·哈克前往特兰西瓦尼亚处理伯爵德古拉的房产事务，却逐渐发现自己被困在城堡，伯爵拥有超自然力量并计划前往英国。德古拉抵达后，露西和米娜等人物卷入吸血鬼袭击，范海辛教授组织众人追查并试图阻止灾难。作品通过不同人物的记录呈现信息不完整、证据积累和协作调查的过程，也让读者看到维多利亚时代对科学、医学、性别、外来者和社会秩序的焦虑。吸血鬼形象既是恐怖威胁，也承载欲望、疾病、死亡和文化边界等象征。小说中的女性人物并非完全被动，但其自主性也受到当时性别观念的限制。斯托克大量使用电报、打字机、录音等现代媒介，形成一种由文件和证词构成的“档案式”恐怖。它并非历史上第一部吸血鬼作品，却极大塑造了现代大众文化中德古拉的经典形象。阅读时可以比较小说的原始设定与后来电影、电视剧对吸血鬼的改编。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Bram Stoker’s *Dracula* is told through diaries, letters, ship logs, newspaper reports, and other documents. The story begins when Jonathan Harker, a young English solicitor, travels to Transylvania to assist Count Dracula with a property transaction. Harker gradually discovers that he is trapped in the count’s castle and that Dracula possesses supernatural powers. The count then travels to England, where his arrival brings danger to Lucy Westenra, Mina Murray, and the people close to them. Professor Van Helsing helps organize an investigation and a campaign to stop the vampire.

The documentary form is central to the novel’s suspense. No single narrator knows everything; readers assemble the truth from partial records, observations, medical reports, and personal testimony. The characters use modern technologies such as typewriters, telegrams, and recording devices, creating a contrast between scientific modernity and supernatural threat. This mixture reflects late Victorian anxieties about disease, sexuality, gender roles, immigration, imperial power, and the stability of social boundaries.

Dracula is both a frightening antagonist and a figure through whom the novel explores desire, contagion, death, and cultural difference. The women in the story have agency and intelligence, but their choices are also framed by the gender expectations of the period. Van Helsing’s group relies on cooperation, evidence, and specialized knowledge, although the novel’s moral world remains shaped by Victorian assumptions that modern readers may wish to question.

Stoker did not invent vampire literature, but his novel established many features of the vampire image that later became familiar in film, television, and popular culture. *Dracula* is useful for studying Gothic fiction, epistolary narration, Victorian social fears, and the relationship between science and superstition. Comparing the original text with later adaptations also shows how cultural icons change over time.

## 核心学习价值

- 学习书信体、日记和多材料拼接叙事。
- 认识维多利亚时代对疾病、性别与外来者的焦虑。
- 分析科学证据与超自然恐惧的冲突。
- 理解吸血鬼形象如何演变为大众文化符号。
- 比较原著与影视改编对人物和主题的重塑。
