# 043 · Midnight's Children

- **作者：** Salman Rushdie
- **首次出版/成书：** 1981
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：1981年布克奖获奖作品，后殖民文学的标志性小说。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《午夜之子》以印度1947年独立时刻为起点，讲述午夜零点出生的萨利姆·西奈及其家庭与国家历史之间的联系。萨利姆声称自己与同一时刻出生的其他儿童拥有特殊能力，并能通过心灵感应与他们交流。随着印度经历分治、政治冲突、战争和紧急状态，萨利姆的身体、记忆和身份也不断变化。小说将个人成长与国家建构并置，追问一个新国家如何处理殖民遗产、宗教差异、语言多样性和政治权力。萨利姆是一个不完全可靠的叙述者，他的记忆会出错，讲述也受到自我想象和家庭故事影响，因此读者必须区分历史事实、个人回忆与魔幻叙事。拉什迪通过夸张、讽刺、神话和多语言表达，展现印度社会的复杂性，同时反思民族历史如何被个人叙述重组。作品并不把国家比作一个稳定统一的整体，而是呈现不同地区、群体和政治力量之间的冲突。阅读时可以结合印度独立、印巴分治和1975—1977年紧急状态的历史资料，理解小说的现实背景。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Salman Rushdie’s *Midnight’s Children* begins at the moment India becomes independent from British rule on 15 August 1947. Saleem Sinai is born at midnight, at the same instant as the new nation, and he believes that his life is mysteriously connected to the country’s history. He discovers that children born in the first hour of independence possess unusual gifts and can communicate through telepathy. Their different abilities and backgrounds become a fictional image of India’s diversity, hopes, and political tensions.

Saleem’s personal story moves through family secrets, Partition, migration, war, political upheaval, and the Emergency of 1975–1977. His body and memory seem to reflect the changes taking place around him, but he is not a fully reliable narrator. He makes mistakes, revises earlier claims, and shapes events through his own imagination. The reader must therefore distinguish historical events from personal memory, myth, exaggeration, and deliberate storytelling.

Rushdie uses magical realism, satire, wordplay, and multiple narrative voices to explore the challenges of building a postcolonial nation. India appears not as a single, unified identity but as a vast and contested collection of languages, religions, regions, and political interests. The novel asks how national history is remembered, who is allowed to tell it, and how private lives become entangled with public events. Saleem’s relationship to the nation is both intimate and unstable: he is proud of his historical connection but also overwhelmed by forces beyond his control.

*Midnight’s Children* is valuable for studying postcolonial identity, national memory, Partition, political power, and the relationship between fiction and history. Readers will gain more from the novel by placing its imaginative episodes alongside reliable accounts of modern South Asian history.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解印度独立、印巴分治和紧急状态的历史背景。
- 分析个人记忆与国家历史如何相互塑造。
- 学习魔幻现实主义、不可靠叙述和讽刺。
- 认识后殖民国家中的语言、宗教和地区多样性。
- 区分文学虚构、历史事件与叙述者的主观记忆。
