# 022 · Journey to the West

- **作者：** Traditionally attributed to Wu Cheng'en
- **首次出版/成书：** 1592 (earliest extant complete printed edition)
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：中国古典小说代表作，影响东亚民间文化、戏曲和现代大众叙事。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《西游记》以唐僧玄奘西行取经的历史传说为基础，加入大量神魔想象和民间故事。孙悟空因大闹天宫被压在五行山下，后来成为唐僧的徒弟，与猪八戒、沙和尚一同保护师父前往西天。取经途中，他们遭遇妖怪、国王、神仙和各种考验，许多冲突既来自外部危险，也来自贪欲、虚荣、恐惧和自我控制不足。孙悟空机智勇敢，却常因冲动和骄傲惹出麻烦；猪八戒贪吃好色，带来喜剧效果，也体现普通人的弱点；唐僧则坚持信念，却有时缺乏辨别能力。故事通过一系列相对独立的冒险，讨论修行、忠诚、师徒关系、欲望与成长。作品融合佛教、道教、儒家伦理、神话、讽刺和民间幽默，既有丰富的想象世界，也映照现实社会中的权力与人情。它不是严格的宗教教义文本，人物和情节经过文学加工。阅读时可以把它看作一部寓言式成长旅程，观察角色如何在一次次危机中认识自己的局限。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

*Journey to the West* is a Chinese classical novel built around the legendary pilgrimage of the Buddhist monk Xuanzang to India to obtain sacred scriptures. The story combines historical inspiration with mythology, popular religion, satire, and imaginative adventure. The most famous companion is Sun Wukong, the Monkey King, who rebels against Heaven, is imprisoned beneath a mountain, and later becomes the monk’s disciple. Along the journey, Sun Wukong is joined by Zhu Bajie and Sha Wujing. Together they protect their master from demons, rulers, spirits, and other dangers as they travel toward the Western regions.

The episodes often work as individual adventures, but they also develop the characters’ moral and spiritual challenges. Sun Wukong is courageous, inventive, and powerful, yet his pride and impatience repeatedly create difficulties. Zhu Bajie is comic and deeply attached to food, comfort, and pleasure; his weaknesses make him recognizable as an ordinary human figure. Sha Wujing is quieter and more dependable, while Xuanzang’s commitment to the pilgrimage is sometimes undermined by his limited ability to judge appearances. Their disagreements and cooperation make the journey a story about discipline, loyalty, trust, and self-transformation.

The novel draws on Buddhist ideas of spiritual cultivation, Daoist cosmology, Confucian social values, and a broad range of folk beliefs. It also uses humor to expose vanity, greed, corruption, and the abuse of authority. The fantastical world should not be mistaken for a direct statement of religious doctrine: its gods, demons, and magical events are shaped by literary invention and popular storytelling. The earliest surviving complete printed edition appeared in 1592, and the work is traditionally attributed to Wu Cheng’en, although questions about authorship and textual history have been discussed by scholars. Readers can approach it as an imaginative adventure, a moral allegory, and a rich introduction to Chinese popular culture.

## 核心学习价值

- 认识中国古典神魔小说、民间信仰和宗教想象。
- 分析孙悟空、猪八戒等人物的性格缺点与成长。
- 理解冒险单元结构如何共同构成修行旅程。
- 讨论欲望、自律、忠诚和团队合作。
- 区分文学改编、历史原型与宗教教义。
