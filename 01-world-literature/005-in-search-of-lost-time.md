# 005 · In Search of Lost Time（追忆似水年华）

- **作者：** Marcel Proust（马塞尔·普鲁斯特）
- **首次出版：** 1913–1927 年分卷出版
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 《卫报》2026 年 100 部最佳小说榜列第 5；以记忆、时间、艺术与意识探索现代小说的可能性。
- **阅读提示：** 这是一部长篇多卷作品。可以先理解叙述者的成长、社交圈变化与“非自愿记忆”这一核心概念。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《追忆似水年华》是一部由七卷组成的长篇小说，叙述者通过童年记忆、家庭生活、社交往来、爱情经历和艺术思考，回望自己如何理解时间与人生。作品最著名的场景之一，是叙述者品尝玛德莱娜小点心后，突然被气味和味觉带回童年贡布雷的往事。这种不由自主涌现的记忆，说明过去并未真正消失，而是潜藏在感官经验中，可能在某个瞬间重新变得鲜活。小说描写贵族沙龙、巴黎社会、爱情中的嫉妒与占有、友谊的变化，以及人们如何通过身份和礼仪构建自我形象。叙述者也逐渐认识到，记忆并不是客观录像：当下的情绪会改变我们对过去的理解，时间则会重新塑造人物和关系。随着经历积累，他开始思考艺术是否能够把流逝的经验转化为持久形式。普鲁斯特以绵长句子、细密观察和反复回环的主题，展示意识如何在记忆、欲望、失落和想象之间移动。作品最终把写作本身变成主题：寻找失去的时间，也是在寻找理解生活、认识自我和创造艺术的可能。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Marcel Proust’s *In Search of Lost Time* is a seven-volume novel sequence in which an unnamed narrator reflects on childhood, family life, friendship, love, social ambition, artistic development, and the passage of time. The work is famous for its exploration of involuntary memory: an ordinary sensory experience can unexpectedly restore a distant moment with extraordinary vividness. In the best-known example, the taste of a madeleine dipped in tea brings the narrator back to childhood visits to Combray. The memory is not deliberately summoned; it emerges through sensation and reveals that the past can remain present in forms that conscious recollection cannot easily reach.

Across the volumes, Proust portrays the social worlds of the French upper classes, including salons, aristocratic families, artistic circles, and the changing public life of Paris. The narrator observes how people construct identities through manners, reputation, desire, and social position. Romantic love is often marked by jealousy and uncertainty, while friendship and status prove unstable. Characters change over time, and the narrator’s understanding of them changes as well. Proust suggests that memory is not a neutral record: present feelings reshape the past, and later knowledge can transform the meaning of earlier experiences.

The novel’s long sentences and reflective digressions reproduce the movement of consciousness. Small details—sounds, gestures, places, tastes, and habits—become gateways to larger questions about identity and mortality. As the narrator matures, he begins to see artistic creation as a way to give form to experiences that time would otherwise dissolve. The search for lost time therefore becomes a search for the conditions of art and self-understanding. Proust’s work is demanding, but it offers an unusually rich study of memory, perception, social behavior, desire, and the relationship between lived experience and literary form.

## 核心学习价值

1. **记忆与认知：** 理解感官经验如何触发非自愿记忆，以及记忆如何重构过去。
2. **社会观察：** 分析阶层、礼仪、声望和社交关系如何塑造身份。
3. **心理描写：** 学习细节、联想和内心独白如何呈现复杂意识。
4. **艺术哲学：** 思考艺术如何保存经验并赋予生活意义。
5. **长篇阅读能力：** 练习追踪多卷作品中的主题变化、人物发展与叙述者成长。
