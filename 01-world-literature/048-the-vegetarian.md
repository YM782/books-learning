# 048 · The Vegetarian

- **作者：** Han Kang
- **首次出版/成书：** 2007 (Korean); English translation 2015
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：2016年国际布克奖获奖作品，韩国当代文学的重要代表。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《素食者》分为三个部分，从不同人物的视角讲述韩国女性英惠突然决定不再吃肉后，生活如何发生剧烈变化。第一部分由丈夫叙述，他把英惠视为普通、顺从的妻子，无法理解她的拒绝；第二部分聚焦姐夫对英惠身体和艺术想象的投射；第三部分则转向姐姐仁惠，呈现她在照顾家庭、承担责任和理解妹妹之间的痛苦。英惠的选择逐渐超出饮食习惯，成为对暴力、父权控制和社会规范的拒绝，但小说并未把她的行为简化成单一政治宣言，也没有提供明确的心理诊断。多重叙述让读者看到，英惠很少拥有直接解释自己的机会，周围人不断替她定义、使用或管理她的身体。作品以冷静而压迫的语言，讨论身体自主、家庭暴力、欲望、疾病和社会排斥。阅读时应尊重文本的复杂性，避免把人物的痛苦浪漫化，或将文学描写当作临床医学说明。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Han Kang’s *The Vegetarian* is a three-part novel about Yeong-hye, a South Korean woman who abruptly decides to stop eating meat. Her decision disrupts her marriage and family relationships, but the novel gradually shows that the conflict is about much more than food. In the first section, her husband narrates his response to a wife he had considered ordinary and compliant. He is disturbed not only by her refusal but also by the loss of control over the domestic role he expected her to perform.

The second section shifts to the perspective of Yeong-hye’s brother-in-law, an artist whose fascination with her body becomes entangled with his own desires and ambitions. In the final section, the focus moves to her sister In-hye, who must confront the demands of family care, responsibility, and her own emotional exhaustion. Yeong-hye rarely receives a direct narrative voice. Instead, readers see how other people interpret, manage, and project meanings onto her body.

The novel explores bodily autonomy, violence, gender expectations, desire, mental distress, and the limits of family care. Yeong-hye’s refusal can be read as resistance to social and familial control, but the text does not reduce her actions to a simple political statement or provide a definitive clinical explanation. The shifting perspectives make the reader aware of how easily a person’s experience can be replaced by the interpretations of others.

Han Kang’s restrained and unsettling style creates a world in which ordinary family interactions can become coercive. *The Vegetarian* is valuable for discussing the body, power, consent, gender, and the ethics of care. Readers should avoid romanticizing the character’s suffering or treating a fictional narrative as a medical case study. The novel’s force lies in its unresolved questions about freedom and the cost of refusing social expectations.

## 核心学习价值

- 分析身体自主权、性别规范与家庭权力。
- 理解多视角叙事如何揭示解释权的不平等。
- 讨论同意、照护、暴力和社会排斥。
- 区分文学象征与临床医学诊断。
- 思考个体拒绝社会规范时所承受的代价。
