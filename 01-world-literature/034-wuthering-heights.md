# 034 · Wuthering Heights

- **作者：** Emily Brontë
- **首次出版/成书：** 1847
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：英国文学中影响深远的哥特与浪漫主义小说。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《呼啸山庄》以英格兰约克郡荒原为背景，通过房客洛克伍德和管家丁耐莉的层层讲述，回溯恩萧与林顿两个家庭两代人的关系。希斯克利夫幼年被恩萧先生带回家，却长期遭受排斥和羞辱；他与凯瑟琳之间形成强烈感情，但阶级差异、家庭压力和个人选择使两人无法共同生活。凯瑟琳嫁给埃德加·林顿后，希斯克利夫离开又归来，逐渐以财富和操纵展开报复，使下一代也陷入仇恨循环。小说中的荒原、天气、房屋和封闭空间强化了人物情绪，也象征阶级边界与无法摆脱的过去。作品并非简单的浪漫爱情故事：希斯克利夫的痛苦不能为他的残酷开脱，凯瑟琳的激情也伴随着自我中心和矛盾。艾米莉·勃朗特通过不可靠叙述、时间跳跃和哥特氛围，探讨爱、创伤、阶级、复仇和代际传递。阅读时要辨认两代人物重复的姓名和关系，并思考故事中谁在讲述、讲述者掌握了多少事实。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Emily Brontë’s *Wuthering Heights* is set on the Yorkshire moors and follows the intertwined histories of the Earnshaw and Linton families. The story reaches readers through layers of narration: the tenant Lockwood records what he hears from the housekeeper Nelly Dean, who recounts events from years earlier. At the center is Heathcliff, an orphan brought into the Earnshaw household by Mr. Earnshaw. He forms an intense bond with Catherine Earnshaw, but social prejudice, humiliation, and Catherine’s decision to marry Edgar Linton separate them.

Heathcliff leaves and later returns with wealth and power. Rather than finding peace, he uses manipulation and revenge to control the people around him, extending the conflict into the next generation. The younger characters inherit the consequences of earlier decisions, although some gradually find a way to escape the cycle of hostility. The moors, the two houses, and the changing weather create a landscape that reflects emotional intensity and social division. The setting is not merely decorative; it helps make the past feel physically present.

The novel is often described as a love story, but its relationships are marked by obsession, cruelty, dependence, and unequal power. Heathcliff’s suffering helps explain his anger but does not excuse the harm he causes. Catherine’s passionate self-image also contains contradictions and selfishness. Brontë uses Gothic atmosphere, unreliable narration, shifts in time, and repeated names to make readers question how stories are remembered and transmitted.

*Wuthering Heights* is valuable for studying class, trauma, revenge, family inheritance, narrative perspective, and the difference between love and possession. Its structure rewards careful attention to the two generations and to the limits of each narrator’s knowledge.

## 核心学习价值

- 分析阶级差异、家庭权力与亲密关系。
- 区分爱、执念、占有欲和暴力。
- 研究不可靠叙述、时间跳跃和双层叙事。
- 理解创伤如何跨代传递并影响家庭。
- 认识哥特文学与浪漫主义传统。

## 关键词

obsession · class · revenge · trauma · Gothic

适合希望理解世界文学、历史背景、人物心理和社会结构，并愿意结合原著与可靠注释进行深入阅读的读者。
