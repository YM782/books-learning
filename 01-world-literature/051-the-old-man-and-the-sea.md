# 051 · The Old Man and the Sea

- **作者：** Ernest Hemingway
- **首次出版/成书：** 1952
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：1953年普利策小说奖获奖作品，海明威代表作。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《老人与海》讲述古巴老渔夫圣地亚哥连续八十四天没有捕到鱼后，独自出海远航，终于钓到一条巨大的马林鱼。他与鱼在海上僵持数日，忍受饥饿、疼痛和疲惫，既尊重对手的力量，也坚持不放弃。捕获大鱼后，鲨鱼不断袭击，最终只留下鱼骨。圣地亚哥回到岸上时身体受伤、收获被毁，却没有完全失去尊严。小说通过极简的语言、重复的动作和自然意象，讨论失败、坚持、劳动、孤独和人与自然的关系。圣地亚哥的胜利并非物质上的成功，而是他在无法控制结果的情况下仍保持勇气和技艺。作品也描写他与少年马诺林之间的师徒情谊，体现经验、关怀和代际传承。阅读时应避免把故事简化成“只要努力就会成功”的励志口号；它更深刻地呈现努力与结果可能分离，以及人在损失面前如何保持自我。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Ernest Hemingway’s *The Old Man and the Sea* tells the story of Santiago, an aging Cuban fisherman who has gone eighty-four days without catching a fish. On the next day he sails far into the Gulf Stream and hooks an enormous marlin. The fish is powerful enough to pull his skiff for days, and Santiago must endure hunger, pain, exhaustion, and isolation while trying to bring it under control. He respects the marlin as a worthy opponent and recognizes that the struggle is not simply a matter of strength. It also requires patience, skill, experience, and an ability to endure uncertainty.

After Santiago finally kills the fish and ties it to his boat, sharks are attracted by its blood. He fights them with the tools he has, but they gradually consume the marlin until only its skeleton remains. Santiago returns to shore physically damaged and without the material reward he hoped to bring home. Yet the loss does not erase his skill or dignity. The boy Manolin, who has been prevented by his parents from fishing with the old man, remains loyal to him and promises to fish together again.

Hemingway’s spare prose emphasizes action, repetition, physical sensation, and the natural environment. The sea is both a source of livelihood and a force beyond human control. The novel explores labor, aging, pride, companionship, defeat, and the meaning of achievement when outcomes cannot be guaranteed. Santiago’s struggle should not be reduced to a simple motivational slogan: the story recognizes that effort does not always produce success, while suggesting that a person’s dignity may depend on how they meet hardship.

The novella is valuable for studying modern prose style, symbolism, human endurance, and the relationship between people and nature. It also offers a subtle portrait of mentorship and intergenerational friendship.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解努力与结果并不总是相等的现实。
- 分析简洁文体、重复动作和象征意象。
- 讨论尊严、衰老、劳动和人与自然的关系。
- 观察圣地亚哥与马诺林之间的代际支持。
- 避免把复杂的失败经验简化为成功学口号。
