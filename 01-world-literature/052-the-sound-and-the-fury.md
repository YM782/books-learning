# 052 · The Sound and the Fury

- **作者：** William Faulkner
- **首次出版/成书：** 1929
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：美国现代主义小说的重要代表，叙事形式影响深远。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《喧哗与骚动》描写美国南方康普生家族的衰败，通过四个部分呈现家族成员不同的意识和记忆。第一部分由具有认知障碍的本吉叙述，时间顺序不断跳跃，气味、声音和场景触发过去与现在交错；第二部分进入昆丁的内心，表现他对妹妹凯蒂、荣誉观念和家族衰落的痛苦执念；第三部分由杰生叙述，展现怨恨、控制和自我辩护；第四部分转向黑人女仆迪尔西及其社区，提供不同于康普生家族的道德视角。小说的形式本身就是主题：家族成员无法共享同一段历史，记忆和语言也无法恢复失去的秩序。福克纳描写南方旧贵族的经济衰败、种族等级、性别规范和现代化冲击，同时批判家族成员用荣誉和传统掩盖自身责任。作品对意识流、时间结构和叙事视角进行了大胆实验，初读可能困难，适合分部分阅读并记录人物、时间和叙述者。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

William Faulkner’s *The Sound and the Fury* portrays the decline of the Compson family, a once-prominent Southern household in Mississippi. The novel is divided into four sections, each presenting events through a different consciousness or narrative perspective. The first section is narrated by Benjy, who has an intellectual disability and experiences time through sensory associations rather than a stable chronological order. Sounds, smells, and familiar places trigger memories that move between past and present. The second section enters the mind of Quentin, whose obsession with his sister Caddy, family honor, and the collapse of the old Southern order leads to emotional crisis. The third section is narrated by Jason, whose bitterness and controlling behavior reveal another form of family dysfunction. The final section shifts toward Dilsey, the Black servant whose perspective offers a moral and communal contrast to the Compsons.

The novel’s structure is not merely a formal experiment. Each narrator remembers and interprets the family differently, and no single voice can restore a complete or stable account of the past. The fragmentation of time reflects the collapse of inherited social structures and the characters’ inability to communicate across emotional and historical divisions. Faulkner examines Southern class identity, racism, gender expectations, economic decline, and the burden of family reputation.

The book can be challenging because it requires readers to reconstruct chronology and distinguish among narrators. That difficulty is central to its artistic design: readers experience how memory can be partial, repetitive, and shaped by obsession. The novel also invites critical attention to the racial hierarchy of its setting and to the limited narrative space given to some characters.

*The Sound and the Fury* is valuable for studying modernist fiction, stream of consciousness, unreliable narration, memory, and the social history of the American South. A practical approach is to read one section at a time and keep a timeline of the events being recalled.

## 核心学习价值

- 学习意识流、多视角和非线性时间结构。
- 分析记忆、创伤与家族叙事之间的关系。
- 认识美国南方旧贵族衰落与种族等级背景。
- 辨认叙述者的偏见和信息局限。
- 训练复杂现代主义小说的分段阅读能力。
