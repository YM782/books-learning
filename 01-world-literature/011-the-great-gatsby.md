# 011 · The Great Gatsby

- **作者：** F. Scott Fitzgerald
- **首次出版/成书：** 1925
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：美国现代文学经典；《卫报》2026 年百大小说榜第 11 名。**

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《了不起的盖茨比》以美国二十世纪二十年代的纽约长岛为背景，通过旁观者尼克·卡拉威的叙述，讲述神秘富豪杰伊·盖茨比对旧爱黛西·布坎南的执着。盖茨比凭借财富举办盛大宴会，希望重新赢回黛西，也希望证明自己能够跨越阶级差异、重写过去。然而黛西已经身处富裕而稳固的婚姻与社交圈，盖茨比的梦想逐渐暴露出建立在幻想、金钱和社会地位之上的脆弱性。小说描写爵士时代的消费文化、投机致富、阶级壁垒、婚姻关系和道德冷漠，并通过绿灯、灰烬谷、汽车等意象表现美国梦的诱惑与幻灭。尼克既被盖茨比的希望打动，也逐渐看清富人逃避责任、让他人承担后果的现实。作品并非简单否定追求梦想，而是追问：当梦想被财富、占有欲和对过去的执念支配时，它是否还具有真正的自由与意义？

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

F. Scott Fitzgerald’s *The Great Gatsby* is set among wealthy communities on Long Island and in New York City during the summer of 1922. The story is narrated by Nick Carraway, a young man who rents a modest house beside the mansion of Jay Gatsby, a mysterious millionaire famous for his extravagant parties. Gatsby has built his wealth and public identity around one private hope: to reunite with Daisy Buchanan, the woman he loved before the First World War. Daisy is now married to Tom Buchanan, whose inherited wealth and social position give him confidence and protection. Gatsby believes that money can restore the past and make Daisy choose him, but the social world he wants to enter is less open than he imagines.

As Nick becomes involved in Gatsby’s plans, the novel reveals the inequalities and moral evasions beneath the glamour of the Jazz Age. The characters move through luxury, automobiles, parties, affairs, and financial speculation, yet their lives are marked by insecurity, carelessness, and dissatisfaction. The valley of ashes represents the human cost hidden behind the wealth of the privileged, while the green light across the water becomes an image of desire and an endlessly postponed future. Gatsby’s dream is moving because it expresses hope and self-invention, but it is also fragile because it depends on an idealized memory and on the belief that wealth can overcome class boundaries.

Fitzgerald’s novel is both a portrait of a particular American decade and a critique of the American Dream. Nick’s changing judgment of Gatsby forces readers to distinguish ambition from illusion, romance from possession, and social success from moral character. The book is valuable for studying class, consumer culture, memory, symbolism, unreliable admiration, and the tension between individual aspiration and structural inequality.

## 核心学习价值

- 分析美国梦、阶级流动与财富特权之间的矛盾。
- 理解象征意象如何承载人物欲望与社会批评。
- 观察叙述者尼克的立场变化及其可靠性。
- 讨论记忆、爱情理想化与现实之间的冲突。
- 认识爵士时代美国的消费文化和社会分层。

## 关键词

American Dream · class · wealth · memory · symbolism
