# 006 · Anna Karenina（安娜·卡列尼娜）

- **作者：** Leo Tolstoy（列夫·托尔斯泰）
- **首次出版：** 1875–1877 年分期连载；1878 年出版单行本
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 《卫报》2026 年 100 部最佳小说榜列第 6；以家庭、婚姻、社会规范与个人选择构成现实主义文学的重要范本。
- **阅读提示：** 除安娜与渥伦斯基的关系外，也要关注列文的乡村生活、劳动体验和精神探索。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《安娜·卡列尼娜》以十九世纪俄国贵族社会为背景，围绕婚姻、爱情、家庭责任与社会道德展开。安娜是一位已婚贵妇，在前往莫斯科处理家庭事务时结识军官渥伦斯基，两人逐渐陷入爱情。她选择离开原有婚姻，却发现上流社会对女性的道德要求极为严苛：同样的情感越界，男性往往仍能保有社会地位，女性却可能失去家庭、名誉和与孩子相处的权利。与安娜的故事并行的是列文，他在乡村经营、婚姻和劳动中寻找生活意义，反复思考信仰、死亡、幸福与社会改革。托尔斯泰并未把人物简单划分为善恶，而是通过舞会、家庭争执、农事、社交活动和内心独白，展示制度压力与个人欲望如何相互冲突。小说既是爱情悲剧，也是对俄国社会阶层、性别双重标准、家庭制度和精神生活的广阔观察。安娜的命运促使读者思考：一个社会如何对待选择不同生活道路的人？个人自由与责任之间又应如何平衡？

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Leo Tolstoy’s *Anna Karenina* is set among the Russian aristocracy in the nineteenth century and examines marriage, desire, family duty, social judgment, and the search for a meaningful life. Anna, a married woman with a young son, meets the cavalry officer Count Vronsky while visiting Moscow. Their attraction develops into a passionate relationship, and Anna eventually leaves her husband. Yet the consequences are unequal and severe. The social world that tolerates male infidelity offers Anna little room to live openly after she rejects the expectations placed on a respectable wife and mother. Her growing isolation exposes the double standards that govern reputation, gender, and public morality.

Running alongside Anna’s story is the life of Konstantin Levin, a landowner who struggles with work, marriage, faith, and the purpose of existence. Levin’s experiences on his estate, his relationship with Kitty, and his reflections on labor and belief provide a contrasting path through the novel. Whereas Anna’s life becomes increasingly constrained by social exclusion and emotional turmoil, Levin searches for a form of happiness grounded in ordinary responsibility and a sense of connection to others.

Tolstoy’s narrative moves between households, cities, rural estates, political conversations, family gatherings, and private consciousness. Its large cast allows the author to examine how social institutions shape personal choices without reducing characters to simple examples. The novel is not merely a story of forbidden love; it is also a study of class, gender inequality, parenting, economic change, religion, and moral uncertainty. Tolstoy asks readers to consider the difference between public respectability and genuine ethical life. The parallel stories of Anna and Levin make the book especially rich for discussing freedom, responsibility, social hypocrisy, and the many competing definitions of happiness.

## 核心学习价值

1. **性别与社会规范：** 分析社会对男性和女性的道德要求为何并不对等。
2. **婚姻与家庭：** 理解爱情、亲子关系、责任和个人选择之间的冲突。
3. **现实主义写作：** 观察多人物、多场景叙事如何构成完整社会图景。
4. **伦理思考：** 区分社会评价、个人欲望与实际道德责任。
5. **人生意义：** 通过列文的经历思考劳动、信仰、幸福与日常生活。
