# 038 · The Count of Monte Cristo

- **作者：** Alexandre Dumas
- **首次出版/成书：** 1844–1846 (serial)
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：世界冒险小说经典，对通俗叙事和复仇题材影响深远。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《基督山伯爵》讲述年轻水手爱德蒙·唐泰斯在即将获得晋升和结婚时，遭到嫉妒者陷害，被投入伊夫堡监狱。多年囚禁期间，他结识法里亚神父，从对方那里学习知识并获知宝藏秘密。越狱后，唐泰斯找到财富，以“基督山伯爵”的身份回到社会，精心安排计划惩罚当年背叛自己的人。复仇逐渐揭示出不同人物的贪婪、野心和道德弱点，但也让伯爵面对一个更困难的问题：一个人是否有权扮演上帝，决定他人的命运？小说以冒险、秘密身份、阴谋和戏剧性转折推动情节，同时讨论司法不公、阶级、财富、忠诚、宽恕和时间。唐泰斯从无辜受害者变成拥有巨大权力的人，复仇带来的满足并非没有代价；无辜者也可能被卷入他的计划。作品提醒读者，正义与报复并不相同，掌握资源和信息的人更需要自我约束。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Alexandre Dumas’s *The Count of Monte Cristo* follows Edmond Dantès, a young sailor whose future appears promising: he is about to become captain of a ship and marry Mercédès. Jealous rivals and political circumstances lead to a false accusation, and Dantès is imprisoned without a fair trial in the Château d’If. During his long confinement, he meets Abbé Faria, a learned prisoner who educates him and reveals the location of a hidden treasure. After escaping, Dantès acquires immense wealth and returns to society under the identity of the Count of Monte Cristo.

The count carefully constructs a plan to expose and punish the people responsible for his imprisonment. His knowledge, money, and ability to assume different identities allow him to influence events on a scale that would once have been impossible. The revenge plot reveals the greed, ambition, corruption, and moral weaknesses of several powerful figures. Yet as the consequences unfold, Dantès must confront a difficult question: does suffering give a person the right to control the lives of others? His actions affect not only his enemies but also innocent people who become entangled in the plan.

Dumas combines adventure, mystery, political intrigue, romance, and social observation. The novel explores wrongful imprisonment, the misuse of public authority, class, wealth, loyalty, forgiveness, and the passage of time. Dantès’s transformation from an innocent young man into a wealthy avenger is thrilling, but it also raises ethical questions about the difference between justice and retaliation. The story suggests that power can create opportunities for correction, yet it can also tempt people to exceed the limits of legitimate responsibility.

Originally published in serial form from 1844 to 1846, the novel is an influential example of popular historical adventure fiction. It is useful for studying narrative suspense, revenge, legal injustice, and the moral limits of personal retribution.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解冤狱、司法不公与权力滥用。
- 区分正义、报复和个人惩罚。
- 分析财富、知识和身份如何改变行动能力。
- 研究连载小说的悬念、秘密身份和情节布局。
- 讨论复仇对无辜者及复仇者自身的代价。

## 关键词

revenge · justice · wrongful imprisonment · power · forgiveness
