# 012 · Nineteen Eighty-Four

- **作者：** George Orwell
- **首次出版/成书：** 1949
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：现代政治寓言经典；《卫报》2026 年百大小说榜第 16 名。**

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《一九八四》描绘一个由党和“大洋国”统治的极权社会。主人公温斯顿·史密斯在真理部工作，负责修改旧报纸和历史记录，使过去永远符合党的当前说法。屏幕、监听、告密制度和无处不在的“老大哥”形象，使人们不仅失去行动自由，也无法确定自己的记忆是否真实。温斯顿暗中写日记，并与朱莉娅建立关系，试图保留私人情感和独立判断。然而思想警察通过监控、审讯和心理操控逐步摧毁他的反抗。小说提出的核心问题不是单纯的政府暴力，而是权力能否控制语言、事实和人的内心。当词汇被删减、历史被篡改、矛盾被要求同时相信时，个人判断能力也会被削弱。“双重思想”“新话”和“思想罪”等概念因此成为理解宣传、信息操控和政治压迫的重要工具。奥威尔写作时借鉴了二十世纪极权主义经验，但作品的价值不在于把任何现实社会简单等同于虚构国家，而在于提供一套观察权力如何侵蚀真相与自由的警示框架。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

George Orwell’s *Nineteen Eighty-Four* imagines a future society in which the Party controls the state of Oceania through surveillance, propaganda, censorship, and systematic psychological pressure. The protagonist, Winston Smith, works at the Ministry of Truth, where his job is to alter historical records so that the past always supports the Party’s current claims. Public screens monitor citizens, children are encouraged to report disloyal behavior, and the image of Big Brother reminds everyone that private life is never entirely private. The Party seeks more than obedience: it wants to control memory, language, and the ability to distinguish truth from falsehood.

Winston begins to resist in small ways. He keeps a secret diary, questions official accounts of history, and develops a relationship with Julia. Their private experiences seem to create a space beyond political control. Yet the Thought Police eventually discover them, and Winston is subjected to interrogation and torture designed to break not only his resistance but also his confidence in his own perception. Orwell’s invented concepts—including Newspeak, doublethink, thoughtcrime, and the rewriting of history—show how political domination can operate through the structure of everyday language and accepted knowledge.

The novel was written in the aftermath of fascism, Stalinism, and the Second World War. It is not a simple prediction of one future regime; it is a warning about the conditions that allow authoritarian power to flourish. When institutions monopolize information, erase evidence, punish dissent, and demand that citizens accept contradictions, independent judgment becomes difficult to sustain. *Nineteen Eighty-Four* remains useful for studying propaganda, political language, privacy, historical memory, surveillance, and civil liberties. Readers should also distinguish Orwell’s fictional scenario from specific contemporary cases by examining evidence rather than relying on slogans.

## 核心学习价值

- 识别宣传、审查和信息垄断的运作方式。
- 理解语言与独立思考能力之间的关系。
- 分析监控、隐私和公民自由的边界。
- 学习区分历史证据、官方叙述与政治口号。
- 讨论恐惧和心理操控如何破坏个人自主性。

## 关键词

totalitarianism · surveillance · propaganda · language · truth
