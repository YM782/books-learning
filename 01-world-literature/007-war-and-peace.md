# 007 · War and Peace（战争与和平）

- **作者：** Leo Tolstoy（列夫·托尔斯泰）
- **首次出版：** 1865–1869 年分期发表；1869 年重要单行本版本
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 《卫报》2026 年 100 部最佳小说榜列第 7；以拿破仑战争为背景，将个人命运与历史进程结合。
- **阅读提示：** 先掌握罗斯托夫、别祖霍夫、博尔孔斯基三大家族及主要人物关系，再跟进战争与家庭两条叙事线。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《战争与和平》以拿破仑战争时期的俄国为背景，将贵族家庭的生活与欧洲军事、政治变局交织在一起。小说通过皮埃尔·别祖霍夫、安德烈·博尔孔斯基、娜塔莎·罗斯托娃等人物的成长，描写爱情、友谊、婚姻、荣誉、信仰和死亡如何改变个人。人物既参加舞会、家庭聚会和日常社交，也被卷入奥斯特里茨、博罗季诺等战争及1812年法国入侵俄国的历史事件。托尔斯泰并不满足于把历史解释成少数统帅的意志或英雄的决定，而是关注士兵、平民、天气、补给、偶然事件和群体行动如何共同影响战争结果。小说还通过大量哲学性讨论，质疑人们是否真的能够控制历史，以及事后叙述如何把复杂事件整理成简单的因果故事。战争带来毁灭，也迫使人物重新理解家庭、爱与生命价值；和平则不是单纯的停战，而是人们在失去之后重建日常生活的过程。作品篇幅宏大，却始终把历史问题落实到具体的人和他们的选择上，适合用来理解个人经验与时代洪流之间的关系。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Leo Tolstoy’s *War and Peace* follows several Russian aristocratic families during the Napoleonic Wars, especially the years surrounding Napoleon’s invasion of Russia in 1812. The novel’s central characters include Pierre Bezukhov, an uncertain and searching young man who unexpectedly inherits great wealth; Prince Andrei Bolkonsky, who seeks distinction and meaning through military service; and Natasha Rostova, whose emotional growth carries her from youthful enthusiasm toward a more mature understanding of love and responsibility. Their lives unfold through family gatherings, courtship, marriages, friendships, military campaigns, and personal crises.

The book moves between private domestic scenes and large historical events, including the battles of Austerlitz and Borodino. Tolstoy gives attention not only to generals and rulers but also to ordinary soldiers, civilians, logistics, weather, fear, confusion, and chance. He challenges the idea that history can be explained simply by the intentions of a few famous individuals. In his view, historical outcomes emerge from countless interacting forces, many of which no single person can control. The novel’s reflective passages question the way historians construct explanations after events have already happened.

Despite its scale, *War and Peace* is deeply concerned with intimate human experiences. Characters struggle with ambition, grief, faith, jealousy, moral responsibility, and the desire to belong. War changes their understanding of life, while peace requires the difficult work of rebuilding relationships and ordinary routines. Tolstoy combines psychological realism with philosophical reflection, making the novel both a family saga and an inquiry into historical causation. Readers can use it to explore the relationship between individual agency and social forces, the limits of power, the cost of war, and the ways people discover meaning through love, service, and everyday life.

## 核心学习价值

1. **历史思维：** 理解历史事件由多种因素共同推动，而非只由少数领袖决定。
2. **战争研究：** 观察战略、后勤、偶然性和平民经历对战争的影响。
3. **人物成长：** 分析皮埃尔、安德烈和娜塔莎在不同阶段的价值变化。
4. **哲学思考：** 讨论自由意志、历史规律、权力与责任。
5. **叙事能力：** 学习如何把个人故事与宏大的时代背景结合。
