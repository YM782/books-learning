# 017 · The Trial

- **作者：** Franz Kafka
- **首次出版/成书：** 1925
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：现代主义文学与制度寓言的重要作品；《卫报》2026 年百大小说榜第 27 名。**

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《审判》讲述银行职员约瑟夫·K在三十岁生日当天突然被捕，却始终不知道自己犯了什么罪。奇怪的是，他并未被立即关押，仍可继续工作和生活，但必须不断面对神秘法院、复杂程序和无法接近的官员。K试图通过律师、熟人和各种关系弄清指控，却发现每一次求助都把他带入更加模糊的制度网络。法院似乎无处不在，却没有清晰的入口、规则或最终解释；被告既无法了解案件，也难以证明自己的无辜。卡夫卡以冷静、近乎日常的叙述方式，制造荒诞而压迫的氛围，使读者感受到个体面对不透明权力时的无力。小说并未给出一个明确的罪名或完整答案，因此可以从官僚主义、法律程序、现代人的焦虑、罪感和权力关系等角度阅读。作品在作者去世后于1925年由马克斯·布罗德整理出版，文本顺序和章节完整性存在编辑问题。阅读时应把它看作未完成小说，而不是一份提供清晰制度说明的现实法律案例。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Franz Kafka’s *The Trial* begins when Josef K., a bank employee, is arrested on the morning of his thirtieth birthday. The officials do not tell him what crime he is accused of, and he is not immediately imprisoned. He is allowed to continue working, but he must somehow respond to a legal process whose rules, authorities, and locations remain obscure. As K. tries to understand the charge, he encounters court offices hidden in ordinary buildings, confusing procedures, intermediaries, lawyers, and people who seem to know more about the system than he does. Each attempt to gain control produces further uncertainty.

The court appears both everywhere and nowhere. It has the power to shape K.’s life, yet it offers no transparent explanation of its decisions. K. believes that reason, status, and personal connections should help him defend himself, but he gradually discovers that the process does not operate according to the clear standards he expects. Kafka’s calm narrative voice makes the situation more disturbing: extraordinary institutional pressure is described in a matter-of-fact way, as if it were an ordinary part of daily life.

The novel can be read as a study of bureaucracy, legal opacity, guilt, anxiety, and the vulnerability of individuals before institutions they cannot understand. It does not provide a simple allegory or a definite explanation of K.’s guilt. Its unresolved structure is central to its effect. Kafka wrote the work in 1914–1915, but it was published posthumously in 1925 by his friend Max Brod, who edited the surviving manuscripts. The text’s arrangement therefore reflects editorial decisions as well as Kafka’s writing. Readers should approach it as an unfinished literary work, not as a factual description of a particular legal system. Its lasting value lies in the questions it raises about due process, transparency, power, and the psychological effects of being judged without knowing the rules.

## 核心学习价值

- 思考程序正义、透明度与有效辩护的重要性。
- 理解官僚体系如何通过不透明程序制造无力感。
- 分析荒诞叙事、象征与未完成结构。
- 讨论罪感、焦虑和现代人的制度处境。
- 区分文学寓言与真实法律制度的证据分析。

## 关键词

bureaucracy · law · opacity · anxiety · power
