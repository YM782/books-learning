# 047 · Snow Country

- **作者：** Yasunari Kawabata
- **首次出版/成书：** 1935–1937 (serialized); 1948 (collected edition)
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：1968年诺贝尔文学奖得主川端康成的代表作之一。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《雪国》描写东京男子岛村多次前往偏远雪乡，与当地艺伎驹子和年轻女子叶子产生复杂关系。岛村经济独立，却缺乏明确生活目标，习惯以旁观者的姿态观察他人的情感和劳动。驹子认真学习艺术、努力维持生活，对岛村既有感情也有清醒认识；叶子的声音和神情则带有神秘、脆弱和距离感。雪景、列车、温泉旅馆、镜面和火光构成小说的主要意象，使自然环境与人物情绪相互映照。川端康成并不通过完整的心理解释说明人物，而是依靠留白、感官细节和含蓄语言，让读者感受欲望、孤独和时间流逝。作品也呈现现代化背景下乡村经济和传统艺伎文化的变化。岛村的观看方式本身值得反思：他能够欣赏美，却未必愿意承担关系中的责任。小说的美学价值与人物关系中的不平等并存，阅读时应同时关注景物描写和人物的现实处境。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Yasunari Kawabata’s *Snow Country* follows Shimamura, a wealthy but emotionally detached man from Tokyo who repeatedly visits a remote hot-spring region covered in snow. During his stays, he becomes involved with Komako, a young woman who works as a geisha, and he also notices Yoko, whose voice and presence seem to carry an unusual intensity. The relationships are not presented through a conventional sequence of dramatic confrontations. Instead, they emerge through brief conversations, gestures, seasonal changes, and the narrator’s attention to visual and sensory details.

Komako works hard to develop her artistic skills and maintain her life in a difficult local economy. She is emotionally invested in Shimamura, yet she also recognizes the limitations of their connection. Shimamura, by contrast, often remains an observer. He can appreciate beauty and emotion without necessarily accepting the responsibilities that accompany intimacy. Yoko’s presence adds another layer of distance and uncertainty to the story.

The snow country itself is essential to the novel’s atmosphere. Trains, hot-spring inns, reflections, firelight, and winter landscapes create images of separation, transience, and fragile beauty. Kawabata uses suggestion and silence rather than extensive psychological explanation, allowing readers to infer the emotional meaning of small details. The novel also reflects changes in rural Japan and the pressures facing traditional forms of entertainment and labor.

*Snow Country* is valuable for studying Japanese literary aesthetics, impermanence, emotional restraint, gender relations, and the relationship between landscape and consciousness. Its beauty should not prevent readers from examining the unequal social and emotional positions of the characters. The work rewards slow reading and attention to what remains unsaid.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解日本文学中的物哀、留白与瞬间美学。
- 分析自然景物如何映照人物心理。
- 讨论旁观、欲望和亲密关系中的责任。
- 认识传统艺伎文化与地方经济背景。
- 训练对含蓄叙事和非直接心理描写的阅读能力。

## 关键词

Japanese aesthetics · impermanence · landscape · desire · silence
