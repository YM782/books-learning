# 040 · The Master and Margarita

- **作者：** Mikhail Bulgakov
- **首次出版/成书：** Written 1928–1940; first serialized 1966–1967
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：二十世纪俄语文学与政治讽刺小说的重要代表。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《大师与玛格丽特》把三个叙事世界交织在一起：神秘的沃兰德和随从来到苏联时期的莫斯科，制造荒诞事件并讽刺文艺机构和官僚社会；一位被称为“大师”的作家因创作关于本丢·彼拉多的小说而遭受审查、打击和精神崩溃；玛格丽特为了寻找失踪的爱人，接受超自然力量的邀请，经历一场奇异的舞会。与此同时，彼拉多与耶稣的故事在古代耶路撒冷展开，讨论恐惧、权力、真理和道德勇气。小说用魔幻、讽刺、宗教寓言和爱情故事挑战审查制度与思想控制，也思考艺术家在压迫环境中如何坚持创作。沃兰德既像恶魔，也像揭露社会虚伪的审判者；莫斯科的荒诞场面则让权力者和投机者暴露出贪婪与恐惧。作品在作者生前未能完整出版，后经删节于1966—1967年连载，文本版本差异值得注意。它的核心不是简单宣扬善恶对立，而是追问勇气、忠诚、宽恕和艺术自由如何在恐惧中存续。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Mikhail Bulgakov’s *The Master and Margarita* interweaves several narrative worlds. In one, the mysterious Woland and his strange companions arrive in Soviet Moscow and create a series of supernatural disturbances that expose the vanity, greed, fear, and opportunism of the city’s literary and bureaucratic institutions. In another, a writer known as the Master has been broken by censorship and persecution after composing a novel about Pontius Pilate. Margarita, who loves the Master, accepts an extraordinary invitation that gives her the chance to search for him and participate in a supernatural gathering.

A third narrative follows Pontius Pilate and the figure of Yeshua in ancient Jerusalem. Their story examines fear, political authority, conscience, and the consequences of failing to act with moral courage. These strands mirror and question one another. The Master’s fictional account becomes part of the larger novel, while the events in Moscow turn the supernatural into a means of revealing the absurdity of everyday power.

Bulgakov uses satire, fantasy, religious allusion, romance, and philosophical reflection to challenge censorship and ideological control. Woland can be read as a devil figure, but he also functions as an observer who exposes the hypocrisy of people who claim to be rational and respectable. Margarita’s loyalty and courage offer a contrasting response to fear, while the Master’s artistic struggle raises questions about the vulnerability of creative work under authoritarian pressure.

The novel was written between 1928 and 1940 but could not be published in full during Bulgakov’s lifetime. A censored version appeared in a Soviet journal in 1966–1967, and later editions restored material that had been removed. Readers should therefore pay attention to the edition and translation they use. *The Master and Margarita* is valuable for studying artistic freedom, censorship, political satire, religious ethics, love, and the relationship between truth and power.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解审查制度、艺术自由与创作者处境。
- 分析政治讽刺、魔幻叙事和多层结构。
- 讨论恐惧、良知与权力之间的关系。
- 比较彼拉多故事与现代莫斯科情节的主题联系。
- 了解苏联文学出版审查与文本版本历史。

censorship · artistic freedom · satire · conscience · love
