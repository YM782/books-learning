# 028 · The Grapes of Wrath

- **作者：** John Steinbeck
- **首次出版/成书：** 1939
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：美国社会现实主义文学经典，具有重要社会史价值。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《愤怒的葡萄》描写美国大萧条时期，俄克拉何马州乔德一家因沙尘暴、土地银行收回农场和经济困境，被迫向加利福尼亚迁徙。旅途中，家庭成员经历饥饿、死亡、道路危险和不断变化的希望；抵达后，他们又发现工作机会被压低工资、临时营地和雇主控制所限制。小说在乔德一家的个人故事与描写移民劳动者整体处境的章节之间交替，展示经济危机并非单个家庭失败，而是土地制度、金融力量、劳动力市场和公共政策共同造成的结构性问题。汤姆·乔德从关心家人逐渐转向理解更广泛的劳动者团结，母亲则以坚韧维系家庭。作品既有对贫困和剥削的愤怒，也呈现互助、尊严和集体行动的可能。斯坦贝克的写作融合现实主义、象征和圣经意象，使迁徙既是现实求生，也是对公平与尊严的追寻。阅读时可以结合美国新政、农业机械化和大萧条史料，辨别文学人物与历史群体之间的关系。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

John Steinbeck’s *The Grapes of Wrath* follows the Joad family as they leave Oklahoma during the Great Depression and travel west to California in search of work. Drought, dust storms, debt, and the repossession of farms by banks and land companies have made it impossible for many tenant farmers to remain on the land. The Joads hope that California will offer employment and a chance to rebuild their lives, but the journey exposes them to hunger, illness, exploitation, and the loss of family members.

The novel alternates between the particular experiences of the Joad family and broader chapters that describe the conditions of migrant workers as a group. These collective passages show that the crisis is not simply the result of individual failure. Mechanized agriculture, concentrated land ownership, financial power, and an oversupply of desperate labor allow employers to lower wages and control workers. When the family reaches California, the promise of opportunity is undermined by hostility toward migrants and by systems that keep people competing for scarce jobs.

Tom Joad’s understanding gradually expands beyond the needs of his immediate family. Ma Joad becomes a central force of endurance, preserving a sense of dignity and mutual obligation even as familiar structures collapse. Steinbeck combines social realism with biblical language and symbolic imagery, giving the migration both historical specificity and moral significance. The title suggests anger produced by injustice, but the novel also emphasizes solidarity and the possibility of collective action.

*The Grapes of Wrath* is valuable for studying the Great Depression, labor relations, migration, poverty, land ownership, and social responsibility. It invites readers to ask how economic systems distribute risk and power, and why people facing similar hardship may be stronger when they cooperate rather than compete.

- 思考经济危机中尊严、家庭和公共责任。
