# 008 · Jane Eyre（简·爱）

- **作者：** Charlotte Brontë（夏洛蒂·勃朗特）
- **首次出版：** 1847 年，初版署名 Currer Bell
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 《卫报》2026 年 100 部最佳小说榜列第 8；以女性主体性、阶级、教育、爱情和道德独立成为英国文学经典。
- **阅读提示：** 关注简在盖茨海德、洛伍德、桑菲尔德和荒原等不同环境中的成长。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《简·爱》讲述一位孤儿女性从受压迫的童年走向独立生活的过程。简在舅母家遭受冷落和羞辱，随后被送进条件艰苦的洛伍德学校，在那里经历疾病、教育和友谊，逐渐形成坚韧的性格。成年后，她成为桑菲尔德庄园的家庭教师，与庄园主人罗切斯特产生感情。然而，当两人的婚姻即将实现时，简发现罗切斯特隐瞒了重要事实，必须在爱情与良知之间作出选择。她离开熟悉的环境，在贫困和孤独中重新寻找生活方向，最终凭借自己的劳动、判断和经济独立获得新的选择空间。小说以第一人称叙述，让读者直接听见简的愤怒、渴望、恐惧和自我辩护。它不仅是一段爱情故事，也挑战了维多利亚时代对女性顺从、阶级地位和婚姻角色的期待。简希望被爱，却不愿为了爱情放弃尊严；她追求平等，也坚持道德原则。作品因此成为关于自我价值、教育、女性独立和人格尊严的重要文学文本。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Charlotte Brontë’s *Jane Eyre* tells the story of an orphan who grows from an unwanted child into a woman determined to live with dignity and independence. Jane is raised by relatives who treat her as an outsider, and her early experiences at Gateshead teach her how easily power can be used to humiliate those with little protection. She is sent to Lowood School, where harsh conditions, illness, friendship, and education shape her character. As an adult, Jane becomes a governess at Thornfield Hall and develops a deep attachment to the estate’s mysterious owner, Edward Rochester.

Their relationship appears to offer Jane affection and a home, but the possibility of marriage is interrupted when she discovers that Rochester has concealed a crucial truth about his life. Jane must decide whether emotional desire can justify compromising her moral principles. She leaves Thornfield and faces poverty, uncertainty, and isolation before finding a new position and a greater sense of independence. Her later choices are shaped not only by love but also by self-respect, financial security, family history, and personal responsibility.

The novel is narrated in Jane’s own voice, giving unusual importance to the inner life of a woman who is socially and economically vulnerable. Brontë examines class hierarchy, gender expectations, religious authority, education, and the limits placed on women’s choices in Victorian society. Jane wants love, but she refuses to become someone else’s possession or to surrender her conscience in exchange for protection. Her insistence on equality gives the novel much of its lasting force. *Jane Eyre* is therefore both a coming-of-age story and an argument for personal dignity. It remains valuable for discussing women’s autonomy, moral independence, social class, and the relationship between love and self-respect.

## 核心学习价值

1. **女性主体性：** 理解女性如何争取教育、经济独立和人生选择权。
2. **阶级与权力：** 分析家庭、学校和雇佣关系中的权力差异。
3. **道德自主：** 思考爱情、良知、自尊和责任之间的关系。
4. **第一人称叙事：** 学习叙述者声音如何影响读者的同情与判断。
5. **历史文化：** 认识维多利亚时代英国的性别观念与社会结构。
