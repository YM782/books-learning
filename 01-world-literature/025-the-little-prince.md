# 025 · The Little Prince

- **作者：** Antoine de Saint-Exupéry
- **首次出版/成书：** 1943 (New York publication; French edition 1946)
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：跨语言传播最广的现代寓言之一，具有持续的教育与文学影响。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《小王子》从一位飞行员迫降沙漠的经历开始。他在那里遇见来自另一颗小行星的小王子，并听他讲述离开故乡、访问不同星球和最终来到地球的旅程。小王子曾与一朵玫瑰建立亲密关系，却因误解和骄傲离开；在旅途中，他遇见国王、爱虚荣的人、酒鬼、商人、点灯人和地理学家，这些人物分别表现权力欲、虚荣、逃避、占有和脱离现实的知识。来到地球后，他通过狐狸理解“驯养”意味着建立关系，也明白自己对玫瑰的责任来自共同度过的时间和付出的心力。作品用童话般简洁的语言讨论爱、友谊、孤独、责任和成人世界的价值观。它并不是只写给儿童的寓言：飞行员与小王子的对话，也让成年人重新审视效率、数字、身份和物质成就是否掩盖了真正重要的东西。阅读时可以把玫瑰、狐狸、星球和沙漠看作关系与成长的象征，同时注意不同译本在语言节奏和隐喻上的差异。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Antoine de Saint-Exupéry’s *The Little Prince* begins with a pilot who crashes in the Sahara Desert and meets a small visitor from another planet. The child, known as the Little Prince, tells the pilot about his home asteroid, his relationship with a rose, and the planets he visited before arriving on Earth. On those planets he encountered adults whose lives were dominated by authority, vanity, drinking, possession, or abstract knowledge. Each encounter exposes a different way in which grown-ups can lose sight of what gives life meaning.

The Little Prince left his home after misunderstanding the rose he loved. During his journey, he gradually learns that affection involves patience, responsibility, and attention. On Earth, a fox teaches him that forming a bond makes one person unique to another, and that the time and care invested in a relationship create obligations. The Little Prince begins to understand that his rose matters not because she is the only flower in the universe, but because of the particular relationship they have built.

The book uses the form of a simple fable, but its themes reach beyond childhood. It questions the adult tendency to measure value through numbers, status, ownership, and practical usefulness. The pilot’s conversations with the child also suggest that imagination and emotional understanding are essential parts of being human. The desert setting creates a contrast between physical emptiness and the possibility of discovering meaning through encounter and friendship.

*The Little Prince* is valuable for discussing love, friendship, responsibility, loneliness, childhood, and the limits of purely instrumental thinking. It was first published in New York in 1943, with the French edition appearing in 1946. Its illustrations by the author are integral to many editions, and translation choices can affect the tone of its concise language.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解亲密关系中的责任、耐心和相互理解。
- 分析寓言象征与简洁语言的表达力量。
- 反思成人世界对数字、权力和物质成就的迷恋。
- 讨论孤独、友谊、成长与想象力。
- 适合进行英语短篇阅读、复述和主题写作。
