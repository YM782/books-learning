# 021 · Dream of the Red Chamber

- **作者：** Cao Xueqin
- **首次出版/成书：** 18th century; printed 1791
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：中国古典小说代表作，兼具文学、社会史和文化史价值。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《红楼梦》以贾、史、王、薛四大家族的兴衰为背景，重点描写贾府内部的日常生活、婚姻安排、亲属关系、经济变化和年轻人的情感世界。贾宝玉厌恶功名仕途，珍视与林黛玉、薛宝钗等女性之间的情感联系；黛玉敏感聪慧，宝钗端庄周全，二人的性格与命运折射出家族制度对女性的不同要求。小说通过宴会、诗社、节庆、疾病、葬礼和家庭冲突，展示繁华生活背后的等级秩序与脆弱性。大观园一度像一个相对自由的青春空间，却最终无法抵挡家族权力、经济衰败和礼法安排的侵入。作品不只是爱情悲剧，也是一幅关于清代贵族家庭、女性处境、消费文化和社会变迁的百科全书式图景。曹雪芹以细腻的人物语言、诗词、饮食、服饰和器物描写，使每个角色都有复杂的性格，而不是简单的善恶标签。通行本的后四十回与作者原稿关系长期存在争论，阅读时应留意版本说明。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

*Dream of the Red Chamber*, traditionally attributed to Cao Xueqin, portrays the rise and decline of the Jia family and its connections with three other prominent households. Much of the novel takes place within the Jia family compound, where ceremonies, meals, poetry gatherings, marriages, illness, financial decisions, and everyday conversations reveal the structure of an elite household in Qing-dynasty China. At the center is Jia Baoyu, a young man who resists the conventional pursuit of official success and feels a deep emotional connection to the women around him. His relationships with Lin Daiyu and Xue Baochai form one of the novel’s central tensions. Daiyu is sensitive, intellectually sharp, and emotionally vulnerable; Baochai is socially capable and conforms more closely to family expectations. Their different positions reveal the limited choices available to women within a patriarchal family system.

The garden known as Grand View Garden becomes a temporary world of youth, friendship, poetry, and relative freedom. Yet it cannot remain separate from inheritance rules, status competition, financial decline, and family authority. As the Jia household loses its wealth and influence, the apparent stability of its rituals is exposed as fragile. The novel’s attention to clothing, food, architecture, poetry, music, and domestic objects makes it an unusually detailed portrait of material and emotional life.

The work is not simply a tragic romance. It examines gender, kinship, class, consumption, education, desire, and the costs of maintaining social appearances. Cao’s characterization is subtle: people are rarely reduced to straightforward moral types, and even minor figures have distinctive voices and motives. The received 120-chapter text has a complex publication history, and the relationship between its final forty chapters and Cao’s original manuscript remains debated. Readers should therefore pay attention to the edition and its editorial notes.

## 核心学习价值

- 认识清代贵族家庭制度、亲属关系与日常物质文化。
- 分析女性人物在家族秩序中的不同处境。
- 理解人物群像、诗词和细节描写的艺术作用。
- 讨论家族衰败、消费和社会地位之间的关系。
- 培养阅读长篇古典小说及版本注释的能力。
