# 004 · To the Lighthouse（到灯塔去）

- **作者：** Virginia Woolf（弗吉尼亚·伍尔夫）
- **首次出版：** 1927 年
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 《卫报》2026 年 100 部最佳小说榜列第 4；现代主义代表作，以时间、意识与家庭关系革新小说表达。
- **阅读提示：** 留意小说三部分的时间跨度，以及同一事件在不同人物意识中呈现出的差异。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《到灯塔去》以拉姆齐一家在苏格兰海边度假的经历为中心，描写家庭成员与朋友之间微妙而复杂的情感。小说分为三个部分：第一部分“窗”发生在一次夏日假期，孩子詹姆斯盼望父亲兑现去灯塔的承诺，母亲拉姆齐夫人努力维系家庭气氛，父亲拉姆齐先生则在理性、权威和情感需求之间显露矛盾；第二部分“岁月流逝”用极为凝练的方式写出战争、死亡和时间对房屋与家庭的改变；第三部分“灯塔”中，幸存者重返海边，终于完成多年前未能实现的航行。伍尔夫并不把重点放在外部事件，而是进入人物的意识，展示同一时刻如何因年龄、性别、记忆和情绪而具有不同意义。灯塔既是一个真实地点，也是人物渴望、距离、理解与未竟愿望的象征。小说还思考艺术如何保存转瞬即逝的经验：画家莉莉·布里斯科试图完成一幅画，正如人物试图理解彼此和时间本身。作品以细腻的语言呈现家庭权力、女性创造力、失去与记忆，提醒读者人生的意义常常存在于无法被简单概括的日常瞬间。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Virginia Woolf’s *To the Lighthouse* centers on the Ramsay family and their friends during visits to a summer house on the Isle of Skye in Scotland. The novel is divided into three sections: “The Window,” “Time Passes,” and “The Lighthouse.” In the first section, the youngest son, James, hopes that his father will allow the family to visit the lighthouse across the water. Mrs. Ramsay tries to preserve harmony among the guests, while Mr. Ramsay’s intellectual authority and emotional insecurity shape the atmosphere of the household. The planned trip becomes a focus for unspoken tensions, affection, disappointment, and competing expectations.

“Time Passes” compresses years of history into a remarkably brief sequence. The house is left largely empty as war and personal loss alter the lives of those connected to it. Woolf shifts attention away from conventional plot and toward the passage of time itself: rooms decay, seasons change, and human events are registered almost indirectly. In the final section, some of the surviving characters return. James finally travels to the lighthouse, while the painter Lily Briscoe works to complete a painting she began years earlier. Her artistic effort becomes a way of bringing separate perceptions into a temporary form.

The lighthouse functions both as a physical destination and as a symbol whose meaning changes from one character to another. Woolf’s stream-of-consciousness technique allows readers to move through thoughts, memories, and impressions without relying on a single stable viewpoint. The novel explores family relationships, gender roles, artistic creation, grief, and the difficulty of truly knowing another person. It also asks how art can preserve moments that time inevitably destroys. Rather than offering a straightforward sequence of dramatic events, *To the Lighthouse* invites readers to attend to the inner life of ordinary experience and to recognize how meaning emerges through memory, perception, and change.

## 核心学习价值

1. **时间意识：** 理解个人生命、历史事件与自然时间的不同尺度。
2. **意识流技巧：** 学习如何从人物内部呈现记忆、情绪和感知。
3. **性别与家庭：** 分析家庭角色、权威结构与女性创造力。
4. **艺术与记忆：** 思考艺术如何保存短暂经验、面对失去。
5. **象征阅读：** 训练对灯塔、房屋、海洋和绘画等意象的分析能力。
