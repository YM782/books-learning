# 049 · My Brilliant Friend

- **作者：** Elena Ferrante
- **首次出版/成书：** 2011
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：国际影响广泛的当代文学作品，四部曲首卷。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《我的天才女友》是那不勒斯四部曲的第一部，由年长的埃莱娜回忆自己与莉拉从童年到青春期的友谊。两人在战后那不勒斯贫困社区长大，聪明、好胜，又受到家庭暴力、阶级限制和性别规范的约束。莉拉天赋突出，却因家庭经济条件无法继续接受正规教育；埃莱娜则通过读书进入更广阔的世界，同时不断与莉拉比较，既羡慕她，也依赖她。两人的关系包含合作、竞争、嫉妒、忠诚和误解，随着周围人物的婚姻、商业活动和暴力冲突而变化。小说不仅描写两个女孩的成长，也呈现意大利战后社会的阶级流动、教育机会、地方政治和女性生活。埃莱娜的回忆并非中立记录，她的叙述带有多年后的判断和不安，因此读者需要注意她如何塑造莉拉，也要思考友谊中的权力与自我认同。作品适合研究女性成长、教育不平等、阶级结构和复杂的女性友谊。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Elena Ferrante’s *My Brilliant Friend* is the first novel in the Neapolitan series. It is narrated by Elena Greco, who looks back on her childhood and adolescence in a poor neighborhood of Naples after the Second World War. The story centers on her intense and complicated friendship with Raffaella Cerullo, known as Lila. Both girls are intelligent and ambitious, but their opportunities are shaped by family poverty, gender expectations, violence, and the social hierarchy of the neighborhood.

Lila displays extraordinary ability, yet her family does not allow her to continue formal education beyond a certain point. Elena, whose parents support further schooling, moves into a different world through books and academic achievement. Their paths diverge, but neither girl can stop measuring herself against the other. Their friendship includes admiration, competition, loyalty, jealousy, dependence, and misunderstanding. The surrounding community is also important: family businesses, marriages, local rivalries, political change, and the threat of violence shape the choices available to young women.

Ferrante’s narrative is a retrospective account rather than a neutral record. Elena’s memory is influenced by years of comparison and unresolved emotion, so the reader must consider how she constructs Lila’s image and how the act of telling a story changes the past. The novel’s social realism makes education and class mobility central to personal development. It also refuses to present female friendship as automatically harmonious; affection can coexist with rivalry and unequal power.

*My Brilliant Friend* is valuable for studying women’s coming of age, education, class, memory, and the social history of postwar Italy. It offers a detailed portrait of how a friendship can shape identity while remaining inseparable from the material conditions of the community in which it develops.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解战后意大利的阶级结构与教育机会差异。
- 分析女性友谊中的竞争、忠诚、嫉妒和依赖。
- 认识家庭暴力与性别规范对成长的影响。
- 研究回忆叙事和叙述者的主观性。
- 讨论教育如何改变个人的社会流动机会。
