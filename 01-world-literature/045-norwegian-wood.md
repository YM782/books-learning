# 045 · Norwegian Wood

- **作者：** Haruki Murakami
- **首次出版/成书：** 1987
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：日本当代文学的重要代表作，具有广泛国际影响。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《挪威的森林》由成年后的渡边彻回忆二十世纪六十年代末在东京的大学生活。好友木月去世后，渡边与木月的恋人直子保持联系，两人之间既有亲近，也受到悲伤和无法言说的心理创伤影响。后来渡边认识了性格开朗、直接而充满生命力的绿子，他在两段关系之间感到摇摆，试图理解自己对爱、陪伴和责任的真实需求。小说描写大学生活、学生运动、城市空间、音乐和青年文化，但重点始终是人物如何面对失去、孤独和成长。村上春树以简洁而带有疏离感的语言，呈现记忆如何把过去变成难以摆脱的情绪空间。作品中的死亡和心理困境需要被认真对待，不应被浪漫化为爱情的装饰；不同人物对痛苦的应对方式也不能简单互相替代。小说适合讨论青春期后的身份形成、亲密关系、悲伤和精神健康，同时也值得结合日本六十年代末的社会背景理解。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Haruki Murakami’s *Norwegian Wood* is narrated by Toru Watanabe, who remembers his university years in Tokyo during the late 1960s. His memories are shaped by the death of his close friend Kizuki and by his complicated relationship with Naoko, Kizuki’s former girlfriend. Toru and Naoko share a sense of loss, but their connection is also marked by emotional distance and the difficulty of speaking openly about grief. Later, Toru becomes close to Midori, a lively and direct young woman whose energy offers a different experience of intimacy and everyday life.

The novel follows Toru as he tries to understand what love, loyalty, and responsibility mean when people are affected by pain that cannot be easily resolved. The story includes university life, student activism, music, city streets, and changing youth culture, but its central concern is the emotional development of its characters. Murakami’s restrained prose often creates a feeling of distance between the narrator and the events he remembers. Memory becomes both a way to preserve relationships and a source of continuing sorrow.

The novel deals with death, grief, and psychological distress. These experiences should not be romanticized as merely beautiful or tragic features of love. Characters respond to suffering in different ways, and their needs cannot be reduced to a single explanation. The setting in late-1960s Japan also provides context for generational change and the uncertainty felt by young people during a period of social and political transition.

*Norwegian Wood* is valuable for studying memory, intimacy, grief, coming of age, and the limits of emotional understanding. It encourages readers to consider how people support one another without assuming that affection alone can solve serious psychological difficulties.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解悲伤、孤独和青年成长中的情感变化。
- 讨论亲密关系中的陪伴、界限和责任。
- 认识日本六十年代末的青年文化与社会背景。
- 分析回忆叙事和音乐意象的作用。
- 避免把心理困境浪漫化，培养对人物处境的审慎理解。
