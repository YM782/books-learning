# 053 · A Passage to India

- **作者：** E. M. Forster
- **首次出版/成书：** 1924
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：英国现代文学中讨论殖民主义和跨文化关系的重要作品。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《印度之行》以英国殖民统治下的印度为背景，描写印度医生阿齐兹与英国学校校长菲尔丁之间的友谊，以及一次马拉巴山洞之行引发的司法危机。英国女子阿德拉指控阿齐兹在洞穴中对她实施侵犯，案件迅速演变为殖民社会的种族对立。英国社群倾向于相信本国人，印度居民则将审判视为殖民权力不公的象征。菲尔丁试图坚持证据和个人判断，却发现友谊难以摆脱政治结构。洞穴中的回声使人物对事件的记忆和理解变得模糊，也成为文化误解、存在焦虑和意义不确定的象征。福斯特既批判殖民制度，也描写印度社会内部的宗教、阶级和文化差异；小说并不提供轻易的跨文化和解。阅读时应关注英国人与印度人之间的权力不对等，也要认识到作品属于特定时代的英国自由主义视角，不能代表所有印度人的经验。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

E. M. Forster’s *A Passage to India* is set during British colonial rule in India and explores the possibility of friendship across an unequal political system. The novel centers on Dr. Aziz, an Indian Muslim physician, and Cyril Fielding, a British school principal who is more willing than many of his compatriots to treat Indians as equals. Their relationship is tested during an excursion to the Marabar Caves, where an incident involving Adela Quested, a young English visitor, leads to Aziz being accused of assault.

The accusation quickly becomes a public crisis. Many members of the British community assume that Aziz is guilty, while Indian residents see the case as evidence of the racial prejudice and unequal power built into colonial rule. Fielding tries to defend the principle of evidence and individual judgment, but his position places him in conflict with the British community. The trial exposes how legal institutions can be shaped by political loyalty and racial assumptions. The mysterious echo in the caves also unsettles the characters’ sense of meaning and certainty, becoming a symbol of experiences that resist easy explanation.

Forster does not present cross-cultural friendship as impossible, but he shows how fragile it becomes when one group governs another. The novel also includes differences within Indian society, including religion, class, and cultural traditions. Its perspective reflects the period in which it was written and should not be treated as a complete representation of Indian experience. Readers can critically examine both its anti-colonial insights and its limitations as a British liberal account of India.

*A Passage to India* is valuable for studying colonialism, race, law, friendship, cultural misunderstanding, and the relationship between personal ethics and political institutions. It asks whether genuine equality can exist when the larger social structure remains unequal.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解殖民统治中的种族权力和司法不平等。
- 分析个人友谊如何受到制度结构限制。
- 讨论证据、偏见与法律程序的关系。
- 认识殖民文学的视角及其历史局限。
- 思考跨文化沟通为何需要平等的制度条件。
