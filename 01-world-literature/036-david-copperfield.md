# 036 · David Copperfield

- **作者：** Charles Dickens
- **首次出版/成书：** 1849–1850 (serial); 1850 (book)
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：狄更斯最具代表性的成长小说之一，具有重要社会史与文学价值。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《大卫·科波菲尔》以主人公成年后的回忆讲述自己的成长历程。大卫幼年丧父，母亲再婚后遭继父默德斯通冷酷对待，被送往寄宿学校，随后又被迫到伦敦工厂劳动。贫困和虐待使他很早体验社会的不平等，但他也遇见米考伯一家、贝西姨婆、艾格妮丝等人物，在不同关系中学习信任、独立和责任。大卫后来成为作家，经历爱情、婚姻、事业和朋友的变化，逐渐理解童年经验如何影响成年选择。小说既是成长故事，也描绘维多利亚时代英国的教育、债务、童工、职业和家庭制度。狄更斯把幽默人物、社会讽刺和感伤情节结合起来，让个体命运与制度问题相互照亮。作品具有明显的自传色彩，但不能简单等同于作者真实经历；它是经过艺术加工的文学叙事。阅读时可关注叙述者成年后的理解与童年时的感受之间的差别，以及大卫如何从受害者逐渐成为能够承担责任的人。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Charles Dickens’s *David Copperfield* is narrated by its title character as he looks back on the experiences that shaped his life. David’s childhood begins with affection from his mother and nurse, but after his mother remarries, his stepfather, Mr. Murdstone, imposes a harsh regime of discipline. David is sent away to school and later forced to work in a warehouse in London. These experiences expose him to poverty, child labor, and the insecurity of people who lack family protection or financial resources.

As David grows older, he encounters a wide range of characters, including the optimistic but financially troubled Micawber family, his formidable aunt Betsey Trotwood, the loyal Agnes Wickfield, and friends whose choices reveal different forms of ambition and weakness. Education, work, friendship, romance, marriage, and writing gradually help David build an independent life. His development is not smooth: he makes mistakes, misunderstands people, and must learn to distinguish affection from idealization.

The novel combines a coming-of-age narrative with a broad portrait of Victorian society. Dickens examines schooling, debt, professional life, domestic authority, social mobility, and the vulnerability of children. Humor and memorable comic characters sit beside scenes of cruelty and emotional loss, allowing the novel to criticize institutions without losing its concern for individual feeling. David’s first-person account also creates a productive distance between the child who experienced events and the adult who interprets them. Readers can observe how memory changes as understanding grows.

The book contains autobiographical elements, especially in its treatment of childhood labor and the development of a writing career, but it should not be treated as a direct factual autobiography. *David Copperfield* is valuable for studying narrative voice, social realism, childhood, resilience, and the relationship between personal growth and social conditions.

## 核心学习价值

- 认识维多利亚时代童工、教育和债务问题。
- 分析童年创伤如何影响成年关系与选择。
- 学习第一人称回忆叙事和时间视角。
- 理解社会现实主义与幽默讽刺的结合。
- 讨论韧性、独立、友谊和责任。

childhood · social realism · memory · resilience · education
