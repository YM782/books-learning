# 058 · The Left Hand of Darkness

- **作者：** Ursula K. Le Guin
- **首次出版/成书：** 1969
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：1969年星云奖、1970年雨果奖获奖作品，科幻文学经典。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《黑暗的左手》讲述人类联盟“周界”派遣使者根利·艾前往寒冷星球格森，希望说服当地国家加入跨星际联盟。格森人多数时间没有固定性别，只有在特定生理周期进入性别状态，这使根利以自身社会的性别观念理解他们时不断产生误判。小说通过外交谈判、政治阴谋、文化冲突和根利与埃斯特拉文共同穿越冰原的旅程，讨论性别、信任、民族身份和人与自然的关系。埃斯特拉文起初被根利怀疑，后来在极端环境中成为最重要的伙伴。冰原旅行让两人不得不依赖彼此，也迫使根利修正对格森社会的理解。勒古恩并非简单提出一个性别制度的乌托邦，而是用科幻设定检验读者习以为常的分类方式，尤其是性别角色如何影响权力、沟通和外交。作品也展示文化理解需要时间、谦逊和共同经历。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Ursula K. Le Guin’s *The Left Hand of Darkness* follows Genly Ai, an envoy sent by the Ekumen, a coalition of human worlds, to the planet Gethen. His mission is to persuade the nations of Gethen to join a wider interplanetary community. The planet’s inhabitants are human, but most of the time they do not have a fixed sexual identity. During a recurring biological period, they may develop male or female characteristics. Genly’s assumptions about gender shape how he interprets the people around him, and his misunderstandings repeatedly complicate diplomacy.

The story moves between political negotiation, cultural observation, myth, and a dangerous journey across an icy landscape. Estraven, a Gethenian statesman whom Genly initially distrusts, becomes his most important companion. Their trek across the ice forces them to depend on one another and to reconsider the categories through which they have judged each other. The physical environment is not simply a backdrop: cold, distance, and scarcity make cooperation necessary and reveal the limits of individual control.

Le Guin uses science fiction as a form of anthropological thought experiment. By imagining a society without permanent gender roles, she invites readers to examine how gender expectations influence identity, power, emotional expression, and political institutions. The novel does not present Gethen as a perfect utopia. Its societies still contain nationalism, hierarchy, rivalry, and fear. The speculative premise makes familiar assumptions visible without offering a single simple solution.

*The Left Hand of Darkness* is valuable for studying gender, cultural relativism, diplomacy, trust, ecology, and the possibilities of speculative fiction. It encourages readers to approach unfamiliar societies with humility and to recognize that their own categories are not universal.

## 核心学习价值

- 反思性别角色如何影响身份和权力。
- 理解文化相对主义与跨文化沟通。
- 分析外交谈判、信任和政治猜疑。
- 观察自然环境如何塑造社会组织与合作。
- 学习科幻如何作为社会思想实验。
