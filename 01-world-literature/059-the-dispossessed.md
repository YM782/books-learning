# 059 · The Dispossessed

- **作者：** Ursula K. Le Guin
- **首次出版/成书：** 1974
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：1975年雨果奖获奖小说，科幻与政治哲学结合的代表作。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《一无所有的人》围绕物理学家谢维克展开。他生活在安纳瑞斯星球，这里由一场反抗运动建立，强调共同劳动、资源共享和反等级制度；但长期形成的组织惯例也可能变成新的权力结构。谢维克提出一套突破现有理论限制的时间物理学，因学术和政治环境受阻，决定前往母星乌拉斯交流。乌拉斯拥有富裕的资本主义社会、国家竞争和明显阶级差异，表面繁荣却受到所有权、财富和政治利益制约。两种社会都不是完美乌托邦：安纳瑞斯可能压制异见，乌拉斯则容易把人和知识商品化。谢维克在两个世界之间移动，逐渐认识到自由不仅是没有传统统治者，也需要持续抵抗僵化制度和思想垄断。小说以交错时间线讲述他的成长与理论探索，把科学创新和社会制度联系起来。勒古恩通过虚构社会比较，讨论财产、合作、个人自由、革命理想和制度腐化，促使读者思考乌托邦应当如何面对自身的失败。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Ursula K. Le Guin’s *The Dispossessed* follows Shevek, a physicist from the moon Anarres, whose society was founded by an anarchist movement that rejected private ownership and rigid hierarchy. Anarres values cooperation, shared labor, and voluntary organization, but its institutions have developed informal pressures that can discourage dissent and restrict intellectual freedom. Shevek is working on a theory of time that could transform physics, yet he finds that professional and political habits on his home world limit the exchange of ideas. He decides to travel to the neighboring planet Urras, from which the original settlers of Anarres once departed.

Urras is wealthy and technologically advanced, but its societies are shaped by private property, class inequality, nationalism, and competition among states. Shevek encounters luxury and intellectual opportunity, but also discovers that knowledge can be controlled by institutions and used to serve political interests. The two worlds are not presented as a simple contrast between a good society and a bad one. Anarres can become rigid and conformist; Urras can be prosperous while preserving deep inequality. Both reveal the difficulty of creating genuine freedom.

The novel moves between Shevek’s life on Anarres and his experiences on Urras, allowing readers to compare the development of an ideal society with the pressures that emerge over time. Scientific discovery becomes connected to social organization: the exchange of knowledge requires openness, trust, and the willingness to challenge established authority.

*The Dispossessed* is valuable for studying anarchism, utopian thought, property, cooperation, freedom, and the politics of science. It asks whether a revolution can remain faithful to its ideals and how individuals can resist the institutions that gradually form around even the most egalitarian systems.

- 比较无政府主义与资本主义社会结构。
- 分析理想制度如何形成新的非正式权力。
- 讨论财产、合作、自由和知识开放。
- 理解科学创新与政治制度之间的联系。
- 思考革命理想如何面对制度僵化和内部批评。
