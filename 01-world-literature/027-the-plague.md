# 027 · The Plague

- **作者：** Albert Camus
- **首次出版/成书：** 1947
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：战后法国文学经典，以疾病叙事讨论人类团结和伦理责任。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《鼠疫》以阿尔及利亚城市奥兰突然爆发鼠疫为背景，描写居民从最初否认危险到封城、隔离、死亡和逐渐适应的过程。医生里厄坚持救治病人，却不把自己塑造成英雄；塔鲁组织志愿卫生队，格朗努力维持普通生活，记者朗贝尔则一度只想离开城市与爱人团聚。不同人物面对灾难的方式各异，小说因此不只写疾病，也讨论恐惧、孤独、责任、团结和道德选择。鼠疫既是具体的传染病，也是战争、极权主义和人类共同脆弱性的象征，但象征意义不应遮蔽小说对医疗劳动和公共卫生现实的关注。加缪强调，面对无法彻底战胜的苦难，人仍可以通过诚实、合作和持续行动减少伤害。里厄不相信宏大的救世叙事，却认为医生应尽力与疾病作斗争。作品由此提出一种不依赖确定胜利的伦理：即使灾难可能再次来临，人也不应放弃对他人的责任。阅读时可结合疫情治理、风险沟通、公共卫生和社会互助等现实议题，但要区分文学象征与具体流行病学知识。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Albert Camus’s *The Plague* is set in the Algerian city of Oran, where an outbreak of plague begins with the sudden appearance of dead rats. At first, residents and officials struggle to accept the danger. As the disease spreads, the city is closed, families are separated, and daily life becomes organized around quarantine, medical work, shortages, and death. The novel follows several characters whose responses to the crisis reveal different ideas about responsibility and human solidarity.

Dr. Bernard Rieux treats patients with determination but refuses to present himself as a heroic savior. Jean Tarrou helps organize volunteer sanitation teams and reflects on the possibility of living without becoming an agent of suffering. Joseph Grand tries to preserve ordinary routines, while the journalist Raymond Rambert initially seeks a way to escape the sealed city and reunite with the woman he loves. Their choices change as the scale of the emergency becomes clear. The epidemic exposes fear, selfishness, denial, courage, and the practical work required to protect others.

The plague can be read as a literal disease and as a symbol of war, authoritarianism, and the recurring presence of destructive forces in human society. Yet the symbolic dimension does not replace the novel’s attention to medical labor, public administration, and collective behavior. Camus suggests that people cannot always defeat suffering permanently, but they can choose how to respond to it. Rieux’s ethic is grounded in honest work and the refusal to abandon those who are ill. The book’s moral force comes from action rather than certainty: solidarity matters even when success is incomplete and the danger may return.

*The Plague* is valuable for thinking about public health, risk communication, social cooperation, isolation, and ethical responsibility during emergencies. Readers can connect its themes to modern crises while remembering that a literary allegory is not a scientific account of infectious disease.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解公共卫生危机中的风险沟通与集体行动。
- 分析否认、恐惧、隔离和社会适应。
- 讨论医护劳动、志愿服务与伦理责任。
- 认识鼠疫作为战争和极权主义象征的文学功能。
- 比较个人自由、公共安全与团结互助。
