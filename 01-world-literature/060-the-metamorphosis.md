# 060 · The Metamorphosis

- **作者：** Franz Kafka
- **首次出版/成书：** 1915
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：现代主义文学的标志性作品，对荒诞文学和存在主义阅读影响深远。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《变形记》开篇时，旅行推销员格里高尔·萨姆沙醒来，发现自己变成了一个巨大的虫形生物。他首先担心的不是身体变化本身，而是无法按时上班、失去收入并影响家庭债务。家人最初惊恐，后来逐渐把他视为负担；妹妹格蕾特曾照料他，却也随着生活压力增加而改变态度。格里高尔无法再用正常语言沟通，身体和房间逐渐成为隔离他的空间。小说以荒诞设定呈现劳动异化、家庭义务、经济依赖和社会排斥：一个人是否只有在能够工作、赚钱和满足他人期待时才被视为有价值？卡夫卡并未解释变形原因，也没有提供明确寓意，因此读者可以从多种角度理解它，但不应把单一解释当作作者唯一意图。作品的冷静语调与极端事件形成反差，使日常生活中的压迫显得更加真实。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Franz Kafka’s *The Metamorphosis* begins when Gregor Samsa, a traveling salesman, wakes up to discover that he has transformed into a large insect-like creature. His first concern is not the strange change itself but the fact that he will miss work, disappoint his employer, and threaten the financial stability of his family. Before the transformation, Gregor has been supporting his parents and sister while carrying the burden of family debt. His new body makes it impossible for him to continue earning money or communicating in ordinary ways.

At first, his family is frightened and attempts to manage the situation. His sister Grete brings him food and tries to care for him, but the family’s response changes as the economic and emotional pressure grows. Gregor becomes increasingly confined to his room, and the space that once belonged to an ordinary family member turns into a place of isolation. The family gradually treats him as a burden rather than as a person whose needs and feelings still matter.

Kafka does not explain the cause of the transformation or provide a single authoritative interpretation. The story can be read as an exploration of alienation, labor, disability, family obligation, shame, and the conditions under which society recognizes a person’s value. Gregor’s predicament raises a difficult question: is he loved as an individual, or valued mainly because he can work and provide income? The contrast between the extraordinary event and the calm narrative style makes the situation both absurd and disturbingly familiar.

*The Metamorphosis* is valuable for studying modernist and absurdist literature, symbolism, family economics, social exclusion, and the relationship between identity and work. Readers should allow multiple interpretations rather than treating the insect transformation as a simple code for one psychological or social condition.

## 核心学习价值

- 讨论劳动价值、经济依赖和人的尊严。
- 分析家庭责任与社会排斥。
- 理解荒诞设定与冷静叙述的反差。
- 研究象征和多义文本的阅读方法。
- 思考一个人是否会因失去生产能力而被社会否定。

## 关键词

alienation · labor · family · identity · absurdism
