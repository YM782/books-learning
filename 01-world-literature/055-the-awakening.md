# 055 · The Awakening

- **作者：** Kate Chopin
- **首次出版/成书：** 1899
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：美国女性主义文学的重要早期作品。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《觉醒》讲述新奥尔良已婚女性埃德娜·庞特利耶在夏季海滨度假后，逐渐意识到自己对婚姻、母职和社会角色的不满。她开始学习游泳、绘画，并发展出独立的情感和生活愿望。与罗伯特的感情、与艾德莱·拉特尼奥尔夫人的友谊，以及搬离丈夫住宅后的生活，使她不断重新审视自我。但十九世纪末的社会规范要求女性优先服从家庭和丈夫，经济资源、名誉和子女责任也限制她的选择。肖邦并未把埃德娜塑造成毫无矛盾的自由象征：她渴望自主，却也难以处理他人的感受和现实后果。小说结尾具有开放性和悲剧性，读者对她的行动可以有不同解释。作品出版时因挑战传统女性角色而引发争议，后来成为美国女性主义文学的重要文本。阅读时应结合当时的婚姻法、财产制度和女性劳动机会，理解“自由”不仅是个人意愿，也受到社会条件制约。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Kate Chopin’s *The Awakening* follows Edna Pontellier, a married woman living in New Orleans at the end of the nineteenth century. During a summer stay on the Gulf Coast, Edna begins to question the expectations that define her life as a wife and mother. She develops new interests in swimming and painting, forms friendships outside her marriage, and becomes increasingly aware of desires that she has previously suppressed. Her emotional connection with Robert Lebrun and her friendship with the independent musician Mademoiselle Reisz encourage her to imagine a life organized around her own choices.

Edna’s awakening is not a simple story of liberation. She wants independence, but her decisions are shaped by marriage, motherhood, social reputation, economic dependence, and the limited opportunities available to women in her society. Moving out of her husband’s home and pursuing artistic interests give her a greater sense of autonomy, yet they do not remove the pressures surrounding her. The novel also shows that freedom can create conflicts with the needs and expectations of other people.

Chopin refuses to provide an easy moral judgment of Edna. The ending is open to interpretation and has often been debated, particularly because it raises difficult questions about autonomy, despair, and the costs of social exclusion. When the novel was published, its treatment of female desire and resistance to conventional domestic roles provoked controversy. Later readers recognized it as an important work in American feminist literary history.

*The Awakening* is valuable for studying gender roles, marriage, motherhood, artistic identity, and the social conditions that limit individual freedom. Readers should place Edna’s choices within the legal, economic, and cultural context of the period rather than treating independence as a purely personal decision.

## 核心学习价值

- 认识十九世纪末女性婚姻、财产和社会角色限制。
- 分析自主、母职、婚姻与经济依赖之间的冲突。
- 讨论女性艺术创作与自我认同。
- 理解开放式结尾及其多种解释。
- 将个人自由放在具体制度条件中考察。
