# 026 · The Stranger

- **作者：** Albert Camus
- **首次出版/成书：** 1942
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：二十世纪法国文学与荒诞哲学的重要作品。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《局外人》讲述生活在阿尔及尔的默尔索。他在母亲去世后表现得冷淡，随后继续工作、交往和恋爱；在海滩的一次冲突中，他开枪杀死一名阿拉伯男子。审判过程中，法庭不仅讨论犯罪事实，也不断审视他在母亲葬礼上的表现、情感表达和生活态度。默尔索似乎因为没有按照社会期待表现悲伤而受到道德审判，案件由此暴露法律事实、社会规范与公众情绪之间的复杂关系。加缪通过第一人称叙述和克制的语言，呈现一个人与世界之间的疏离感，以及人们面对死亡、偶然性和意义缺失时的困境。小说常与“荒诞哲学”联系起来，但默尔索并不只是一个哲学命题的化身；他的沉默、感官经验和行为也需要放在殖民地阿尔及利亚的历史背景中理解。尤其值得注意的是，被杀害的阿拉伯人物几乎没有个人身份和声音，这一点引发了后来的后殖民批评。阅读时应同时讨论作品的存在主义问题和殖民叙事局限，避免只把它解释成抽象的自由寓言。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Albert Camus’s *The Stranger* follows Meursault, a French Algerian office worker whose emotional responses often appear detached from the expectations of the people around him. After his mother dies in a care home, he attends the funeral without displaying the grief others expect. He returns to ordinary routines, begins a relationship with Marie, and becomes involved in the affairs of his neighbor Raymond. During a confrontation on a beach, Meursault shoots and kills an unnamed Arab man. The subsequent trial examines not only the circumstances of the killing but also Meursault’s behavior at his mother’s funeral and his apparent indifference to conventional emotional rules.

The courtroom becomes a place where social judgment and legal judgment overlap. The prosecution treats Meursault’s failure to perform expected grief as evidence of his moral character, even though it is not the same as proof of the crime itself. Camus uses this tension to explore the absurd: human beings seek meaning and moral order in a world that does not necessarily provide clear answers. Meursault’s attention to heat, light, physical discomfort, and immediate sensation contrasts with the abstract explanations demanded by society.

The novel is often read as a work of existential and absurdist literature, but its colonial setting also matters. The victim is identified primarily through his racial and colonial position and is given little individual voice. Later readers and critics have questioned this narrative absence and the way the novel reflects French Algeria. A responsible reading should therefore consider both Camus’s philosophical concerns and the historical limits of the perspective he presents.

*The Stranger* is useful for examining alienation, mortality, social conformity, legal judgment, colonial representation, and the search for meaning. Its restrained style makes the reader confront the distance between what a person experiences internally and what society expects that person to express.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解荒诞哲学、死亡意识与意义追问。
- 区分法律事实、道德评价和社会情绪。
- 分析第一人称叙述与情感疏离的写法。
- 从殖民历史角度批判小说中被边缘化的声音。
- 讨论个人表达与社会规范之间的冲突。
