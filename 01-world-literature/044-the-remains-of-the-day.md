# 044 · The Remains of the Day

- **作者：** Kazuo Ishiguro
- **首次出版/成书：** 1989
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：1989年布克奖获奖小说，现代英国文学的重要作品。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《长日将尽》由英国管家史蒂文斯以第一人称回忆叙述。他在退休前驾车前往西部探望昔日同事肯顿小姐，旅程让他重新审视自己在达林顿府服务数十年的经历。史蒂文斯把“尊严”理解为忠诚、克制和专业，曾全心服务贵族主人达林顿勋爵，却不愿承认主人在战前政治活动中的错误，也压抑了自己对肯顿小姐的感情。随着回忆逐渐展开，读者发现他的叙述存在选择性：他强调职业责任，却回避个人遗憾；他认为自己只是履行职责，却不得不面对服务对象的道德判断。石黑一雄通过克制、含蓄和不可靠叙述，讨论阶级、职业身份、情感压抑和历史责任。作品还反思英国贵族制度衰落以及战前绥靖政治的历史背景。史蒂文斯的悲剧不在于他没有做出宏大选择，而在于他把判断权交给主人，因而错过了审视自身生活的机会。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Kazuo Ishiguro’s *The Remains of the Day* is narrated by Stevens, an English butler who has devoted his working life to serving the aristocratic household of Darlington Hall. Near the end of his career, Stevens takes a motoring trip through the English countryside to visit Miss Kenton, a former housekeeper with whom he once worked closely. The journey prompts him to reflect on his professional ideals, his memories of the household, and the choices that shaped his life.

Stevens defines dignity through loyalty, emotional restraint, and the ability to perform one’s duties without allowing personal feelings to interfere. He was deeply committed to Lord Darlington, even when his employer became involved in political activities that later appeared morally compromised. Stevens often describes himself as a professional who had no responsibility to judge the decisions of the person he served. Yet his recollections reveal gaps and contradictions. He avoids acknowledging his own grief, his feelings for Miss Kenton, and the consequences of placing loyalty above independent moral judgment.

Ishiguro uses a restrained first-person voice to make the reader notice what Stevens cannot or will not say. The novel’s emotional force comes from understatement: small details and pauses reveal more than direct confession. Its historical setting also connects private life to the decline of the British aristocracy and the political atmosphere of the years before the Second World War.

*The Remains of the Day* is valuable for studying unreliable narration, class, professional identity, emotional repression, and moral responsibility. It asks whether a person can preserve dignity by serving an institution without questioning its purposes. Stevens’s journey suggests that a life organized entirely around duty may leave too little room for independent judgment, love, and regret.

## 核心学习价值

- 分析职业伦理、忠诚和独立判断之间的冲突。
- 理解英国贵族制度衰落与战前政治背景。
- 学习克制叙事和不可靠叙述。
- 讨论情感压抑、遗憾与个人选择。
- 思考服务组织时个人是否仍负有道德责任。

dignity · loyalty · memory · class · moral responsibility
