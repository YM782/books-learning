# 050 · The Kite Runner

- **作者：** Khaled Hosseini
- **首次出版/成书：** 2003
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：具有广泛国际传播力的当代小说，以个人命运呈现阿富汗历史变迁。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《追风筝的人》以阿富汗喀布尔为起点，讲述富家男孩阿米尔与仆人之子哈桑之间复杂的友谊。两人一起放风筝，哈桑忠诚而勇敢，阿米尔却因嫉妒、恐惧和渴望父亲认可，在关键时刻没有保护朋友，之后又长期逃避内疚。苏联入侵后，阿米尔和父亲逃往美国，阿富汗的政治局势和家庭命运随之改变。多年后，阿米尔得知哈桑的身世秘密，并获得回到阿富汗弥补过去的机会。小说通过个人记忆与国家历史交织，描写种族歧视、阶级差异、战争、流亡和代际关系。它将“救赎”写成艰难的行动，而不是一句道歉：阿米尔必须面对自己曾经的沉默，也要承担帮助下一代的风险。作品是虚构小说，不能代替对阿富汗历史和哈扎拉人处境的系统研究；阅读时可结合可靠历史资料，理解故事所处的社会背景。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Khaled Hosseini’s *The Kite Runner* begins in Kabul, Afghanistan, where Amir, the son of a wealthy businessman, grows up alongside Hassan, the son of his father’s servant. The boys share games and kite-flying competitions, but their friendship is shaped by class difference and ethnic hierarchy. Hassan is loyal and courageous, while Amir is insecure and longs for his father’s approval. After a traumatic incident, Amir fails to protect Hassan and spends years trying to avoid the guilt associated with his silence.

The Soviet invasion forces Amir and his father to leave Afghanistan and settle in the United States. Their lives change, but Amir’s memories remain unresolved. Years later, he receives an opportunity to return to Afghanistan and confront the consequences of his childhood choices. He also discovers a family secret that changes his understanding of Hassan and of his own past. The journey becomes an attempt at moral repair, but it requires action and risk rather than a simple apology.

The novel connects private relationships with Afghanistan’s political history, including war, displacement, the rise of the Taliban, and the persecution of the Hazara community. It explores guilt, loyalty, fatherhood, class, ethnic discrimination, migration, and the possibility of redemption. Its emotional narrative makes historical events accessible through individual lives, but it remains a work of fiction rather than a comprehensive historical account. Readers should use reliable historical sources to understand the broader context and avoid treating one family’s story as representative of every Afghan experience.

*The Kite Runner* is valuable for studying moral responsibility, memory, friendship, migration, and the consequences of political violence. It asks whether people can repair past harm and what responsibilities they have when they gain the chance to act differently.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解阿富汗战争、流亡与社会变迁背景。
- 分析阶级、族群歧视和哈扎拉人处境。
- 讨论沉默、背叛、内疚与道德责任。
- 理解救赎需要行动而不只是道歉。
- 区分虚构人物经历与真实历史群体的多样性。

Afghanistan · friendship · guilt · migration · redemption
