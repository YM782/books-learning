# 018 · Moby-Dick

- **作者：** Herman Melville
- **首次出版/成书：** 1851
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：美国文学史上的重要实验性小说；《卫报》2026 年百大小说榜第 15 名。**

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《白鲸》由水手以实玛利讲述。他加入捕鲸船“裴廓德号”，船长亚哈却把航行变成追捕一头曾咬伤自己的白色巨鲸莫比·迪克的复仇行动。船员来自不同国家和文化背景，最重要的伙伴之一是来自南太平洋的捕鲸手魁魁格。随着航程推进，亚哈越来越执着于把白鲸视为邪恶力量或命运的化身，逐渐忽视船员安全和现实风险。小说穿插大量关于鲸类、捕鲸技术、海洋生态、船只结构和水手生活的知识性章节，使作品既像冒险故事，也像百科全书和哲学沉思。以实玛利的观察则不断追问人类能否真正认识自然、命运和世界的深层意义。白鲸可以被理解为自然的不可掌控、宇宙的冷漠、未知事物或人类强加意义的对象，但小说并未给出唯一答案。亚哈的悲剧提醒读者，执着追求一个绝对解释或复仇目标，可能让人把复杂世界简化成单一敌人，并牺牲共同体。作品融合海洋冒险、宗教典故、戏剧化独白和知识分类，是美国文学中极具实验性的经典。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Herman Melville’s *Moby-Dick* is narrated by Ishmael, a sailor who joins the whaling ship Pequod. The vessel is commanded by Captain Ahab, whose life has been dominated by a desire for revenge against Moby Dick, a great white whale that previously injured him. Ahab turns the voyage into a personal pursuit, treating the whale as the embodiment of evil, fate, or an enemy that must be confronted. His obsession gradually places the entire crew in danger. Among the sailors is Queequeg, a skilled harpooner from the South Pacific whose friendship with Ishmael challenges narrow assumptions about culture and identity.

The novel combines an adventure story with extensive discussions of whales, whaling methods, ships, ocean life, labor, religion, and philosophy. Some chapters resemble scientific classification or technical explanation, while others use dramatic speeches, symbolism, biblical references, and comic scenes. This unusual structure reflects the narrator’s attempt to understand a world that cannot be reduced to one simple meaning. The white whale may represent nature’s power, the unknowable universe, evil, or the human tendency to project personal fears onto something beyond control. Melville does not settle the question.

Ahab’s pursuit becomes a warning about the danger of turning uncertainty into an absolute enemy. His determination may appear heroic, but it also narrows his vision and makes him indifferent to the lives of others. Ishmael’s more reflective perspective offers a different response: curiosity, observation, and recognition of human limits. *Moby-Dick* is important for studying American literature, maritime history, ecological imagination, symbolism, and the conflict between knowledge and obsession. It also invites readers to consider how people create meaning when the natural world refuses to provide clear answers.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解象征如何承载自然、命运和未知等多重意义。
- 认识十九世纪捕鲸业、海洋劳动与航海知识。
- 分析亚哈的执念及其对群体造成的风险。
- 学习混合叙事、知识性章节和哲学独白。
- 思考人类认识自然的边界与生态伦理。
