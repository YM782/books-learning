# 003 · Ulysses（尤利西斯）

- **作者：** James Joyce（詹姆斯·乔伊斯）
- **首次出版：** 1922 年
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 《卫报》2026 年 100 部最佳小说榜列第 3；现代主义小说的重要代表，深刻影响叙事形式与文学语言。
- **阅读提示：** 不必一开始追求读懂所有典故。先把握 1904 年 6 月 16 日都柏林一天内的人物行动与关系。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《尤利西斯》把故事集中在1904年6月16日的爱尔兰都柏林，通过广告推销员利奥波德·布卢姆、年轻作家斯蒂芬·迪达勒斯以及布卢姆的妻子莫莉等人物，描绘普通人在城市中度过的一天。小说的情节看似平凡：人物走路、工作、吃饭、交谈、回忆和思考，但乔伊斯将这些日常经验与荷马史诗《奥德赛》的结构相互映照，使现代城市生活获得古典史诗般的广阔意义。作品最突出的特点是形式不断变化：有时接近传统叙事，有时使用意识流、戏剧脚本、新闻标题、模仿不同文体的语言实验，最后以莫莉绵延不绝的内心独白收束。读者会进入人物跳跃、矛盾而私密的意识，看到欲望、羞耻、孤独、记忆和爱如何在日常生活中交错。小说也描写爱尔兰的民族身份、宗教、殖民历史、性别关系与城市文化。它并不依赖惊险事件，而是挑战读者对“小说应该怎样讲故事”的预期。《尤利西斯》既是关于都柏林的城市肖像，也是关于现代人如何在庞杂的思想与社会环境中寻找归属的实验性作品。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

James Joyce’s *Ulysses* follows several characters through Dublin on 16 June 1904. Its central figures include Leopold Bloom, an advertising canvasser; Stephen Dedalus, a young intellectual and aspiring writer; and Molly Bloom, Leopold’s wife. The events of the day are mostly ordinary: people walk through streets, attend work, eat, talk, remember, argue, and return home. Yet Joyce gives these everyday activities an epic dimension by loosely paralleling the structure of Homer’s *Odyssey*. Bloom becomes a modern, domestic counterpart to Odysseus, while the city itself provides the varied landscape through which the characters move.

The novel is famous for its radical experiments with form and language. Each episode adopts a different narrative technique or stylistic pattern. Some passages resemble conventional third-person narration; others use interior monologue, dramatic dialogue, newspaper headlines, parody, musical structures, or elaborate verbal games. The reader is invited to experience consciousness from the inside, where thoughts may shift suddenly between memory, desire, embarrassment, observation, and fantasy. The final episode, presented through Molly’s extended interior monologue, is particularly influential for its representation of thought without ordinary punctuation or a stable external narrator.

At the same time, *Ulysses* is deeply rooted in its historical setting. It engages with Irish nationalism, British rule, Catholic culture, antisemitism, sexuality, family life, and the changing modern city. Its complexity can make the book demanding, but the difficulty is part of its artistic project: Joyce asks readers to notice how language shapes perception and how a seemingly insignificant day contains an entire world of experience. The novel is valuable for studying modernism, narrative innovation, urban culture, identity, and the relationship between classical tradition and modern life.

## 核心学习价值

1. **现代主义文学：** 理解意识流、多视角和形式实验如何改变小说。
2. **语言敏感度：** 观察语体、修辞、声音与叙事视角如何影响意义。
3. **城市与身份：** 认识都柏林的历史、宗教、民族与社会背景。
4. **比较阅读：** 将现代日常生活与《奥德赛》的史诗结构联系起来。
5. **阅读耐心：** 学会把复杂作品拆分成章节、人物线索和主题层次。
