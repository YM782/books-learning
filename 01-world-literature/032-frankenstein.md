# 032 · Frankenstein

- **作者：** Mary Shelley
- **首次出版/成书：** 1818 (revised edition 1831)
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：哥特文学和现代科幻传统的奠基作品。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《弗兰肯斯坦》讲述年轻科学家维克多·弗兰肯斯坦试图揭开生命奥秘，并利用实验创造出一个有生命的存在。造物者看到自己创造的生物外貌可怕，便惊恐逃离，没有承担照料和教育的责任。被遗弃的生物在观察人类家庭、学习语言和阅读书籍的过程中，逐渐形成情感、道德判断和对陪伴的渴望，却因外貌屡遭拒绝，最终走向愤怒与报复。小说通过维克多、造物者和探险家沃尔顿的多层叙述，讨论科学野心、孤独、社会排斥、责任与人性的边界。它并非简单警告科学本身危险，而是追问知识追求者是否应当预见实验后果，并对自己创造的生命负责。造物者既有令人同情的遭遇，也实施了不可辩护的暴力；维克多既是有创造力的研究者，也是逃避责任的人。玛丽·雪莱把哥特小说、早期科学想象和哲学问题结合起来，使作品成为现代科幻文学的重要先驱。阅读时应注意原著中的“弗兰肯斯坦”是科学家而不是造物本身，这一常见误称也反映作品形象如何进入大众文化。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Mary Shelley’s *Frankenstein* tells the story of Victor Frankenstein, a young scientist whose ambition leads him to investigate the secret of life and create a living being through experimental methods. When the creature awakens, Victor is horrified by its appearance and abandons it. Without guidance, care, or companionship, the creature must learn how to survive. It observes a family, studies language, and reads books, gradually developing emotional awareness and a desire to be accepted. Yet people repeatedly reject it because of its appearance, and the creature’s loneliness turns into anger and revenge.

The novel is told through a series of nested narratives, including the account of the explorer Robert Walton, Victor’s story, and the creature’s own testimony. This structure encourages readers to question how stories are shaped by perspective and how sympathy changes when a supposedly frightening figure is allowed to speak. The creature is capable of tenderness and moral reflection, but it also commits terrible acts. Victor is intellectually gifted, yet he refuses the responsibilities that follow from his experiment. Neither character can be reduced to a simple symbol of good or evil.

Shelley explores scientific ambition, the limits of knowledge, social exclusion, parental responsibility, isolation, and the human need for recognition. The novel does not merely claim that science is dangerous. It asks what ethical duties accompany discovery and whether creators must consider the consequences of what they bring into the world. Written in the context of Romantic-era debates about science, nature, and human possibility, *Frankenstein* combines Gothic atmosphere with philosophical speculation and is often regarded as a foundational work of science fiction. Readers should remember that Frankenstein is the scientist’s name, not the creature’s. That popular confusion itself demonstrates how the story has been reshaped by later adaptations.

## 核心学习价值

- 讨论科学研究中的责任、风险和伦理边界。
- 理解社会排斥如何影响个体身份与行为。
- 分析多层叙述与视角转换。
- 区分创造者责任、受害经历与个人暴力责任。
- 认识哥特文学与早期科幻传统的联系。

## 关键词

science · responsibility · isolation · creation · ethics

## 推荐读者

适合希望理解世界文学、历史背景、人物心理和社会结构，并愿意结合原著与可靠注释进行深入阅读的读者。
