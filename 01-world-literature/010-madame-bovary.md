# 010 · Madame Bovary（包法利夫人）

- **作者：** Gustave Flaubert（居斯塔夫·福楼拜）
- **首次出版：** 1856 年连载；1857 年出版单行本
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 《卫报》2026 年 100 部最佳小说榜列第 10；现实主义文学的代表作，影响现代小说的叙事风格。
- **阅读提示：** 留意艾玛如何把浪漫文学中的想象带入现实生活，以及消费、债务和社会地位如何影响她的选择。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《包法利夫人》讲述乡村医生夏尔·包法利的妻子艾玛对现实生活逐渐失望的过程。艾玛年轻时阅读浪漫小说，向往激情、奢华和上流社会生活；婚后却发现日常婚姻、乡村环境和丈夫平庸的性格无法满足她的想象。她先后通过婚外情、购买昂贵物品和追求社交享受，试图摆脱生活的空虚，但这些行为不仅没有带来真正的满足，反而让她陷入债务、欺骗和更深的孤立。福楼拜以冷静、精确而富有讽刺意味的叙述，展示个人幻想如何受到消费文化、性别规范、阶级欲望和现实经济条件的塑造。艾玛并非单纯的受害者，也不是可以轻易谴责的坏人；她既渴望自主与美好生活，也不断被自己无法辨认的欲望牵引。小说的重要意义在于揭示浪漫想象与现实之间的落差，以及社会如何为女性提供有限的生活道路。作品还以高度讲究的语言和叙事视角，推动现实主义小说的发展。阅读时可以思考：当一个人把幸福完全寄托于消费、爱情幻想和外部身份时，为什么容易陷入失望？

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Gustave Flaubert’s *Madame Bovary* follows Emma, the wife of a country doctor, as she becomes increasingly dissatisfied with the ordinary life available to her. As a young woman, Emma has absorbed romantic novels that promise passion, luxury, elegance, and extraordinary emotional experiences. Marriage to Charles Bovary does not fulfill those expectations. Their provincial home seems dull to her, and Charles’s kindness cannot provide the excitement or social distinction she imagines. Emma begins to seek escape through romantic affairs, expensive purchases, and attempts to enter a more glamorous social world.

These efforts bring temporary excitement but also deepen her financial and emotional difficulties. Consumer desires lead to debt; secret relationships create anxiety and disappointment; and the gap between fantasy and reality becomes harder to ignore. Flaubert presents Emma neither as a simple villain nor as an uncomplicated victim. Her choices matter, but they are also shaped by the limited opportunities available to women, the power of social status, and the commercial culture that encourages people to measure happiness through possessions and appearances.

The novel is renowned for its precise prose, irony, and controlled narrative perspective. Flaubert often allows readers to see the difference between what characters believe and what their circumstances reveal. This distance creates a powerful critique of romantic clichés and social pretension. *Madame Bovary* is not only a story about adultery; it is an examination of desire, boredom, money, gender, and the stories people tell themselves about the lives they deserve. It helped establish the methods of literary realism and influenced later writers through its attention to ordinary details and its refusal to impose easy moral explanations. Readers can use the novel to consider how expectations are formed, how consumerism shapes identity, and why imagined happiness may become destructive when it is disconnected from reality.

## 核心学习价值

1. **现实主义文学：** 学习作者如何通过日常细节呈现社会与心理问题。
2. **消费与欲望：** 分析物质消费、身份焦虑和幸福想象之间的关系。
3. **性别与社会：** 理解十九世纪女性面临的教育、婚姻和经济限制。
4. **叙事技巧：** 观察反讽、自由间接引语和叙述距离的作用。
5. **现实判断：** 思考如何区分个人愿望、社会宣传与真实生活条件。
