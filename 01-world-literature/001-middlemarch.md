# 001 · Middlemarch（米德尔马契）

- **作者：** George Eliot（乔治·艾略特，Mary Ann Evans 的笔名）
- **首次出版：** 1871–1872 年分册连载；1872 年结集出版
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 《卫报》2026 年 100 部最佳小说榜列第 1。该榜单由 172 位作家、评论家和学者参与评选。
- **阅读提示：** 人物众多，建议先记住多萝西娅、利德盖特、卡苏朋、布鲁克等主要人物，再观察他们的关系如何交织。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《米德尔马契》以十九世纪英国虚构小镇为舞台，描绘改革前后的社会生活。小说并没有依靠单一英雄推动情节，而是让多萝西娅、利德盖特、卡苏朋、弗雷德等人物的命运彼此交错，呈现婚姻、金钱、职业、宗教、教育和地方政治如何影响普通人的选择。年轻的多萝西娅渴望过一种有意义、能够服务他人的生活，却在婚姻中逐渐发现理想与现实之间的距离；医生利德盖特希望以科学改善社会，却被债务、名誉和人际关系牵制。小镇里每个人都拥有自己的愿望，也受到阶级、性别、家庭和舆论的限制。乔治·艾略特通过细腻的心理描写与多线叙事，展示善意如何被误解、理想如何受到现实考验，以及一个人的微小决定怎样影响周围的人。作品提醒读者，社会进步不仅由伟大事件构成，也由无数普通人的责任、偏见、同情和日常选择共同形成。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

George Eliot’s *Middlemarch* is set in a fictional English provincial town during the years surrounding the Reform era of the early nineteenth century. Rather than following a single hero, the novel brings together several families and social circles whose ambitions, disappointments, and decisions gradually become intertwined. Dorothea Brooke longs for a purposeful life devoted to learning and service, but her marriage to the much older scholar Edward Casaubon exposes the distance between intellectual idealism and emotional reality. Tertius Lydgate, an ambitious young doctor, hopes to modernize medical practice and improve public health, yet his professional plans are complicated by debt, local politics, reputation, and marriage. Other characters, including Fred Vincy, Mary Garth, Rosamond Vincy, and Nicholas Bulstrode, reveal different ways in which money, status, affection, and moral responsibility shape everyday life.

Eliot’s achievement lies in showing how private choices are connected to wider social structures. The town is not merely a backdrop: its gossip, institutions, religious expectations, economic pressures, and political arguments influence what individuals can imagine and what they can actually do. The narrative moves between perspectives, allowing readers to understand characters who misunderstand one another. It is especially attentive to the moral consequences of limited knowledge: people often act with incomplete information, interpret others unfairly, or mistake social approval for genuine goodness.

The novel does not offer a simple division between heroes and villains. Instead, it asks readers to consider how sympathy, self-deception, ambition, and responsibility coexist in ordinary lives. *Middlemarch* is valuable for studying realism, social change, gender expectations, marriage, professional identity, and ethical judgment. Its central insight is that historical progress is made not only through public reforms but also through countless small acts of attention, courage, generosity, and restraint.

## 核心学习价值

1. **社会结构：** 理解阶级、性别、金钱与地方舆论如何塑造个人机会。
2. **批判性阅读：** 从多视角叙事中识别偏见、误解与不完整信息。
3. **伦理判断：** 思考善意、责任、动机与实际后果之间的差异。
4. **历史理解：** 观察十九世纪英国社会改革如何进入普通人的生活。
5. **英语学习：** 积累人物描写、社会关系、道德评价与现实主义文学词汇。

## 关键词

realism · social reform · marriage · sympathy · moral responsibility
