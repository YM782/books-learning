# 056 · One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest

- **作者：** Ken Kesey
- **首次出版/成书：** 1962
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：二十世纪美国社会批判小说，影响关于制度权力和精神医疗的公共讨论。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《飞越疯人院》由精神病院患者酋长布罗姆登以第一人称讲述。他假装听不见、看不见，以此观察病房中护士拉契特严格控制日常生活、药物、集体会议和患者行为。新来的麦克墨菲以反抗规则、幽默和挑战权威的方式打破病房秩序，鼓励患者重新表达意愿。两人之间的冲突逐渐演变成关于权力、服从、治疗、尊严和自由的斗争。小说通过布罗姆登带有幻觉色彩的叙述，把病房写成一个被机械化、监控和制度化管理的空间，反映二十世纪中期美国对精神医疗机构和社会规范的怀疑。但作品并不是可靠的精神医学教材，部分描写反映其时代观念，也受到后来精神健康领域的发展和批评。阅读时应区分文学象征与真实临床实践，避免把患者、医护人员或精神疾病简单刻板化。作品的核心问题是：治疗制度如何在保护和控制之间保持边界，患者的声音如何被尊重。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Ken Kesey’s *One Flew Over the Cuckoo’s Nest* is narrated by Chief Bromden, a patient in an Oregon psychiatric hospital who pretends to be deaf and mute. From this position, he observes the routines of the ward and the authority exercised by Nurse Ratched. Medication, group meetings, schedules, and disciplinary procedures organize the patients’ lives. When Randle P. McMurphy arrives, his humor, defiance, and willingness to challenge the rules disrupt the established order. He encourages other patients to express their preferences and to imagine that they can make choices for themselves.

The conflict between McMurphy and Nurse Ratched becomes a struggle over authority, conformity, treatment, and personal dignity. Bromden’s narration is shaped by fear, memory, and hallucination-like imagery. He describes the institution through metaphors of machinery and control, suggesting that social systems can make people feel powerless even when they claim to be acting for their benefit. As the story develops, the patients’ relationships reveal both the possibility of solidarity and the risks of resistance.

The novel reflects mid-twentieth-century American debates about psychiatric institutions, social conformity, and the treatment of people considered different. However, it should not be used as a factual guide to modern mental-health care. Some of its representations reflect the period in which it was written and have been questioned in light of later changes in psychiatric practice and disability studies. Readers should avoid turning patients or professionals into simple stereotypes.

*One Flew Over the Cuckoo’s Nest* is valuable for discussing institutional power, consent, autonomy, stigma, and the ethics of care. Its central question remains important: how can institutions provide support and safety without silencing the people they are meant to serve?

## 核心学习价值

- 讨论治疗、照护与制度控制之间的边界。
- 分析患者自主权、知情同意和污名化。
- 学习不可靠叙述与象征性空间描写。
- 认识二十世纪美国精神医疗制度的历史争论。
- 区分文学表现与现代精神健康专业知识。
