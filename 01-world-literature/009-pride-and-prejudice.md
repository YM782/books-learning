# 009 · Pride and Prejudice（傲慢与偏见）

- **作者：** Jane Austen（简·奥斯汀）
- **首次出版：** 1813 年
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 《卫报》2026 年 100 部最佳小说榜列第 9；以机智的社会观察和婚恋叙事持续影响英语文学。
- **阅读提示：** 关注伊丽莎白与达西如何修正对彼此的判断，并留意婚姻背后的财产继承与阶级条件。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《傲慢与偏见》围绕班纳特一家五个女儿的婚姻与生活展开，描绘十九世纪初英国乡绅社会的财产制度、阶级观念和社交规则。由于家庭财产受到男性继承制度限制，女儿们的婚姻不仅关乎爱情，也关系到经济安全和未来生活。聪明而有主见的伊丽莎白·班纳特初次遇见富有的达西先生时，认为他傲慢冷漠；达西则因阶级偏见低估班纳特家庭。随着舞会、拜访、求婚、误会和信件逐渐展开，两人开始发现自己的判断并不完整。伊丽莎白必须承认自己受到第一印象和偏见影响，达西也需要反思傲慢、身份优越感与行为方式。小说以幽默、对话和细致的社交场景，揭示女性在财产、婚姻和社会地位方面受到的限制，同时赞美理性判断、诚实和自我修正。奥斯汀没有把爱情写成脱离现实的浪漫幻想，而是让人物在情感、经济条件、家庭责任和社会评价之间作出选择。作品最重要的主题之一，是人们如何通过反省克服偏见，并在平等与尊重的基础上建立关系。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Jane Austen’s *Pride and Prejudice* follows the Bennet family, especially the second daughter, Elizabeth, as she navigates courtship, family pressure, and the social expectations of early nineteenth-century England. The Bennet household has five daughters, but the family estate is subject to inheritance rules that favor a male heir. Marriage is therefore not simply a private romantic decision; it can determine a woman’s financial security and social future. When the wealthy and reserved Mr. Darcy arrives in the neighborhood, Elizabeth interprets his behavior as arrogance. Darcy, meanwhile, initially looks down on the Bennet family because of their connections and social position.

Through dances, visits, proposals, misunderstandings, and letters, both characters gradually discover that their judgments are incomplete. Elizabeth must recognize how pride in her own powers of observation has made her vulnerable to prejudice. Darcy must confront the arrogance that comes from wealth, rank, and confidence in his own status. Their relationship develops not because either character is perfect, but because both become capable of self-examination and change.

Austen’s comedy is built from sharp dialogue, irony, social observation, and carefully controlled narrative perspective. Beneath the romantic plot, the novel examines class, property, reputation, gender, family obligations, and the limited choices available to women. It neither dismisses love nor treats it as sufficient on its own: a successful relationship requires judgment, mutual respect, emotional maturity, and an awareness of practical circumstances. The novel remains widely read because its characters are recognizable and its central problem is enduring. People often misunderstand others when they mistake first impressions for knowledge. *Pride and Prejudice* invites readers to question their assumptions and to see self-knowledge as a necessary part of both love and ethical life.

## 核心学习价值

1. **批判性思维：** 认识第一印象、偏见和自我确信如何影响判断。
2. **社会制度：** 理解财产继承、阶级和婚姻如何限制女性选择。
3. **沟通能力：** 观察对话、信件和沉默如何改变人际关系。
4. **自我修正：** 学习人物承认错误、调整观点和成熟成长的过程。
5. **英语阅读：** 适合学习讽刺、礼貌表达、社交对话与人物性格描写。
