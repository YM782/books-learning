# 054 · The Scarlet Letter

- **作者：** Nathaniel Hawthorne
- **首次出版/成书：** 1850
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：美国文学经典，深刻讨论宗教、道德与社会惩罚。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《红字》发生在十七世纪清教徒统治下的新英格兰，讲述海丝特·白兰因婚外生女，被迫在衣服上佩戴象征“通奸”的红色字母A。她拒绝公开孩子父亲的身份，独自抚养女儿珠儿，在社区羞辱和宗教监督下努力生活。牧师丁梅斯代尔暗中承受罪疚，海丝特的丈夫齐灵渥斯则以复仇为目的接近他。小说通过三人关系讨论罪、羞耻、公开惩罚、秘密、宽恕和道德判断。红字的意义并非固定不变：社会试图把它当作永久污名，海丝特却通过劳动和行动重新定义它；珠儿则成为秘密关系的活生生见证。霍桑既批判清教社会的严苛，也关注个人如何面对自己的责任。作品还揭示性别双重标准：海丝特承担公开惩罚，男性人物却能够隐藏身份。阅读时可以分析象征物如何在不同人物和时代中改变意义，也应注意小说是十九世纪作者对殖民地清教历史的文学再现，而非直接的十七世纪档案。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Nathaniel Hawthorne’s *The Scarlet Letter* is set in a Puritan settlement in seventeenth-century New England. Hester Prynne is publicly punished for adultery and forced to wear a scarlet letter A on her clothing. She refuses to reveal the identity of the child’s father and raises her daughter, Pearl, while living under the judgment of the community. The minister Arthur Dimmesdale secretly carries guilt, while Hester’s estranged husband, Roger Chillingworth, returns under another identity and becomes increasingly consumed by revenge.

The novel examines sin, shame, public punishment, secrecy, conscience, and the authority of religious institutions. The letter A is intended to mark Hester permanently as an outsider, but its meaning changes as she works, helps others, and develops a life of her own. Pearl becomes a living reminder of the relationship that the community wants to define and control. Dimmesdale’s hidden guilt contrasts with Hester’s public humiliation, revealing a gendered double standard: the woman bears visible punishment while the man is able to preserve his public position for a time.

Hawthorne criticizes the harshness of Puritan moral authority, but he also asks individuals to face responsibility rather than simply blame society. The novel’s symbols—the letter, the scaffold, the forest, and Pearl—carry different meanings for different characters. Their significance changes as the story develops, making interpretation part of the reading experience.

Written in the nineteenth century, the book is a literary reconstruction of colonial Puritan history rather than a direct historical record. Readers should distinguish Hawthorne’s historical imagination from documentary evidence about the period. *The Scarlet Letter* is valuable for studying gender, religion, public shaming, moral judgment, symbolism, and the relationship between private conduct and social control.

## 核心学习价值

- 分析公开羞辱、社会惩罚和宗教权威。
- 理解性别双重标准及其社会后果。
- 学习象征意象如何在叙事中改变含义。
- 讨论秘密、罪疚、责任和宽恕。
- 区分历史小说与真实殖民地档案。

## 关键词

Puritanism · shame · gender · sin · symbolism
