# 013 · One Hundred Years of Solitude

- **作者：** Gabriel García Márquez
- **首次出版/成书：** 1967
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：拉丁美洲文学代表作；《卫报》2026 年百大小说榜第 17 名。**

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《百年孤独》讲述布恩迪亚家族在虚构小镇马孔多七代人的兴衰。家族创始人何塞·阿尔卡蒂奥·布恩迪亚与妻子乌尔苏拉建立村庄，后代不断重复相似的名字、性格、欲望和错误。马孔多从与世隔绝的村落逐渐接触外部世界，经历技术传入、内战、政治冲突、外国公司进入和社会灾难，最终走向衰败。马尔克斯把日常生活与奇异事件并置：升天、预言、幽灵和不可思议的自然现象，在人物眼中常与战争、爱情和劳动一样真实。这种魔幻现实主义写法并非单纯制造奇观，而是以神话、记忆和民间叙事呈现拉丁美洲复杂的历史经验。小说反复出现孤独、遗忘、权力、暴力和时间循环等主题，家族成员似乎无法从祖先的命运中真正脱身。作品也提醒读者，历史并不总以清晰、完整的档案保存；胜利者的叙述、集体沉默和反复发生的暴力，可能让社会忘记自身经历。阅读时应关注家族谱系与历史事件的交织，以及“孤独”如何成为个人心理和社会处境的共同隐喻。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Gabriel García Márquez’s *One Hundred Years of Solitude* follows the Buendía family across several generations in the fictional town of Macondo. The settlement is founded by José Arcadio Buendía and his wife Úrsula, and it gradually changes from an isolated village into a place connected to national politics, commerce, technology, and foreign investment. As the family expands, its members repeatedly inherit similar names, temperaments, desires, and mistakes. Their lives include passionate relationships, civil conflict, prosperity, exploitation, and loss. The town itself experiences moments of wonder and catastrophe, until its history reaches an ending that appears both personal and historical.

The novel is a central example of magical realism. Extraordinary events—such as levitation, prophetic manuscripts, ghosts, and unusual natural phenomena—are narrated alongside ordinary activities without a sharp boundary between the fantastic and the real. This approach does not simply add fantasy to a realistic setting. It draws on myth, oral storytelling, regional memory, and the historical experience of Latin America, where political violence and social upheaval can seem as astonishing as legend. The novel also explores how official narratives can erase suffering. One episode involving a massacre and the denial of its memory shows how a community may be forced to live with a past that powerful institutions refuse to acknowledge.

Solitude is both a personal condition and a pattern repeated throughout the family. Characters struggle to communicate, to escape inherited behavior, or to understand the consequences of their actions. Time often feels cyclical: the future resembles the past because the family cannot fully learn from its history. The book invites readers to examine memory, power, colonial and economic influence, political violence, and the relationship between individual identity and collective history. Its complex family tree rewards careful reading and makes the novel especially useful for comparing literary imagination with historical memory.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解魔幻现实主义及其与历史、神话和口述传统的关系。
- 梳理家族谱系，观察代际创伤与行为重复。
- 思考历史记忆如何被权力机构压制或改写。
- 分析孤独、权力、暴力和时间循环等主题。
- 认识拉丁美洲历史与文学表达的关联。
