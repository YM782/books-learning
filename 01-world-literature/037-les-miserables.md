# 037 · Les Misérables

- **作者：** Victor Hugo
- **首次出版/成书：** 1862
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：法国文学与社会正义主题的世界级经典。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《悲惨世界》以十九世纪法国社会为背景，围绕冉阿让从苦役犯到努力行善的转变展开。冉阿让因偷面包而服刑多年，获释后仍受到社会排斥，直到主教米里哀的宽恕改变了他的人生方向。他改名经营事业，帮助工人芳汀和她的女儿珂赛特，却一直受到警探沙威追捕。小说还描写马吕斯、街垒起义青年和巴黎贫民的生活，把个人命运与法国革命传统、贫困、教育、法律和政治理想联系起来。雨果既批判制度性贫困和不平等，也讨论法律秩序与道德正义之间的冲突：冉阿让是否永远只能被过去定义？沙威是否能接受法律之外的宽恕？作品篇幅宏大，包含大量历史、社会和宗教议论，但核心始终是人能否在苦难中保留尊严，并通过怜悯和行动改变他人的命运。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Victor Hugo’s *Les Misérables* is a sweeping novel about poverty, justice, love, and political change in nineteenth-century France. Its central figure, Jean Valjean, is released after years of hard labor for stealing bread. Society continues to treat him as a criminal, but an act of mercy by Bishop Myriel gives him the opportunity to rebuild his life. Valjean changes his identity, becomes a successful industrialist and mayor, and later risks his security to protect Fantine and her daughter Cosette. Inspector Javert pursues him because he believes that law and social order must be applied without exception.

The novel follows several generations and social groups. It portrays workers, students, prisoners, street children, religious figures, and the urban poor. Marius and the young revolutionaries become involved in the Paris uprising of 1832, linking private relationships to the wider history of political protest. Hugo repeatedly pauses the plot to discuss institutions, education, religion, architecture, war, and the causes of poverty. These digressions are part of his attempt to understand how social systems shape individual lives.

At the heart of the book is a conflict between legal judgment and moral justice. Valjean’s past cannot simply be erased, but his actions show that a person can change. Javert’s crisis reveals the limits of a worldview in which law is treated as the only possible source of moral order. Hugo argues for compassion and social reform without suggesting that change is easy or that suffering can be solved by individual kindness alone.

*Les Misérables* is valuable for studying criminal justice, poverty, labor, political movements, social exclusion, and moral responsibility. It asks readers to consider how institutions should treat people who have committed offenses and whether rehabilitation and dignity can coexist with public safety.

## 核心学习价值

- 区分法律秩序与道德正义。
- 理解贫困、失业和社会排斥的结构性原因。
- 认识十九世纪法国社会与1832年巴黎起义背景。
- 讨论惩罚、改过自新、宽恕与公共安全。
- 分析宏大叙事中的人物成长和社会评论。
