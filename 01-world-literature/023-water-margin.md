# 023 · Water Margin

- **作者：** Traditionally attributed to Shi Nai'an
- **首次出版/成书：** 14th century; major editions appeared later
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：中国古典章回小说代表作，反映民间叙事与社会伦理。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《水浒传》围绕一百零八位好汉聚集梁山泊的故事展开，描写他们因官府压迫、社会不公、个人恩怨或被迫反抗而走上聚义之路。林冲遭权贵陷害，鲁智深嫉恶如仇，武松勇猛果断，宋江则以讲义气和组织能力成为重要领袖。各人物的经历既有传奇色彩，也反映了传统社会中的官民关系、司法腐败、地方权力和生存困境。梁山群体以“替天行道”为旗号，建立内部纪律和兄弟秩序，但他们的行动也伴随暴力、报复和复杂的道德矛盾。作品后半部分写招安与征战，展示反抗力量被纳入国家秩序后的变化和代价。不同版本的结局差异较大，文本形成过程也较复杂，因此不宜把它看成作者一次性写成的单一作品。阅读时既要欣赏人物塑造和民间叙事，也要追问“正义”与暴力之间的界限，以及个人英雄主义能否改变制度性不公。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

*Water Margin*, also known as *Outlaws of the Marsh*, tells the story of a large group of men who gather at Liangshan Marsh after becoming victims of injustice, political persecution, personal conflict, or social exclusion. The novel includes many memorable figures. Lin Chong is driven toward rebellion after being framed by powerful officials; Lu Zhishen is impulsive but compassionate toward the vulnerable; Wu Song is famous for courage and physical strength; and Song Jiang becomes a central leader because of his reputation for loyalty and his ability to organize others. Their stories combine popular legends, local traditions, and reflections on social order.

The Liangshan group adopts the slogan of acting in the name of Heaven and develops its own codes of loyalty, discipline, and mutual protection. Yet the outlaws’ resistance is morally complicated. Their actions may expose official corruption and defend people who have been abused, but they also involve violence, revenge, and the use of force. The later episodes, in which the group accepts imperial amnesty and participates in military campaigns, raise questions about the limits of rebellion and the costs of being absorbed into the political system that once persecuted them.

The work’s history is complex. It grew from oral storytelling and earlier narrative traditions, and different editions contain different arrangements and endings. It is traditionally associated with Shi Nai’an, but the precise authorship and textual development remain debated. Readers should therefore identify which edition they are using. Beyond its exciting episodes, *Water Margin* offers material for studying popular justice, loyalty, official power, violence, social hierarchy, and the relationship between individual resistance and institutional reform. It asks whether heroic opposition can create a more just order, or whether it risks reproducing the same coercive structures it rejects.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解传统社会中的官僚权力、司法腐败与民间反抗。
- 分析忠义伦理与暴力行为之间的矛盾。
- 比较不同人物的正义观和行动方式。
- 认识口头文学、说书传统与章回小说的发展。
- 讨论反抗运动被国家吸纳后的制度困境。
