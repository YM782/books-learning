# 016 · Crime and Punishment

- **作者：** Fyodor Dostoevsky
- **首次出版/成书：** 1866
- **分类：** 世界文学与小说
- **入选依据：世界文学中影响深远的心理与道德小说；《卫报》2026 年百大小说榜第 69 名。**

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《罪与罚》讲述贫困的前大学生拉斯柯尔尼科夫在圣彼得堡杀害放高利贷的老妇人及其妹妹后，陷入恐惧、发热、孤立和精神挣扎的过程。他曾用一种理论说服自己：某些“非凡人物”可以为了更高目标越过普通道德规则。但真正实施犯罪后，他发现抽象思想无法消除现实中的痛苦，也无法让自己摆脱良知和他人的联系。警探波尔菲里通过谈话和心理观察逐渐逼近真相；索尼娅则以贫困中的善良、信仰和同情，为他提供另一种面对罪责的可能。小说的重点并非犯罪手法，而是犯罪前后的心理变化、道德自我辩护和社会环境。陀思妥耶夫斯基描绘贫困、城市拥挤、阶级差距和绝望如何影响人的选择，同时坚持人物仍需面对自己的行为。拉斯柯尔尼科夫最终走向认罪与流放，但真正的转变并非法律惩罚自动完成，而是他开始承认自己与他人的关系，重新理解责任和生命。作品适合讨论道德相对主义、权力幻想、心理压力、司法调查与悔罪。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Fyodor Dostoevsky’s *Crime and Punishment* follows Rodion Raskolnikov, a former university student living in extreme poverty in St. Petersburg. He develops a theory that certain extraordinary individuals may be entitled to violate ordinary moral rules if they believe their actions serve a higher purpose. Trying to test this idea, he murders an elderly pawnbroker and then kills her innocent sister, who unexpectedly enters the scene. The crime does not make him feel powerful or free. Instead, he becomes physically ill, psychologically unstable, suspicious of others, and increasingly unable to separate his abstract theory from the suffering he has caused.

The investigation is shaped by Porfiry Petrovich, a perceptive magistrate who uses conversation and psychological insight rather than relying only on direct evidence. Raskolnikov’s relationships with his family and with Sonia Marmeladov further complicate his attempt to remain isolated. Sonia, who has endured poverty and humiliation, offers compassion and a form of moral faith that contrasts with his pride and intellectual self-justification. Their conversations force him to confront the human consequences of his actions.

Dostoevsky is interested less in the mechanics of murder than in the mental and ethical crisis that follows it. The crowded city, economic hardship, social inequality, and limited opportunities form the novel’s environment, but they do not erase individual responsibility. Raskolnikov’s eventual confession and punishment begin a difficult process of change rather than providing an instant solution. The epilogue points toward the possibility of moral renewal through relationship, humility, and acceptance of responsibility.

The novel is valuable for studying moral reasoning, rationalization, guilt, investigative psychology, and the limits of theories that place certain people above ordinary ethical obligations. It also encourages readers to distinguish social causes of crime from the question of personal accountability.

## 核心学习价值

- 识别用抽象理论为伤害行为辩护的逻辑。
- 分析犯罪后的认知失调、恐惧和罪疚。
- 讨论社会贫困与个人责任之间的关系。
- 学习心理调查、对话和人物动机分析。
- 思考认罪、惩罚、同情与道德重建。

crime · guilt · rationalization · poverty · redemption
