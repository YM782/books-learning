# 093 · The Origins of Totalitarianism

- **作者：** Hannah Arendt
- **首次出版/成书：** 1951 (expanded edition 1958)
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：二十世纪政治理论与极权主义研究的奠基性著作。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

汉娜·阿伦特的《极权主义的起源》分析反犹主义、帝国主义和极权统治之间的历史联系，试图解释纳粹德国和斯大林时期苏联为何形成不同于传统专制制度的政治形态。阿伦特讨论民族国家、种族主义、殖民扩张、无国籍者、群众社会、宣传、秘密警察和集中营，指出极权主义不仅依靠暴力，也试图通过意识形态和恐怖摧毁人与人之间的信任及独立判断。作品特别关注现代社会中孤立的群众如何被政治运动动员，以及国家如何把人变成可以管理和消灭的类别。阿伦特的历史解释影响深远，但其对纳粹主义与斯大林主义的比较、对反犹主义和帝国主义因果关系的处理，以及某些历史细节，长期受到学者讨论。阅读时应把它视为政治理论与历史解释相结合的经典，而不是对所有独裁政体都适用的简单分类表。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Hannah Arendt’s *The Origins of Totalitarianism* investigates the historical conditions that contributed to modern totalitarian movements, especially Nazism in Germany and Stalinism in the Soviet Union. The book is organized around three major themes: antisemitism, imperialism, and totalitarianism. Arendt examines the development of racial ideology, colonial expansion, the crisis of the nation-state, stateless populations, mass society, propaganda, secret police, and concentration camps.

Arendt argues that totalitarian rule differs from older forms of tyranny because it seeks not only to dominate public institutions but also to reshape the conditions of human experience. Ideology claims to explain the whole of history, while terror destroys trust, independent judgment, and the spaces in which people can act together. The isolation of individuals and the breakdown of stable political communities can make mass movements more powerful. Stateless people and those excluded from legal protection reveal the vulnerability of rights when they depend entirely on membership in a political community.

The book has had a major influence on political theory, but its historical arguments remain debated. Scholars have questioned aspects of Arendt’s comparison between Nazism and Stalinism, the causal links she draws among antisemitism, imperialism, and totalitarian movements, and some details of her historical interpretation. Readers should therefore engage with it as a major theoretical argument rather than as a universally accepted classification of every authoritarian regime.

*The Origins of Totalitarianism* is valuable for studying modern political violence, propaganda, statelessness, racism, imperialism, and the relationship between individual isolation and mass politics. It encourages readers to examine how institutions, ideology, and fear can combine to destroy the conditions required for political freedom.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解反犹主义、帝国主义与极权运动的历史联系。
- 分析宣传、意识形态、恐怖和秘密警察。
- 认识无国籍者与法律权利保障的关系。
- 讨论群众孤立、社会瓦解与政治动员。
- 批判性比较阿伦特理论与后续历史研究。

## 关键词

totalitarianism · antisemitism · imperialism · ideology · statelessness

适合希望建立世界史框架、理解历史证据与因果解释，并愿意比较不同学术观点的读者。
