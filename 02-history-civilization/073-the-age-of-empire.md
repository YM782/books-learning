# 073 · The Age of Empire: 1875–1914

- **作者：** Eric Hobsbawm
- **首次出版/成书：** 1987
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：霍布斯鲍姆现代世界史三部曲第三部，理解一战前世界秩序的重要著作。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《帝国的年代》研究1875年至第一次世界大战爆发前的全球变化，重点讨论工业资本主义、帝国主义和民族国家竞争如何重塑世界秩序。霍布斯鲍姆分析欧洲列强对非洲和亚洲的殖民扩张、全球贸易和金融联系、民族主义政治、大众文化、城市生活和工人运动。工业化国家需要原料、市场和战略基地，殖民地则在不平等的权力关系中被纳入世界经济。与此同时，现代大众政治、报纸、教育和消费文化改变了普通人的社会经验。作品也讨论科学、艺术和文化如何反映对进步的信心及其背后的焦虑。作者强调帝国主义与资本主义结构之间的联系，但学者对帝国扩张的动因、殖民地社会的能动性和地区差异有不同解释。阅读时应结合殖民地本地史和受殖民者的材料，避免只从欧洲列强的视角理解帝国。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Eric Hobsbawm’s *The Age of Empire: 1875–1914* examines the decades before the First World War, when industrial capitalism, imperial expansion, and competition among nation-states transformed the global order. Hobsbawm explores the European powers’ expansion into Africa and Asia, the growth of international trade and finance, the spread of nationalism, and the changing culture of mass society. Industrial economies sought raw materials, markets, investment opportunities, and strategic positions, while colonized regions were incorporated into global systems under highly unequal conditions.

The book also examines developments within Europe and other industrial societies. Mass politics, newspapers, education, urban life, consumer culture, and labor movements changed the relationship between governments and ordinary people. Scientific and technological progress generated confidence, but it also existed alongside social anxiety, political rivalry, and new forms of militarism. Hobsbawm connects these developments to the tensions that would eventually contribute to the outbreak of the First World War.

His analysis emphasizes the relationship between capitalism and imperialism, reflecting his Marxist historical approach. This perspective is influential, but imperial expansion had multiple causes, and historians disagree about the relative importance of economics, strategy, ideology, domestic politics, and local collaboration. Readers should also examine the agency of colonized societies rather than viewing them only through the decisions of European powers.

*The Age of Empire* is valuable for studying imperialism, globalization, nationalism, mass politics, and the origins of modern international conflict. It helps readers understand how the late nineteenth century created both unprecedented global connections and deep structural inequalities.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解十九世纪末帝国主义与全球资本主义扩张。
- 分析殖民地如何被纳入不平等的世界经济。
- 认识民族主义、大众政治和现代消费文化。
- 讨论帝国扩张的经济、战略和意识形态动因。
- 结合殖民地史料补充欧洲中心的历史视角。
