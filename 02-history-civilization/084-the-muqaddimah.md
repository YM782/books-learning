# 084 · The Muqaddimah

- **作者：** Ibn Khaldun
- **首次出版/成书：** 1377
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：世界史学与社会理论史上的重要经典，影响后世历史和社会思想。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

伊本·赫勒敦的《历史绪论》原本是其世界史著作的导论，后来成为独立的历史与社会理论经典。作者讨论人类社会如何形成、游牧与定居群体如何互动、王朝为何兴起和衰亡，以及税收、劳动、城市、教育和知识如何影响国家能力。其核心概念“阿萨比亚”通常译为群体凝聚力或社会团结，指共同身份和互助关系如何支撑政治组织。伊本·赫勒敦认为，具有强凝聚力的群体可能建立王朝，但随着财富和奢侈增加，统治集团的团结会减弱，政权也更容易被新的力量取代。作品并非现代社会科学，但它以比较和因果分析反思历史传说，强调研究者应检验事件是否符合社会运行的条件。阅读时要结合十四世纪北非和伊斯兰世界背景，避免把其王朝循环理论当作所有社会必然遵循的规律。


 其方法论意义还在于要求历史学家把传说放回社会环境中检验，避免仅因故事流传广泛就相信其真实性。这种批判意识是理解历史研究的重要基础。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Ibn Khaldun’s *The Muqaddimah* began as an introduction to a larger universal history and became one of the most influential works of historical and social thought in the medieval Islamic world. Written in the fourteenth century, it examines how human societies form, how nomadic and settled communities interact, why dynasties rise and fall, and how taxation, labor, cities, education, and knowledge affect political power.

One of its central concepts is *asabiyyah*, often translated as group solidarity or social cohesion. Ibn Khaldun argues that strong bonds of shared identity and mutual support can enable a group to establish political authority. Over time, however, ruling elites may become accustomed to luxury and lose the solidarity that helped them gain power. A new group with stronger cohesion may then replace them. This model offers a way to think about political change through social organization rather than through the actions of rulers alone.

The work also reflects on how historians should evaluate reports. Ibn Khaldun warns that accounts may be unreliable when they are repeated without considering whether the events described are plausible given the social, economic, and political conditions of the time. This emphasis on causal reasoning and critical examination makes the book especially important for the history of historiography.

The *Muqaddimah* should not be treated as a modern scientific theory or as a universal law that every society must follow. Its arguments emerged from the political and intellectual world of fourteenth-century North Africa and the wider Islamic world. Readers should place its concepts in that context and compare them with later scholarship.

The work is valuable for studying state formation, social cohesion, taxation, urbanization, historical method, and the relationship between collective identity and political power.


 Its methodological importance lies in requiring historians to test inherited stories against social conditions and evidence, rather than accepting a report simply because it has been repeated. This remains a central discipline of historical inquiry.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解阿萨比亚，即群体凝聚力在政治组织中的作用。
- 分析王朝兴衰、税收、劳动和城市发展。
- 学习检验历史传闻是否符合社会条件。
- 认识十四世纪北非与伊斯兰思想背景。
- 比较中世纪社会理论与现代社会科学的异同。
