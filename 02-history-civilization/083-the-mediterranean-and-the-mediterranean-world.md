# 083 · The Mediterranean and the Mediterranean World in the Age of Philip II

- **作者：** Fernand Braudel
- **首次出版/成书：** 1949
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：年鉴学派史学的里程碑，深刻影响社会经济史和全球史。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

费尔南·布罗代尔的《菲利普二世时代的地中海与地中海世界》以十六世纪地中海为中心，提出一种不同于传统政治事件史的历史写法。作者把历史分成多个时间层次：地理和气候变化缓慢，经济与社会结构以中等速度变化，战争、外交和统治者决策则属于较短的事件时间。作品描写航海路线、港口、山脉、气候、贸易、人口、农业、海盗和帝国竞争，说明地理环境与经济网络如何长期塑造政治行动。菲利普二世的外交和军事政策只是其中一个层次，不能脱离更广阔的地中海结构来理解。布罗代尔的“长时段”方法改变了社会经济史和全球史研究，但其宏大结构也可能弱化个人行动和局部差异。阅读时应注意作者如何从地图、气候和贸易数据中构建历史解释，并思考结构条件与政治决策如何相互作用。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Fernand Braudel’s *The Mediterranean and the Mediterranean World in the Age of Philip II* is a major work of historical writing that shifts attention away from political events alone and toward the long-term structures that shape human life. Focusing on the sixteenth-century Mediterranean, Braudel examines geography, climate, mountains, coastlines, sea routes, ports, agriculture, population, trade, piracy, and imperial rivalry. The sea is treated not simply as a setting for battles but as a connected environment that influences economies, societies, and political possibilities.

Braudel distinguishes among different layers of historical time. Geography and climate change slowly; economic and social structures change over medium periods; wars, diplomacy, and the decisions of rulers unfold more rapidly. Philip II’s policies are therefore only one part of the story. They must be understood within the broader patterns of maritime exchange, regional resources, transport, and social organization that made certain actions possible and constrained others.

This approach, associated with the Annales school, transformed social and economic history by encouraging historians to study structures that may be overlooked in conventional narratives. It also has limitations. A strong focus on long-term forces can make individual agency, local variation, and sudden political choices appear less important than they sometimes are. Readers should examine how Braudel uses maps, climate information, economic records, and regional comparison to construct his explanation.

The book is valuable for studying the Mediterranean, early modern trade, environmental history, imperial competition, and the concept of longue durée. It teaches readers to connect events to the physical and social conditions that surround them, while also asking when those structures change and when human decisions can redirect them.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解布罗代尔的长时段历史方法。
- 分析地理、气候和贸易网络对政治的长期影响。
- 认识地中海作为跨区域经济与文化空间。
- 比较结构性条件与个人政治决策。
- 学习使用地图、经济数据和环境材料进行历史分析。
