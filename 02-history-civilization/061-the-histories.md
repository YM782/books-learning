# 061 · The Histories

- **作者：** Herodotus
- **首次出版/成书：** c. 430 BCE
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：西方史学传统的奠基文本之一，具有方法论和古代史价值。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《历史》是古希腊作家希罗多德对希波战争及其前因的广泛调查，内容从波斯帝国的扩张、各地民族风俗写到希腊城邦之间的联盟和冲突。希罗多德不仅记录战役和统治者，也收集地理、宗教、习俗、传说和旅行见闻，试图解释波斯与希腊为何走向战争。作品包含大量不同来源的故事，其中有些后来被证实，有些则存在夸张、传闻或事实争议。希罗多德有时会说明自己听到了什么、相信什么，以及哪些说法无法确认，因此这部作品既是历史叙述，也是早期调查方法的记录。阅读时不应把每一则故事都当作可靠事实，而应关注作者如何比较不同民族的说法、如何解释因果，以及如何区分观察、传闻和推测。作品的重要价值在于它尝试把战争放进更广阔的地理和文化背景中，体现历史写作从单纯歌颂英雄向调查与解释发展的早期形态。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Herodotus’s *The Histories* is an extensive inquiry into the causes and course of the conflicts between the Greek city-states and the Persian Empire in the fifth century BCE. The work begins with stories about earlier rulers and the expansion of Persian power, then moves through geography, customs, religious practices, political rivalries, and the major campaigns of the Greco-Persian Wars. Herodotus does not limit his account to battles and generals. He also records what he has heard about different peoples, their environments, institutions, and traditions, seeking to explain how a large international conflict became possible.

A distinctive feature of the work is its mixture of observation, testimony, legend, and speculation. Herodotus sometimes identifies competing versions of an event and comments on which account he finds more convincing. At other times, he preserves stories that later readers may regard as unreliable or impossible to verify. This does not make the book useless as history; rather, it gives readers an opportunity to study how evidence was collected and evaluated in an early form of historical inquiry.

The narrative explores the rise of imperial power, the limits of rulers, the role of geography, the unpredictability of war, and the relationship between freedom and political domination. It also reflects the cultural assumptions of a Greek author writing about societies beyond his own. Readers should therefore distinguish Herodotus’s observations from the perspectives and prejudices of his time, and compare important claims with archaeological evidence and other ancient sources.

*The Histories* is valuable for studying the origins of historical writing, the Greco-Persian Wars, ancient geography, cultural comparison, and the problem of evidence. Its enduring lesson is methodological: historical understanding requires asking who reported an event, what they could know, and how later interpretation may change its meaning.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解希波战争及波斯帝国扩张的历史背景。
- 学习区分观察、证词、传闻和推测。
- 分析古代历史作者的文化立场与局限。
- 认识地理、制度和文化如何影响战争。
- 掌握交叉验证史料的基本思路。
