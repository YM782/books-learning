# 078 · The Diary of a Young Girl

- **作者：** Anne Frank
- **首次出版/成书：** 1947 (first Dutch edition)
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：大屠杀历史中最重要的个人证词之一，具有世界范围的教育意义。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《安妮日记》记录犹太少女安妮·弗兰克在纳粹占领荷兰期间，与家人和其他人躲藏在阿姆斯特丹秘密密室中的生活。日记从1942年开始，写到1944年被捕前，内容包括狭小空间中的日常冲突、恐惧、学习、青春期情感、家庭关系和对未来的希望。安妮不仅记录战争和迫害，也反思自己如何成长、如何与父母相处，以及希望成为怎样的人。日记因此既是个人成长文献，也是纳粹大屠杀期间普通受害者生活的珍贵证词。1944年秘密密室成员被捕后，安妮最终死于贝尔根-贝尔森集中营。父亲奥托·弗兰克幸存并推动日记出版。不同版本在删改和编辑方面存在差异，后来的批判版和完整版本提供了更丰富的文本背景。阅读时应尊重其历史证词性质，避免把安妮仅仅塑造成抽象的“希望象征”，而忽视她作为具体少女的思想、矛盾和写作能力。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

*The Diary of a Young Girl* records the experiences of Anne Frank, a Jewish teenager who went into hiding with her family and several other people in a concealed annex in Amsterdam during the Nazi occupation of the Netherlands. Anne began writing in 1942 and continued until the group was discovered and arrested in 1944. Her diary describes the practical difficulties of living in a confined space, tensions among those in hiding, fear of discovery, family relationships, schoolwork, friendships, and the emotional changes of adolescence.

The diary is not only a record of persecution. Anne reflects on her personality, her relationship with her parents, her hopes for the future, and her ambition to become a writer. Her voice combines humor, criticism, uncertainty, and a growing awareness of the world beyond the annex. This makes the diary both a personal coming-of-age document and an important testimony about the lives of people hiding from Nazi persecution.

After the arrest, Anne and the others were deported. Anne died at Bergen-Belsen concentration camp in 1945. Her father, Otto Frank, survived and later worked to publish her writings. The first Dutch edition appeared in 1947. Different editions have included editorial changes and material that was initially omitted, so readers should pay attention to which version they use. Critical and expanded editions provide a fuller understanding of the manuscript and its publication history.

The diary should be read with respect for its historical status and for Anne as an individual writer, not only as a symbol of hope. It is valuable for studying the Holocaust, antisemitism, occupation, family life under persecution, adolescence, and the power of personal testimony. It also demonstrates why diaries and letters are essential historical sources, while reminding readers that one person’s account cannot represent every victim’s experience.

## 核心学习价值

- 认识纳粹占领荷兰与大屠杀历史。
- 理解个人日记如何成为重要历史证词。
- 关注躲藏生活中的家庭关系和心理压力。
- 认识不同版本的编辑和出版历史。
- 尊重受害者个体经验，避免把真实人物简化为象征。
