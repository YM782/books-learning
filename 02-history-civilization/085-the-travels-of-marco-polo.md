# 085 · The Travels of Marco Polo

- **作者：** Marco Polo and Rustichello da Pisa
- **首次出版/成书：** c. 1300
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：中世纪跨文化旅行叙事的重要文本，对欧洲地理想象影响深远。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《马可·波罗游记》记录威尼斯商人马可·波罗关于亚洲旅行和蒙古帝国的见闻。文本通常被认为是在马可·波罗与鲁斯蒂谦诺·达·比萨相遇后，以口述和合著方式形成，内容涉及中亚、波斯、印度洋、元朝中国及其城市、贸易、行政和风俗。作品向欧洲读者介绍了远距离贸易、纸币、邮驿制度、城市规模和不同宗教文化，扩大了中世纪欧洲对亚洲的地理想象。但文本的具体路线、细节真实性和作者贡献长期存在争论，有些描述可能来自他人报告、旅行者传闻或文学加工。阅读时应区分作者亲历、转述和后世版本增补，不能把所有内容当作现代意义上的精确旅行日志。它的历史价值不仅在于提供旅行信息，也在于展示跨文化知识如何通过商人、翻译、抄写者和出版网络传播。


 文本在欧洲传播后不断被翻译、删改和重新解释，因此它既是关于旅行的记录，也是关于中世纪知识传播和跨文化想象的历史材料。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

*The Travels of Marco Polo* presents accounts of Asia associated with the Venetian merchant Marco Polo. The text is generally understood to have taken shape around 1300, when Marco Polo’s experiences were written down with the assistance of Rustichello da Pisa, a writer he encountered while imprisoned in Genoa. The narrative describes regions of Central Asia, Persia, the Indian Ocean world, and the Mongol-ruled territories of China. It includes observations about cities, trade, administration, religious practices, transport, paper money, and the court of Kublai Khan.

For medieval European readers, the work offered an expansive view of places and institutions that were often known only through indirect reports. Its descriptions contributed to geographical imagination and later European interest in Asian trade and travel. The book is not simply a personal diary. It combines reported information, remembered experience, literary conventions, and material that may have come from other travelers or informants.

Scholars have debated the accuracy of particular passages, the precise routes Marco Polo followed, and the extent to which every description reflects his direct observation. Different manuscript traditions also complicate the text’s history. Readers should therefore distinguish between firsthand testimony, secondhand information, and later editorial or narrative additions. It would be misleading to treat the work as a modern, fully verified travel record.

Its historical value lies partly in showing how knowledge moved across cultures through merchants, interpreters, writers, and manuscript networks. *The Travels of Marco Polo* is useful for studying medieval Eurasian trade, the Mongol Empire, cross-cultural exchange, travel writing, and the formation of geographical knowledge. It also teaches readers to evaluate travel accounts by asking what the author could have seen, what was reported by others, and how the text changed during transmission.


 The text was translated, edited, and reinterpreted as it circulated in Europe. It is therefore not only a travel narrative but also evidence for the movement of knowledge and the formation of cross-cultural images in the medieval world.

## 核心学习价值

- 认识蒙古帝国时期欧亚贸易与交通网络。
- 了解中世纪欧洲对亚洲城市、行政和商品的认识。
- 区分亲历、转述和文本后期增补。
- 研究跨文化知识通过商人和抄写网络传播。
- 培养对旅行记和历史见闻的证据判断能力。
