# 077 · The Warmth of Other Suns

- **作者：** Isabel Wilkerson
- **首次出版/成书：** 2010
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：2010年美国国家图书评论奖非虚构类获奖作品，现代美国社会史的重要著作。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

伊莎贝尔·威尔克森的《他方太阳的温暖》讲述美国“大迁徙”时期数百万非裔美国人从南方前往北方和西部城市的历史。作者以三位真实人物的生命经历为主线，展现他们为何离开种族隔离、暴力和有限经济机会的南方，以及在芝加哥、纽约和洛杉矶等城市重新建立生活时遇到的住房歧视、就业限制和文化冲突。作品把个人家庭故事与人口数据、新闻材料和历史研究结合，说明迁徙不是单纯的地理移动，而是对安全、尊严、教育和经济机会的追寻。迁徙改变了美国城市政治、音乐、劳动市场和文化生活，也暴露出北方社会并非没有种族歧视。作者采用叙事非虚构写法，让宏观历史通过人物选择和代际记忆呈现。阅读时应注意三位主人公的经历不能代表所有迁徙者，但能帮助读者理解大规模社会变化如何进入普通人的生活。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Isabel Wilkerson’s *The Warmth of Other Suns* tells the story of the Great Migration, the movement of millions of African Americans from the American South to cities in the North and West during the twentieth century. Wilkerson follows three individuals whose lives illuminate different stages and routes of migration. Their decisions to leave are shaped by racial segregation, violence, restricted economic opportunity, and the desire for safety, dignity, education, and a better future for their families.

The book traces their experiences in cities such as Chicago, New York, and Los Angeles. Migration creates new opportunities, but it does not eliminate discrimination. Housing restrictions, unequal employment, racial hostility, and cultural adjustment continue to shape the lives of migrants. The movement also transforms American cities, politics, labor markets, music, and popular culture. By connecting individual stories with demographic research, newspaper accounts, and historical scholarship, Wilkerson shows how a large social transformation can be understood through the choices and memories of ordinary people.

The narrative nonfiction approach makes the history emotionally accessible, but the three lives at its center cannot represent every migrant’s experience. The Great Migration involved many regions, generations, occupations, and personal motivations. Readers should therefore use the book as a powerful set of case studies and place it alongside broader demographic and regional research.

*The Warmth of Other Suns* is valuable for studying internal migration, racial inequality, urban history, labor, family memory, and the pursuit of citizenship and opportunity. It helps readers understand that migration is not only movement across a map; it is also a response to political structures and a force that reshapes the places people leave and the places where they arrive.


 The stories also show that migration reshapes both the destination and the place left behind. Family networks, remittances, cultural practices, and political participation connect regions across distance. The Great Migration should therefore be understood as a national transformation rather than as a series of isolated individual departures.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解美国大迁徙的历史规模、路线和社会原因。
- 分析种族隔离、就业与住房歧视。
- 认识迁徙如何改变城市政治和文化。
- 学习叙事非虚构如何结合个人故事与人口研究。
- 区分个案经历与整体人口历史。
