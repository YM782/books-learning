# 095 · The Great Game

- **作者：** Peter Hopkirk
- **首次出版/成书：** 1990
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：中亚近代史和英俄帝国竞争的通俗历史经典。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

彼得·霍普柯克的《大博弈》讲述十九世纪英国与俄罗斯帝国在中亚地区的战略竞争。英国担心俄罗斯南下威胁英属印度，俄罗斯则试图扩大在中亚的影响力；双方通过外交使团、情报活动、地理测绘、军事远征和地方联盟争夺影响范围。作品描写探险家、军官、间谍和地方统治者在阿富汗、布哈拉、希瓦和其他地区的行动，展现帝国竞争如何改变中亚政治。作者采用故事性很强的叙述，突出秘密行动和个人冒险，但这种写法也容易把复杂地区历史集中到英俄两大帝国的视角。中亚社会并非被动舞台，地方政权和居民也会利用列强竞争维护自身利益。阅读时应结合阿富汗、波斯、中亚汗国和当地社会的历史研究，避免把“大博弈”当成解释整个地区历史的唯一框架。


地方统治者和社区对外部压力的回应并不一致，有时合作，有时抵抗，也可能在不同帝国之间寻求空间。理解这些选择，才能还原中亚政治的复杂性。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Peter Hopkirk’s *The Great Game* examines the nineteenth-century strategic rivalry between the British and Russian empires in Central Asia. British officials feared that Russian expansion southward might threaten the security of British India, while Russian leaders sought greater influence across the region. The competition involved diplomatic missions, intelligence gathering, exploration, mapping, military expeditions, and alliances with local rulers.

Hopkirk follows officers, travelers, spies, and political agents as they move through Afghanistan, Bukhara, Khiva, Persia, and other parts of Central Asia. Their journeys reveal how imperial rivalry shaped diplomacy and how incomplete geographical knowledge could influence strategic decisions. The book’s narrative style is vivid and emphasizes adventure, secrecy, and individual personalities, making a complex geopolitical history accessible to general readers.

However, the phrase “Great Game” can make the region appear to be merely a chessboard on which Britain and Russia moved their pieces. Central Asian rulers, merchants, communities, and political institutions had their own interests and strategies. They sometimes used imperial competition to strengthen local authority, resist outside pressure, or negotiate more favorable arrangements. A fuller understanding therefore requires regional histories that give greater attention to local agency and social change.

*The Great Game* is valuable for studying imperial strategy, intelligence, exploration, Afghanistan, and the history of Central Asia. Readers should treat it as an engaging account of Anglo-Russian rivalry while supplementing it with scholarship on the societies affected by that rivalry. This approach helps avoid reducing a diverse region to a single story about two competing empires.


 Local rulers and communities responded to outside pressure in different ways: some cooperated, some resisted, and others negotiated between competing empires. Examining these choices restores the political complexity of Central Asia and prevents the region from disappearing behind an imperial rivalry.


 The imperial rivalry is therefore best understood as a relationship among competing powers and local political actors, not as a two-sided contest conducted over an empty territory.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解十九世纪英俄帝国在中亚的战略竞争。
- 分析情报、测绘、外交和地方联盟的作用。
- 认识阿富汗与中亚地区的地缘政治位置。
- 避免把中亚社会简化成帝国竞争的被动舞台。
- 结合地方史研究补充英俄中心叙事。
