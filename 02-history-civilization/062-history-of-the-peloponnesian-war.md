# 062 · History of the Peloponnesian War

- **作者：** Thucydides
- **首次出版/成书：** 5th century BCE
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：古代战争史和政治分析的经典文本，对国际关系思想影响深远。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

修昔底德的《伯罗奔尼撒战争史》记录公元前431年至404年雅典与斯巴达及其盟友之间的长期战争。作者曾亲历战争并担任雅典将领，后来因军事失利被流放，得以观察冲突的多个阶段。作品分析战争起因、联盟体系、国内政治、瘟疫、围城、远征和领导者决策，尤其重视恐惧、荣誉和利益如何推动国家行动。著名的伯里克利葬礼演说、米洛斯对话和西西里远征叙事，展示民主政治、帝国扩张和现实主义权力逻辑之间的张力。修昔底德强调自己追求准确记录，但演说部分是根据情境重构，而非逐字录音；读者需要认识古代史料的证据边界。作品没有把雅典或斯巴达描写成纯粹正义的一方，而是呈现战争如何改变制度、公共道德和人的判断。它常被用于国际关系研究，但不能把古希腊城邦简单等同于现代国家。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Thucydides’ *History of the Peloponnesian War* examines the long conflict between Athens and Sparta and their respective allies from 431 to 404 BCE. Thucydides had direct experience of the war as an Athenian general. After a military failure led to his exile, he was able to observe events from a wider perspective. He seeks to explain not only the battles but also the political, economic, and psychological forces that shaped the conflict.

The work discusses alliances, military strategy, plague, civil strife, imperial expansion, public debate, and the decisions of political leaders. Its famous episodes include Pericles’ funeral speech, the debate over the fate of Mytilene, the Melian Dialogue, and the disastrous Athenian expedition to Sicily. These passages explore the tension between democratic ideals, imperial ambition, fear, honor, and material interest. Thucydides is especially interested in how crisis changes political language and moral judgment. Under pressure, communities may abandon principles they once claimed to defend.

The author presents himself as a careful investigator who wants to preserve an account useful for understanding future conflicts. However, the speeches in the work are reconstructed according to what Thucydides believed was appropriate to each situation; they are not verbatim transcripts. Readers should therefore distinguish his method of historical explanation from modern documentary standards. His account also reflects the political assumptions of an ancient Greek observer and should be compared with other evidence where possible.

The book is important for the development of analytical history and remains influential in international relations and political theory. It is valuable for studying the causes of war, alliance politics, democratic decision-making, strategic calculation, and the moral consequences of power. It also teaches readers to examine how leaders justify decisions during emergencies.

## 核心学习价值

- 分析战争的结构性原因、联盟体系和决策过程。
- 理解恐惧、荣誉和利益对国家行为的影响。
- 研究民主政治、帝国扩张和公共辩论。
- 认识古代演说重构与现代史料标准的差异。
- 学习从战争史中提炼政治分析问题。
