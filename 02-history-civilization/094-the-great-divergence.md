# 094 · The Great Divergence

- **作者：** Kenneth Pomeranz
- **首次出版/成书：** 2000
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：全球经济史研究的重要转折性著作，引发关于欧洲工业化起源的持续讨论。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

肯尼斯·波默兰兹的《大分流》重新讨论为什么十八世纪以前中国部分地区与西欧一些地区在经济发展水平上存在相似之处，而工业革命后欧洲逐渐取得持续增长优势。作者重点比较中国长江三角洲与英国等地区，分析土地、能源、劳动力、消费市场和全球贸易的关系。他强调英国能够利用煤炭资源，并从美洲殖民地获得土地密集型产品和资源，从而缓解生态约束；而中国面临的人口、土地和能源压力则有所不同。作品挑战把欧洲崛起完全归因于文化优越或制度先天先进的解释，也强调殖民体系和全球资源流动的作用。学界对比较地区的选择、煤炭和殖民资源的相对重要性，以及中国经济状况的具体判断存在持续争论。阅读时应把“大分流”视为一场比较经济史辩论，而非已经定论的单一原因解释。


这一争论也说明，解释工业革命不能只看某个国家内部的发明或文化特征，还需要研究能源、土地、殖民关系和国际市场如何共同改变发展条件。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Kenneth Pomeranz’s *The Great Divergence* revisits the question of why parts of Western Europe, especially Britain, began to experience sustained industrial growth while major regions of China did not follow the same path. Pomeranz compares economically developed regions such as the Yangzi Delta with parts of Western Europe and argues that before the late eighteenth century, some similarities in living standards, markets, and commercial development were greater than older Eurocentric narratives had assumed.

The book focuses on ecological constraints, land, energy, labor, consumption, and global trade. Pomeranz argues that Britain’s access to coal helped overcome limits on energy supply, while resources from the Americas—including land-intensive products—relieved pressures that might otherwise have constrained industrial expansion. He therefore places colonial connections and global resource flows at the center of the explanation, rather than treating European industrialization as the inevitable result of unique cultural or institutional superiority.

The argument has generated extensive debate. Scholars have questioned the choice of comparison regions, the reliability and comparability of historical data, the relative importance of coal and colonial resources, and the economic conditions of different Chinese regions. Later research has refined many aspects of the debate. Readers should therefore approach the book as a major intervention in comparative economic history, not as a final consensus explanation.

*The Great Divergence* is valuable for studying industrialization, global trade, ecological limits, colonialism, and comparative economic development. It encourages readers to ask how resource access and international power relations shape economic change, and why explanations of modern prosperity must consider both domestic institutions and global connections.


 The debate demonstrates why industrialization cannot be explained only through inventions or cultural characteristics within one country. Energy, land, colonial relationships, labor, and international markets interacted to change the conditions under which economic growth became possible.


 This comparative method also invites debate about what counts as a fair comparison: historians must explain why particular regions, periods, and indicators are chosen before drawing conclusions about long-term economic performance.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解工业革命前中欧经济比较的核心争论。
- 分析煤炭、土地、能源和殖民资源的作用。
- 认识全球贸易与欧洲工业化之间的联系。
- 评估比较地区、数据和因果解释的局限。
- 建立比较经济史和全球史的分析框架。
