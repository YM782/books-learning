# 079 · The Gulag Archipelago

- **作者：** Aleksandr Solzhenitsyn
- **首次出版/成书：** 1973–1975 (Russian publication abroad)
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：揭露苏联政治镇压的重要纪实与证词作品，具有重大历史影响。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

索尔仁尼琴的《古拉格群岛》通过个人经历、幸存者证词、书信和历史材料，调查苏联强制劳动营体系及其背后的逮捕、审讯、判刑、运输和营地生活。作者把遍布苏联的劳改营比作“群岛”，强调它并非少数孤立事件，而是与国家制度和社会恐惧相连的庞大体系。作品记录政治清洗、任意拘押、酷刑、劳动剥削和家庭破裂，也讨论普通人在极端环境中的顺从、抵抗、互助与道德选择。它兼具纪实、回忆录和文学叙事特点，写作立场鲜明，旨在揭露国家暴力。作品出版时苏联档案尚未全面开放，因此它是理解受害者经验和当时社会恐惧的重要文本，却不能替代后来基于档案的专业研究。阅读时应把作者证词、他人证言、统计估算和文学评论区分开来，并与后苏联时期档案研究相互参照。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Aleksandr Solzhenitsyn’s *The Gulag Archipelago* investigates the Soviet system of forced labor camps through the author’s own experience, testimony from other prisoners, letters, historical documents, and literary reflection. Solzhenitsyn uses the image of an archipelago to describe a network of camps spread across the Soviet Union. The system was not presented as a collection of isolated abuses but as an institutional structure connected to arrest, interrogation, sentencing, transportation, forced labor, and the climate of fear in society.

The work describes political purges, arbitrary imprisonment, coercive interrogation, exploitation, family separation, and the daily conditions of camp life. It also examines how people respond to extreme pressure: some comply, some resist, some help one another, and others struggle to preserve a sense of moral responsibility. The book combines memoir, documentary investigation, historical argument, and literary storytelling. Its moral and political purpose is explicit: to expose state violence and restore the experiences of people whose suffering had been hidden.

When the work was published abroad in the 1970s, Soviet archives were not fully accessible to independent researchers. It therefore became a major source for understanding the experience and memory of repression, but it should not be treated as a complete statistical or archival history of the Gulag. Later research based on newly available documents has expanded knowledge of the camp system, its changing scale, and its regional variations. Readers should distinguish the author’s personal testimony, the accounts of other survivors, numerical estimates, and literary interpretation.

*The Gulag Archipelago* is valuable for studying totalitarian institutions, political repression, forced labor, testimony, and moral choices under coercion. It should be read alongside archival scholarship and other survivor accounts to understand both the human experience and the historical structure of the system.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解苏联古拉格体系的制度结构与运作过程。
- 认识逮捕、审讯、强迫劳动和社会恐惧。
- 学习区分个人证词、他人证言和统计估算。
- 讨论极端压迫下的顺从、抵抗与道德选择。
- 结合后苏联档案研究修正和补充早期叙述。
