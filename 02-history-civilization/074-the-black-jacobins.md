# 074 · The Black Jacobins

- **作者：** C. L. R. James
- **首次出版/成书：** 1938
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：反殖民史学和黑人政治思想的重要经典。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

C. L. R. 詹姆斯的《黑色雅各宾派》讲述1791年至1804年海地革命，重点描写被奴役的非洲裔人民如何推翻法国殖民统治和奴隶制，建立独立国家。作品以杜桑·卢维杜尔为核心人物，同时呈现奴隶、自由有色人种、法国革命者、西班牙和英国势力之间的复杂冲突。詹姆斯把海地革命放在法国大革命和大西洋世界的历史中，说明自由、平等和公民权利的普遍口号如何在殖民地受到现实检验。革命并非单一领袖创造，而是由被奴役者的集体行动、军事组织、政治谈判和国际局势共同推动。作者的马克思主义和反殖民立场十分鲜明，后来的研究对人物、阶级和国际关系的解释有所补充，但这部作品仍是理解奴隶制、革命主体性和黑人政治思想的重要经典。阅读时应关注被奴役者如何成为历史行动者，而不是只把他们视为欧洲革命的被动受益者。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

C. L. R. James’s *The Black Jacobins* is a history of the Haitian Revolution, the struggle that began in 1791 and led to the abolition of slavery in Saint-Domingue and the creation of independent Haiti in 1804. James places the revolution within the wider history of the French Revolution and the Atlantic world. He follows enslaved Africans, free people of color, French officials, and foreign powers as they fought over slavery, citizenship, political authority, and control of the colony.

Toussaint Louverture is a central figure, but James does not present the revolution as the achievement of one exceptional leader. He emphasizes the collective action of enslaved people, the organization of military resistance, the changing political situation in France, and the strategic pressures created by Spain and Britain. The revolutionary language of liberty and equality was tested in a colonial society where slavery remained the foundation of wealth and power. The Haitian Revolution exposed the contradiction between universal rights and the continued enslavement of millions.

James writes from a Marxist and anti-colonial perspective. His interpretation has been highly influential in Black political thought and in the study of slavery and revolution. Later scholarship has expanded the evidence and offered additional interpretations of class, leadership, religion, and international diplomacy. Readers should therefore use the book as a foundational work while comparing it with newer research and primary sources.

*The Black Jacobins* is valuable for understanding the Haitian Revolution, the history of slavery, the limits of Enlightenment universalism, and the role of oppressed people as makers of history. It challenges narratives that treat colonial populations as passive recipients of freedom granted by European powers.


 The revolution also transformed the political imagination of enslaved people and free communities throughout the Atlantic world. News of events in Saint-Domingue influenced debates about abolition and citizenship elsewhere, while foreign governments attempted to contain the example. The history therefore connects local resistance to a wider struggle over the meaning of universal rights.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解海地革命、奴隶制废除与独立建国。
- 分析法国大革命的普遍权利主张与殖民现实矛盾。
- 认识被奴役者作为历史行动者的主体性。
- 理解革命中的军事、外交和国际政治因素。
- 比较反殖民史学与后续研究对革命的解释。
