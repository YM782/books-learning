# 067 · The Silk Roads

- **作者：** Peter Frankopan
- **首次出版/成书：** 2015
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：全球史领域具有广泛影响的综合性著作，推动欧亚互联史讨论。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

彼得·弗兰科潘的《丝绸之路》试图把世界历史的观察中心从西欧转向欧亚大陆的中部和连接东西方的交通网络。作品从古代波斯、丝绸贸易和宗教传播写起，追踪帝国、商人、军队、传教士和知识如何沿着陆路与海路流动。作者强调中亚、西亚和印度洋地区不仅是文明之间的通道，也是政治、经济和文化创造的重要中心。书中涉及丝绸、香料、宗教、蒙古扩张、殖民竞争、石油和现代地缘政治，展示全球历史并非单一欧洲故事。然而，“丝绸之路”并不是一条固定道路，历史上存在多条路线和不断变化的网络；作者的宏观叙事也受到学者对选择性材料和因果解释的讨论。阅读时应把它作为一种重新安排历史视角的综合叙事，而不是把所有地区变化都归因于贸易路线。其价值在于促使读者关注跨区域联系、资源流动和欧亚大陆在全球史中的重要性。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Peter Frankopan’s *The Silk Roads* offers a broad reinterpretation of world history by shifting attention away from a Europe-centered narrative and toward the networks connecting Central Asia, the Middle East, South Asia, and the wider Eurasian world. Beginning with ancient empires and trade routes, Frankopan follows the movement of goods, religions, technologies, armies, and ideas across land and sea. He argues that regions often treated as margins of world history were in fact central to the development of political and economic power.

The book ranges across the Persian empires, the spread of Buddhism, Christianity, and Islam, the Mongol expansion, European colonial competition, the discovery and exploitation of oil, and modern geopolitical rivalries. It presents the Silk Roads not as a single road but as a changing network of connections through which merchants, diplomats, soldiers, pilgrims, and scholars moved. Trade created wealth, but it also carried disease, conflict, cultural exchange, and political transformation.

Frankopan’s approach challenges readers to consider how global history looks when the center of attention is placed between Europe and Asia rather than in Western Europe alone. At the same time, the book’s wide scope requires careful reading. The phrase “Silk Roads” can become too broad if it is treated as a single explanation for every major historical development. Scholars have debated the selection of evidence and the strength of some causal claims. Readers should therefore compare its sweeping narrative with more specialized regional studies.

*The Silk Roads* is valuable for studying global history, trade networks, cultural exchange, empire, energy politics, and the geography of power. Its main contribution is to encourage a connected view of the past, in which regions influence one another through movement, competition, and exchange rather than developing in isolation.

## 核心学习价值

- 建立以欧亚大陆为中心的全球史视角。
- 理解贸易网络如何传播商品、宗教和技术。
- 认识中亚、西亚和印度洋地区的历史作用。
- 分析资源、交通和地缘政治之间的联系。
- 批判性评估宏大历史叙事的证据与因果解释。
