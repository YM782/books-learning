# 080 · A People's History of the United States

- **作者：** Howard Zinn
- **首次出版/成书：** 1980
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：影响广泛的自下而上美国社会史著作，引发关于历史叙述视角的讨论。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

霍华德·津恩的《美国人民史》尝试从原住民、被奴役者、工人、移民、女性和反战者等群体的角度重写美国历史。作者从欧洲殖民者抵达北美、原住民土地被占、奴隶制扩张和独立战争写起，追踪工业化、劳工运动、种族斗争、对外战争和社会改革。作品强调国家历史不能只由总统、将军和富有精英的决策构成，还必须关注普通人如何承受政策后果、组织抗争并争取权利。津恩的写作立场明确，意在纠正传统国家叙事中被忽略的暴力和不平等；但批评者指出，作品有时选择性使用材料、弱化制度复杂性或过度突出冲突。阅读时应把它作为一种有意识的“自下而上”历史视角，与其他政治史、经济史和原住民研究并读，而不是将其视为唯一版本。它的学习价值在于提醒读者追问谁的声音被记录、谁的经验被遗漏，以及历史叙事如何受到权力关系影响。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Howard Zinn’s *A People’s History of the United States* retells American history from the perspectives of groups often marginalized in conventional national narratives, including Indigenous peoples, enslaved people, workers, immigrants, women, and antiwar activists. Beginning with European colonization and the dispossession of Native communities, Zinn follows the development of slavery, the American Revolution, industrialization, labor struggles, racial conflict, overseas wars, and social movements.

The book argues that national history should not be told only through presidents, generals, wealthy leaders, and official institutions. It should also examine how ordinary people experienced public decisions, organized resistance, and fought for rights. Zinn emphasizes the violence and inequality that accompanied the expansion of the United States, including the seizure of Indigenous land, the exploitation of labor, racial discrimination, and the costs of military intervention. His narrative deliberately challenges patriotic accounts that present national development as a steady and unified story of progress.

Zinn’s approach is influential but also contested. Critics have argued that the book sometimes selects evidence to support a particular political interpretation, gives less attention to institutional complexity, or emphasizes conflict at the expense of other historical forces. Readers should therefore treat it as a clearly positioned interpretation rather than as a neutral or comprehensive account. A strong reading practice is to compare its claims with other political, economic, regional, Indigenous, and diplomatic histories.

*A People’s History of the United States* is valuable for studying social movements, labor history, race, colonialism, and the politics of historical memory. Its most important methodological lesson is to ask whose experiences are included in a historical narrative, whose are missing, and how power influences the preservation and interpretation of evidence.


 The testimony also raises difficult questions about how to record mass suffering. Personal accounts preserve experiences that official documents may omit, while archival research can test dates, institutional structures, and numerical claims. Reading these forms together helps readers respect individual testimony without confusing a literary narrative with a complete statistical history.

## 核心学习价值

- 从普通人和边缘群体角度重新观察美国历史。
- 认识原住民、奴隶、工人和女性的历史经验。
- 分析社会运动如何争取权利和改变制度。
- 辨认作者的政治立场、材料选择和解释局限。
- 与其他史学流派交叉阅读，避免单一叙事。
