# 089 · The Great War and Modern Memory

- **作者：** Paul Fussell
- **首次出版/成书：** 1975
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：1976年美国国家图书奖获奖作品，战争文化史与文学研究经典。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

保罗·福塞尔的《大战与现代记忆》研究第一次世界大战如何改变英国文学、语言和集体记忆。作者重点分析战壕生活、士兵经验、讽刺、黑色幽默和战争意象，讨论诗歌、小说、回忆录和流行表达如何记录工业化战争带来的荒诞与创伤。福塞尔认为，前线经验与战前社会对荣誉、进步和英雄主义的想象发生剧烈冲突，形成一种以反讽和怀疑为特征的现代战争文化。作品把文学文本作为理解历史经验的证据，展示语言如何塑造人们对战争的记忆。不过，它主要关注英国男性士兵和英语文学材料，其他国家、女性、殖民地军人和平民经验相对有限；后来的研究也对其“现代记忆”概括提出补充。阅读时应把它视为文学文化史的重要研究，而非第一次世界大战的完整社会史。


 文学记忆与军事档案能够互相补充，却不能彼此替代。把不同来源放在一起阅读，有助于理解战争经验如何在当时被表达，又如何在后世被重新塑造。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Paul Fussell’s *The Great War and Modern Memory* examines how the First World War transformed British literature, language, and collective memory. Rather than presenting a conventional military history, Fussell studies the cultural forms through which soldiers and writers represented trench warfare, industrialized violence, fear, irony, and loss. He analyzes poetry, novels, memoirs, popular expressions, and recurring images to show how the experience of the front challenged prewar ideals of honor, progress, heroism, and patriotic certainty.

A central argument is that the contrast between public language and the realities of war produced a lasting culture of irony. Soldiers encountered conditions that made traditional heroic language seem inadequate or absurd. Their writing often used dark humor, understatement, parody, and symbolic images to communicate experiences that could not easily be expressed through conventional narratives. Fussell treats literary texts as historical evidence, arguing that language does not merely describe memory; it helps shape what later generations understand about the war.

The book is influential, but its scope is selective. It concentrates primarily on British male soldiers and English-language literature, giving less attention to women, colonial troops, civilians, and the experiences of other national armies. Later scholars have expanded the study of war memory to include different languages, regions, and social groups. Readers should therefore treat Fussell’s account as a major study of British literary culture rather than as a complete history of all wartime experience.

*The Great War and Modern Memory* is valuable for studying war literature, cultural memory, irony, trauma, and the relationship between language and historical experience. It shows how literature can reveal dimensions of war that military records alone may not capture, while reminding readers to ask whose voices are represented and whose are missing.


 Literary memory and military records can illuminate one another, but neither can replace the other. Reading different kinds of evidence together helps explain how war was experienced at the time and reshaped in later cultural memory.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解一战战壕经验如何改变英国文学和公共语言。
- 分析讽刺、黑色幽默和反英雄叙事。
- 认识文学作品作为历史经验材料的价值。
- 评估以英国男性士兵为中心的研究范围局限。
- 讨论语言如何塑造战争记忆。
