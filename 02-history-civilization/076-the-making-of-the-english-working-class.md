# 076 · The Making of the English Working Class

- **作者：** E. P. Thompson
- **首次出版/成书：** 1963
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：新社会史和劳工史的经典著作，改变了阶级研究方法。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

E. P. 汤普森的《英国工人阶级的形成》研究十八世纪末至十九世纪初英国工人阶级如何在工业化和政治冲突中形成。作者反对把阶级仅仅看作经济统计中的位置，强调阶级意识是在共同经验、组织、文化和斗争中逐渐形成的。作品描写工匠、工厂工人、宗教异议者、激进改革者和早期工会如何回应机器化生产、工资变化、政治压迫和传统权利的消失。汤普森特别关注工人如何通过集会、出版物、互助组织和抗议表达自身利益。书名中的“形成”强调阶级不是自然存在的固定标签，而是历史行动和社会关系的结果。作者的叙述具有鲜明的社会主义史学立场，后来的研究对其代表性、性别和殖民维度作出补充，但其对普通劳动者能动性的重视改变了社会史写作。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

E. P. Thompson’s *The Making of the English Working Class* examines the formation of working-class identity in England from the late eighteenth century into the early nineteenth century. Thompson challenges the idea that class is simply a fixed economic category determined by occupation or income. Instead, he argues that class emerges through shared experiences, cultural practices, political organization, and collective struggle. People become conscious of common interests through the conditions they face and the institutions they build.

The book follows artisans, factory workers, religious dissenters, radical reformers, and early labor organizations as they respond to industrialization, changing wages, mechanized production, political repression, and the loss of older customary rights. Thompson pays close attention to the ways ordinary people expressed their interests through meetings, pamphlets, newspapers, mutual-aid societies, protests, and political movements. He shows that workers were not merely passive victims of economic change; they actively interpreted events and developed forms of collective identity.

The word “making” in the title is central. The working class was not created automatically by factories or machines. It developed through relationships and historical action, and its identity was shaped by both economic conditions and cultural traditions. Thompson’s approach reflects a socialist and Marxist historiographical perspective. Later scholars have added further attention to gender, empire, race, and the diversity of working-class experience, but the book remains influential for its insistence on historical agency from below.

*The Making of the English Working Class* is valuable for studying industrialization, labor history, class consciousness, popular politics, and social movements. It encourages readers to examine how groups form identities and institutions through common experience rather than treating social categories as timeless or inevitable.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解英国工业化早期的劳动与政治变化。
- 认识阶级意识如何通过共同经验和组织形成。
- 关注普通劳动者的历史主体性。
- 分析工会、出版物、集会和抗议的作用。
- 补充性别、种族和帝国维度，检验经典社会史框架。
