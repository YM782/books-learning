# 069 · Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind

- **作者：** Yuval Noah Harari
- **首次出版/成书：** 2011 (Hebrew); English translation 2014
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：全球畅销的通俗人类史著作，广泛影响公众对文明史的讨论。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

尤瓦尔·赫拉利的《人类简史》以宏观叙事回顾智人的演化和社会组织变化，主要分为认知革命、农业革命、人类社会的统一和科学革命几个阶段。作者强调语言、共同想象和大规模合作使智人能够建立宗教、法律、货币、国家和企业等制度。农业带来人口增长和定居生活，也可能增加劳动负担、疾病和社会等级；帝国、宗教和贸易网络则推动不同地区的整合。近代科学、资本主义和工业化改变了人类对自然与未来的控制能力，同时带来新的伦理和生态风险。作品善于用跨学科故事建立整体视野，但不少论点属于作者的综合解释或大胆假说，学界对某些细节、因果关系和过度概括提出过批评。阅读时应把它当作启发思考的通俗历史导论，而不是可以替代考古学、遗传学和专业历史研究的教材。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Yuval Noah Harari’s *Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind* offers a wide-ranging account of the development of Homo sapiens and the social systems humans have created. The book is organized around several major transformations: the Cognitive Revolution, the Agricultural Revolution, the unification of humankind through empires, religions, and trade, and the Scientific Revolution. Harari argues that language and shared stories allow large numbers of people to cooperate around institutions that exist because people collectively believe in them, including money, laws, states, religions, and corporations.

The book challenges the assumption that every major historical change has made human life easier. Agriculture increased food production and population size, but it also encouraged settled labor, hierarchy, disease, and dependence on crops. Empires and trade networks connected distant regions, sometimes spreading knowledge and prosperity while also enabling conquest and exploitation. The rise of modern science and capitalism expanded human power over nature, but it also created new risks involving inequality, ecology, and the meaning of progress.

Harari’s strength is his ability to connect topics across biology, anthropology, history, and economics in an accessible narrative. However, some of his broad explanations are debated by specialists. Archaeologists, historians, and anthropologists have challenged particular claims, questioned simplified causal links, and emphasized regional differences that a global overview may overlook. The book should therefore be read as an influential synthesis and a source of questions, not as a substitute for specialist research or a definitive account of every period.

*Sapiens* is valuable for building a broad historical framework, comparing different forms of social organization, and thinking critically about the institutions that shape modern life. Readers should note which statements are well-established evidence, which are interpretations, and which are speculative generalizations.

## 核心学习价值

- 建立人类演化与文明发展的宏观时间框架。
- 理解共同想象、制度和大规模合作的关系。
- 分析农业革命、帝国、宗教与科学革命。
- 辨别通俗历史叙事中的证据、解释和推测。
- 结合专业研究批判性评估作者的宏大论点。
