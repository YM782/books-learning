# 063 · The History of the Decline and Fall of the Roman Empire

- **作者：** Edward Gibbon
- **首次出版/成书：** 1776–1789
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：启蒙时代史学代表作，对现代历史写作产生深远影响。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

爱德华·吉本的《罗马帝国衰亡史》从公元二世纪罗马帝国的鼎盛时期写起，追踪西罗马帝国衰亡以及东罗马帝国延续至十五世纪的漫长历史。作品讨论皇帝更替、军队、财政、行政、宗教、民族迁徙和外交战争，试图解释一个庞大帝国为何逐渐失去整合能力。吉本以宏大的叙事和讽刺性的文风批评政治腐败、军事失序和制度衰弱，也对基督教在罗马世界的作用提出了当时颇具争议的解释。现代研究已经修正了吉本的一些判断，例如将帝国转型简单理解为“衰落”，或过度强调单一因素；东罗马社会在西罗马灭亡后仍长期保持复杂的制度和文化活力。因此阅读时应把它看作十八世纪启蒙史学的重要作品，而不是当代罗马史研究的最终结论。它的价值在于展示历史学家如何构建长时段解释，并提醒读者检验作者的价值观、证据选择和因果假设。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Edward Gibbon’s *The History of the Decline and Fall of the Roman Empire* traces the history of the Roman world from the height of imperial power in the second century CE through the fall of the Western Roman Empire and the later history of the Eastern Roman Empire, which continued until the fifteenth century. Published in six volumes between 1776 and 1789, the work examines emperors, military institutions, taxation, administration, religion, diplomacy, migration, and warfare in an effort to explain how a vast political system changed over many centuries.

Gibbon’s narrative is famous for its scale, literary style, and critical attitude toward political and religious authority. He often attributes institutional weakness to corruption, military disorder, poor leadership, and the erosion of civic responsibility. His discussion of Christianity’s role in the transformation of Roman society was especially controversial in his own time. The book became a landmark of Enlightenment historiography because it attempted to explain historical change through human institutions and political causes rather than relying only on providential accounts.

Modern scholarship has revised many of Gibbon’s interpretations. The transition from Roman rule to post-Roman societies was uneven and regionally varied, and the Eastern Roman Empire remained politically and culturally significant for centuries after the western imperial government disappeared. The idea of a single, straightforward decline can therefore obscure adaptation, continuity, and transformation. Readers should treat Gibbon as an influential historian with a particular eighteenth-century perspective, not as the final authority on Roman history.

The work is valuable for studying long-term historical explanation, imperial administration, religious change, military institutions, and the writing of history itself. It encourages readers to ask whether large political transformations can be explained by a few causes or whether they emerge from interacting economic, social, cultural, and institutional pressures.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解罗马帝国从古典时期到中世纪的长期变迁。
- 分析军队、财政、行政和宗教制度的作用。
- 认识启蒙时代史学的写作方式与价值观。
- 比较“衰亡”与“转型”两种历史解释。
- 训练长时段历史因果分析。
