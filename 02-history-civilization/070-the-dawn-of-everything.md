# 070 · The Dawn of Everything

- **作者：** David Graeber and David Wengrow
- **首次出版/成书：** 2021
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：2021年出版后引发广泛讨论的跨学科历史与人类学著作。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《万物的黎明》由人类学家戴维·格雷伯和考古学家戴维·温格罗共同撰写，挑战人类社会必然从平等小群体逐步走向农业、城市、国家和等级制度的单线进化叙事。作者通过考古学、人类学和历史材料，展示古代社会曾采用多种政治组织形式，有些群体能够在季节之间改变治理方式，有些城市拥有复杂生活却未必由高度集权国家统治。作品还重新讨论启蒙思想与原住民政治批评之间的联系，指出现代欧洲关于自由和平等的观念并非只在欧洲内部产生。作者主张，人类社会过去具有比传统教科书叙述更大的制度实验空间。但这部书的论证范围很广，部分考古证据的解释和对其他学者观点的概括引发争议。阅读时应关注作者挑战的具体模型、所使用的案例和证据是否充分，而不是把它理解成所有古代社会都自由平等。其价值在于打开新的问题：国家、阶级和等级是否不可避免？人类是否曾经主动设计并改变自己的政治制度？

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

*The Dawn of Everything*, by anthropologist David Graeber and archaeologist David Wengrow, challenges familiar accounts of human social evolution. Many traditional narratives suggest that societies moved through a predictable sequence from small, egalitarian groups to agriculture, cities, states, and permanent hierarchies. The authors argue that archaeological and anthropological evidence reveals a much wider range of political experiments. Some communities organized themselves in different ways across seasons; some large settlements appear to have existed without the centralized authority often assumed to be necessary; and some societies deliberately changed their forms of governance.

The book also revisits the intellectual history of ideas about freedom and equality. Graeber and Wengrow suggest that Indigenous political thinkers and encounters between European and non-European societies influenced Enlightenment debates more than conventional accounts acknowledge. Their argument is that human beings have repeatedly reflected on political arrangements and have not simply been carried along by an inevitable process of increasing complexity.

The book’s ambition is substantial, and its interpretations have generated debate. Archaeological evidence is often incomplete, and specialists disagree about how particular settlements, artifacts, and social structures should be understood. Some reviewers and scholars have questioned whether the authors characterize earlier theories fairly or draw conclusions that go beyond the available evidence. Readers should therefore examine the examples and arguments rather than accepting the book as a new universal theory of prehistory.

Its central contribution is to widen the questions historians and readers ask. Are states and social hierarchies inevitable? How much freedom did people in earlier societies have to redesign their institutions? What forms of cooperation and authority are possible beyond the modern state? *The Dawn of Everything* is valuable for studying archaeology, anthropology, political imagination, social inequality, and the history of ideas. It encourages a more open and evidence-conscious view of humanity’s political past.

## 核心学习价值

- 批判社会从平等到国家等级的单线进化叙事。
- 认识考古学和人类学如何重建古代政治组织。
- 讨论国家、阶级和等级是否具有必然性。
- 理解原住民思想与启蒙政治观念的联系争论。
- 评估跨学科宏大论证的证据强度和局限。
