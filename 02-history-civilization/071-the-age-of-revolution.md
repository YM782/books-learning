# 071 · The Age of Revolution: Europe 1789–1848

- **作者：** Eric Hobsbawm
- **首次出版/成书：** 1962
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：霍布斯鲍姆现代世界史三部曲第一部，现代史研究的重要综合著作。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《革命的年代》研究1789年法国大革命到1848年欧洲革命之间的世界变化。霍布斯鲍姆把法国政治革命与英国工业革命并置，认为两者共同改变了现代国家、资本主义经济、阶级关系和政治思想。作品讨论封建制度衰退、资产阶级兴起、工人阶级形成、民族主义、自由主义、社会主义和工业城市的发展。作者并不只叙述法国和英国，也关注革命思想如何传播到欧洲其他地区及拉丁美洲。书中强调革命并非单一事件，而是政治制度、经济生产和社会结构同步变化的过程。作为马克思主义历史学家，霍布斯鲍姆对阶级和资本主义的分析具有鲜明理论立场，读者应将其与其他史学解释比较。它的价值在于建立从革命时代理解现代世界形成的框架，并帮助读者观察政治理念如何与经济转型相互作用。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Eric Hobsbawm’s *The Age of Revolution: Europe 1789–1848* examines the transformation of the modern world between the French Revolution and the European revolutions of 1848. Hobsbawm places two major developments at the center of this period: the political revolution in France and the Industrial Revolution in Britain. Together, he argues, they reshaped states, economies, class relations, and political ideas. The book explores the decline of older feudal structures, the rise of the bourgeoisie, the formation of industrial working classes, and the spread of liberalism, nationalism, socialism, and other movements.

Hobsbawm does not treat the French Revolution as an isolated national event. He follows the circulation of revolutionary ideas across Europe and into parts of the wider Atlantic world. Likewise, industrialization is presented not simply as a technological change but as a transformation in labor, urban life, production, wealth, and social power. Political institutions and economic systems change together, creating new forms of conflict and new expectations about citizenship and social progress.

The author writes from a Marxist historical perspective, emphasizing class relations and the development of capitalism. This approach provides a strong analytical framework, but readers should compare it with other interpretations that give different weight to religion, culture, state institutions, and regional variation. The book is a major synthesis rather than a detailed account of every country or social group.

*The Age of Revolution* is valuable for understanding the origins of modern political ideologies, industrial society, nationalism, and class politics. It helps readers connect the events of 1789 and 1848 with the institutions and social conflicts that shaped later modern history.


 A further strength of the book is its attention to the way political language travels. Ideas such as citizenship, rights, nationhood, and popular sovereignty acquire different meanings when they are used by social groups with unequal access to power. This makes the period important not only as a sequence of revolutions, but also as a transformation in the vocabulary through which people argued about legitimate government.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解法国大革命与英国工业革命的相互影响。
- 认识现代阶级关系、民族主义和政治意识形态的形成。
- 分析经济生产方式与政治制度的联动变化。
- 辨认马克思主义史学的解释框架与局限。
- 建立理解现代欧洲形成的时间线。
