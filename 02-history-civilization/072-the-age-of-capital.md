# 072 · The Age of Capital: 1848–1875

- **作者：** Eric Hobsbawm
- **首次出版/成书：** 1975
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：霍布斯鲍姆现代世界史三部曲第二部，资本主义史的重要综合研究。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

《资本的年代》延续霍布斯鲍姆对现代世界形成的研究，聚焦1848年革命失败之后至1875年前后的资本主义扩张。作者描写铁路、工厂、银行、国际贸易和工业技术如何改变欧洲及世界经济，也讨论资产阶级文化、城市中产阶层、工人运动和民族国家建设。1848年革命虽然未能实现许多政治目标，却推动了制度改革和社会力量重组；随后数十年，资本主义经济迅速扩张，欧洲列强的影响力也通过贸易、金融和帝国扩张向外延伸。霍布斯鲍姆将经济增长与阶级关系、政治妥协和文化观念联系起来，展示繁荣背后仍存在贫困、不平等和社会冲突。作品具有鲜明的马克思主义解释视角，部分判断需要结合不同地区的研究进行比较。它的核心价值在于让读者理解工业化不只是机器增加，而是资本、劳动、国家和全球市场关系的重新组织。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

Eric Hobsbawm’s *The Age of Capital: 1848–1875* examines the decades following the European revolutions of 1848, when capitalism expanded rapidly and industrial society became more firmly established. Hobsbawm traces the growth of railways, factories, banks, international trade, finance, and new technologies. These developments changed the organization of work, the scale of markets, the structure of cities, and the influence of European states across the world.

The failed revolutions of 1848 did not simply disappear from history. Their political demands were reshaped through reforms, compromises, and new forms of national organization. During the following decades, bourgeois institutions gained strength, industrial production increased, and middle-class culture became more influential. Yet economic expansion did not remove inequality. Workers continued to face difficult conditions, and social movements challenged the distribution of wealth and political power.

Hobsbawm connects economic change with class relations, national politics, and cultural assumptions. His Marxist perspective emphasizes the development of capitalism and the social groups created by industrialization. This framework is influential, but readers should compare it with regional histories that may place different emphasis on religion, state policy, colonial relationships, and local institutions. The book is a broad synthesis and cannot represent every national experience in equal detail.

*The Age of Capital* is valuable for understanding how industrial capitalism became a global system. It helps readers see that economic growth depends on institutions, finance, labor, infrastructure, and political power, and that prosperity can coexist with social conflict. It also provides a bridge between the revolutionary upheavals of the first half of the nineteenth century and the imperial expansion examined in Hobsbawm’s next volume.


 The volume also helps explain why the expansion of markets did not produce identical outcomes everywhere. Infrastructure, state policy, access to credit, and the bargaining power of workers shaped how industrial growth was experienced. Comparing these factors across regions prevents readers from assuming that capitalism followed one uniform path.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解1848年后资本主义扩张与工业社会发展。
- 分析铁路、金融、贸易和技术对经济结构的影响。
- 认识资产阶级、工人阶级与民族国家的变化。
- 讨论经济繁荣与社会不平等并存的原因。
- 比较马克思主义解释与其他区域史研究。
