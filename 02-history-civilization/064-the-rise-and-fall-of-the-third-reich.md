# 064 · The Rise and Fall of the Third Reich

- **作者：** William L. Shirer
- **首次出版/成书：** 1960
- **分类：** 历史与世界文明
- **入选依据：** 入选依据：纳粹德国史的重要大众综合著作，广泛影响战后历史认知。

## 中文内容概述（约 300 字）

威廉·夏伊勒的《第三帝国的兴亡》是一部关于纳粹德国的综合性历史叙述，从希特勒崛起、魏玛共和国危机和纳粹党夺权写起，追踪独裁政权如何建立、扩张并最终在第二次世界大战中崩溃。作者曾在欧洲担任记者，使用纳粹政府文件、战后审讯材料、日记和回忆录，重建政治决策、宣传机器、外交政策、战争行动和种族迫害。作品的重要贡献是让大量原始文件进入大众历史写作，使读者看到纳粹统治并非一夜之间出现，而是在制度失灵、政治暴力、精英妥协和社会动员中逐步形成。与此同时，这是一部出版于1960年的综合性著作，后来学界在档案开放、纳粹社会史和大屠杀研究方面积累了更多证据，也对其中一些判断和叙述作出修正。阅读时应把它作为重要入门史著，再与后来的专业研究和受害者证词交叉核对。

## English Summary (approximately 300 words)

William L. Shirer’s *The Rise and Fall of the Third Reich* is a broad history of Nazi Germany, tracing the movement from Hitler’s political emergence through the establishment of dictatorship, territorial expansion, the Second World War, and the collapse of the regime in 1945. Shirer had worked as a journalist in Europe and drew on captured Nazi documents, trial records, diaries, memoirs, and other materials that became available after the war. The book helped bring a large documentary record into popular historical writing.

Shirer examines the crisis of the Weimar Republic, the Nazi Party’s organization, propaganda, political violence, the destruction of democratic institutions, diplomatic decisions, military campaigns, and the persecution and murder of millions of people. One of the work’s central lessons is that dictatorship did not emerge in a single moment. It developed through a sequence of political choices, institutional failures, elite compromises, intimidation, and mass mobilization. The regime’s crimes depended on both ideological leadership and the cooperation or acquiescence of many institutions and individuals.

The book was published in 1960 and remains influential, but it should be read as an important synthesis from its period rather than as the final word on Nazi history. Later scholarship has used newly available archives, local studies, victim testimony, and more detailed research into the Holocaust and German society to revise or complicate some interpretations. Readers should compare Shirer’s narrative with contemporary academic studies and primary documents, especially when examining specific decisions or responsibility.

*The Rise and Fall of the Third Reich* is valuable for studying authoritarianism, propaganda, the collapse of democracy, state violence, war, and the relationship between institutions and individual responsibility. It encourages readers to recognize warning signs early and to investigate how ordinary administrative systems can become instruments of persecution.

## 核心学习价值

- 理解魏玛共和国危机与纳粹夺权过程。
- 分析宣传、政治暴力和制度妥协如何摧毁民主。
- 认识纳粹国家机器与大屠杀的历史背景。
- 学习使用政府文件、日记和审判材料。
- 将经典综合史著与后来的专业研究交叉核对。
